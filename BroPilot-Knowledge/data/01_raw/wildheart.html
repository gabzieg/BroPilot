Wildheart is one of the Subclasses of Barbarian. These Barbarians are attuned with nature and the beasts that live in it. Their rages resemble the animals that inspired them.

“
Your attunement with nature and its beasts inspires your rage, empowering you with supernatural might.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Barbarian, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 8[edit section | visual editor]

Land's Stride: Difficult Terrain
You have become an expert at moving through the wilderness.  Difficult Terrain no longer slows you down.

The following are features not listed anywhere, but are nonetheless granted:

Land's Stride: Advantage
You have become an expert at moving through the wilderness. You have  Advantage on  Saving Throws against plants that are magically created to impede your movement.
Land's Stride: Plants
You have become an expert at moving through the wilderness. Plant-based surfaces with thorns, spines, or similar hazards no longer harm you.
