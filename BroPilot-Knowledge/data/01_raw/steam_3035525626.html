Introduction
First of all, English is not my native language, so I apologize for any grammatical errors.

Obviously, this guide will contain spoilers. If you want to avoid them, it's best not to use this guide during your first playthrough.

This guide is not a step-by-step walkthrough, so you won't find exact information on how to reach specific areas in the game. Instead, it serves as a list to help you identify items you might have missed, along with their coordinates in their respective areas. However, I've also included a link to the Wiki[bg3.wiki] for each item in case you need more details about it.

Disclaimer: The seller's price may vary depending on your charisma and attitude.

The Blood of Lathander[bg3.wiki]
In the western hallway at (X:1330, Y:-660), there are two statues that need to be rotated: the one to the south requires an Athletics check to move and should be turned west, while the other should be turned east. This action opens a path in the wall with some barrels that can be jumped over or destroyed. Proceed west to reach the Secret Stairway. Use ranged attacks to destroy the Energy Source crystal on the south side of the barrier, then climb the rocks to access the next area. In the following room, there's a Dawnbreaker that emits pulses to knock the party away. Wait for breaks in the pulses to approach and disarm it (DC 14). Unfortunately, the path forward is still blocked. Move to some rocks, climb them, and follow the path to locate another energy source at (X:1118, Y:-790). The final energy source is situated on the northern rocks. Avoid the Dawnbreaker's pulses and consider disarming it to easily destroy the Energy Source. Proceeding onward, the party eventually encounters the Blood of Lathander. If the Dawnmaster's Crest has been obtained, simply place it in the altar's item slot to safely acquire the Blood of Lathander. Placing the crest after taking the mace also deactivates the trap. If the Blood of Lathander is taken without the Dawnmaster's Crest, it triggers a deadly trap that jeopardizes the Monastery's destruction. The party has only four turns to either escape the Monastery or dismantle the four Lathander Solar Machines that activate the trap.
