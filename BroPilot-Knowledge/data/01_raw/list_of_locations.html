Act One[edit section | visual editor]

Over-world[edit section | visual editor]

Wilderness[edit section | visual editor]

The Wilderness is a heavily forested region in  Elturgard, encompassing a section of the Risen Road and part of the River Chionthar.

Ravaged Beach

A beach along the river Chionthar, now strewn with the remains of a crashed Nautiloid.

Overgrown Ruins

A large ruin near to the grove, with the remnants of what was once a chapel.

Mountain Pass

A trail at the edge of the wilderness, leading off to Rosymorn Monastery.

Sunlit Wetlands

Picturesque wetlands that give way to a Putrid Bog when held under scrutiny.

  • Overgrown Tunnel

Blighted Village

An abandoned village, now occupied by goblins.

  • Apothecary's Cellar
  • Whispering Depths

Emerald Grove

A sacred grove and home to druids, and temporary refuge for a group of tiefling refugees.

  • Inner Sanctum
  • Sacred Pool
  • Secluded Cove
  • The Hollow
  • Underground Passage

Forest

A patch of woodland west of the grove.

  • Owlbear Nest

Goblin Camp

An old Selûnite temple in use as a base of operations for the Cult of the Absolute.

  • Defiled Temple
  • Shattered Sanctum
  • Worg Pens

The Risen Road

A long stretch of road that connects Elturel and Baldur's Gate, running parallel to the river Chionthar.

Waukeen's Rest

An inn on the Risen Road, in flames after an assault from the Absolute.

Zhentarim Basement

A hidden cavern beneath the inn, the base of operations for the Black Network.


Rosymorn Monastery Trail[edit section | visual editor]

Rosymorn Monastery Trail

A pilgrimage trail leading between the wilderness, Rosymorn Monastery and the Shadow-Cursed Lands.

Rosymorn Monastery

A secluded monastery for monks of Lathander.

Crèche Y'llek

Rosymorn Monastery's living quarters, transformed into a githyanki base.

  • Astral Plane
  • Secret Chamber

Under-world[edit section | visual editor]

Underdark[edit section | visual editor]

The Underdark is a vast system of caverns and tunnels beneath the surface, home to the drow, duergar and deep gnomes.

Arcane Tower

An isolated wizard tower protected by Arcane Cannons.

Ebonlake Grotto

Home of the myconids, lead by Sovereign Spaw.

Decrepit Village

An abandoned village on a beach beside the Ebonlake.

Dread Hollow

A cavernous hollow growing a large Sussur tree.

Festering Cove

A hidden cove, home to followers of BOOOAL

Selûnite Outpost

An abandoned outpost which previously housed followers of Selûne.

Storehouse

A lone storehouse with an elevator leading to the surface

Grymforge

The legendary Grymforge, built by Sharran worshippers in times past.

  • Adamantine Forge
  • Abandoned Refuge
  • Ancient Temple

Over-world[edit section | visual editor]

Shadow-Cursed Lands[edit section | visual editor]

The Shadow-Cursed Lands is a region shrouded in its namesake Shadow Curse, encompassing Reithwin Town and a large, Ruined Battlefield.

Ruined Battlefield

A large battlefield spanning half the region, with remnants of a conflict from before the shadow curse.

  • House in Deep Shadows

Last Light Inn

A sanctuary within the Shadow-Cursed Lands, currently occupied by Harpers and members of the Flaming Fist.

  • Last Light Inn - Cellar

Reithwin Town

A now empty town, previously home to followers of Selûne and the Thorm family.

  • House of Healing
  • House of Healing Morgue
  • Mason's Guild
  • Reithwin Graveyard
  • Reithwin Tollhouse
  • Sharran Sanctuary
  • The Waning Moon

Grand Mausoleum

The Thorm family mausoleum, resting place of Melodia Thorm and entrance to the Gauntlet of Shar.

  • Gauntlet of Shar

Moonrise Towers

The seat of the Absolute and base of their operations, located south of Reithwin Town.

  • Mind Flayer Colony
  • Oubliette
  • Moonrise Towers Prison
  • Moonrise Towers Rooftop

Over-world[edit section | visual editor]

Rivington[edit section | visual editor]

Rivington

The southern-most district of Baldur's Gate, located on the southern shore of the River Chionthar and south of of Wyrm's Crossing.

  • Abandoned Windmill
  • Gur Camp
  • Rivington Well
  • Western Beach

Arfur's Mansion

The home of toymaker Arfur Gregorio

Circus of the Last Days

An extraplanar circus run by ringmaster Lucretious.

  • Jungle

Open Hand Temple

A temple dedicated to Ilmater, grieving after the loss of their head priest.

  • Open Hand Temple Cellar
  • Under Temple Cave Area

Requisitioned Barn

A large barn, currently in use as storage for refugee requisitions.

South Span Checkpoint

An intermediate checkpoint between Rivington and Wyrm's Crossing.

Sword Coast Couriers

Rivington's post office, complete with pigeon carriers.

Rivington General

A weapons and armour store, run by Gyldro Angleiron and his clerk Exxvikyap.

  • Angleiron's Cellar

Wyrm's Crossing[edit section | visual editor]

Wyrm's Crossing

A crossing between Rivington and the Lower City of Baldur's Gate.

  • Carm's Garms
  • Fraygo's Flophouse
  • Stylin' Horst Potion Shop

Danthelon's Dancing Axe

A weapons and armour store owned by Entharl Danthelon, as well as a front to a Harper hideout.

Sharess' Caress

A house of pleasure run by Mamzell Amira and Kira.

Wyrm's Rock Fortress

An island-fortress which serves as one of the Flaming Fist's headquarters.

  • The Wyrmway
  • Wyrm's Rock Prison

Baldur's Gate[edit section | visual editor]

Baldur's Gate is a large city, settled along the bank of the River Chionthar and one of its natural bays.

Lower City

Baldur's Mouth

A print shop for news broadsheets, run by editor Ettvard

Basilisk Gate Barracks

Barracks for the Flaming Fist and location of Heapside Prison.

Bloomridge Park

A large park in the north of the Lower City.

Candulhallow's Tombstones

An unassuming funerary store nestled along a back street.

  • Murder Tribunal

Counting House

A banking and storage facility found along the southern coast of the Lower City.

Devil's Fee

A curio store run by Helsik.

Elerrathin's Home

The home of Jaheira and her wards, with a large, lush basement cavern.

Elfsong Tavern

A well-known tavern, popular with adventurers and independent operators.

  • Knights of the Shield Hideout

Felogyr's Fireworks

A fireworks store in the south-east of the city.

Flymm Cargo

A dock-side warehouse and access point to the Lower City Sewers and Iron Throne.

  • Iron Throne

Forge of the Nine

An outdoor smithy, possibly run by Dammon.

Graveyard (Lower City)

The Lower City's graveyard, housing several mausoleums and a morgue.

  • Ancient Mausoleum
  • Durinbold Mausoleum
  • Gorion Mausoleum
  • Hhune Mausoleum

House of Grief

A healing house run by Sharran cultists, and aboveground facade to their cloister.

  • Cloister of Sombre Embrace

Lady Jannath's Estate

The home of Lady Jannath and her husband, the artist Oskar Fevras.

Lustrous Lass

A cursed ship docked at Greyharbour.

Old Garlow's Place

A somewhat rundown house and hideout for a group of hag survivors.

Philgrave's Mansion

A waterside mansion and home of Mystic Carrion.

  • Vonayn's Home

Steel Watch Foundry

Factory for Gortash's Steel Watch, found near the Grey Harbour.

Sorcerous Sundries

A large magical marketplace, run by the renound wizard Lorroakan.

  • Ramazith's Tower
  • Sorcerous Vault

Stormshore Tabernacle

A house of worship in the north-eastern part of the city.

Szarr Palace

Palace of Cazador Szarr and home to his vampire spawn.

  • Cazador's Dungeon

The Blushing Mermaid

A large tavern with an old ship's bow protruding from the side.

Highberry's Home

A small festival taking place on the Highberry's home porch, hosted by Cora Highberry.

The Lodge

Headquarters of the Society of Brilliance, perched high above the Greyharbour Docks.

Water Queen's House

An outdoor temple to Bitch Queen Umberlee, overseen by Wavemistress Allandra Grey.
