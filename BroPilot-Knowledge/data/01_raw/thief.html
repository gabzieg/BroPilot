Thief is a subclass of Rogue. Most notably, its  Fast Hands passive grants a second  Bonus Action. Together with the cunning actions the rogue gains at level 2, this subclass allows for exceptional mobility and enhanced versatility.

“
Your quick hands and mastery of the larcenous arts make stealing second nature - be it from a third floor window or a forgotten ruin.
„

The extra bonus action from Fast Hands allows thieves to use  Cunning Action: Dash twice to triple their Movement Speed without sacrificing an action. Cunning Action: Dash combined with  Cunning Action: Disengage allows for moving between enemies without triggering  Opportunity Attacks. This extra bonus action can also be paired with the same from items, weapon actions, and other classes.

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Rogue, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

Fast Hands
You gain an extra  Bonus Action, which you can use with any of your existing Bonus Actions.
Second-Story Work
You've mastered the art of falling and gain Resistance to Falling damage.

Level 9[edit section | visual editor]

 Supreme Sneak ()
Blend into the environment so completely that you become  Invisible. ( Recharge: Short rest.)
