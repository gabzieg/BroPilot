The Fiend is one of the subclasses of Warlock. These Warlocks are servants of chaotic and destructive beings such as demons or devils and traded their souls to these dark forces in exchange for power. They also have the ability to gain additional Hit Points upon dealing the finishing blow to enemies.

“
You have pledged your soul to the Hells or Abyss in return for a deadly arsenal of fiendish arcana.
„
