Trickery Domain is one of the Subclasses of Cleric. These Clerics are more specialised in stealth and illusion magic.

Player selectable Deities associated with this domain:  Garl Glittergold,  Lolth,  Shar (Shadowheart only),  Tiamat, and  Tymora.

“
You wield the unorthodox divine tools of deceit and illusion in worship of your chosen deity.
„
