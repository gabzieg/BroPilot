Circle of Stars is one of the subclasses of Druid. It can choose to transform into special Starry Forms instead of beast forms which grant unique abilities. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.[url 1]

“
You are guided by the infinite mysteries of the cosmos. It is through your understanding of the stars that your druidic magic gains its potency.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Druid, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 2[edit section | visual editor]

You gain the following cantrip:

  •  Guidance 
Starry Form
You can expend a  Wild Shape Charge to take on a Starry Form rather than transforming into a beast.
Starry Form: Archer ( + )
A constellation of an archer appears on you. As a  Bonus Action while in this Starry Form, you can cast  Luminous Arrow to deal 1d8 + Spellcasting modifier⁠Radiant.
Starry Form: Chalice ( + )
A constellation of a life-giving chalice appears on you. Whenever you cast a healing spell that consumes a spell slot, you can cast  Chalice Healing to heal 1d8 + Wisdom modifier⁠Healing.
Starry Form: Dragon ( + )
A constellation of a wise dragon appears on you. When you make a  Saving Throw to maintain Concentration on a spell, a roll result of 9 or lower is considered a 10. As a  Bonus Action while in this Starry Form, you can cast  Dazzling Breath to deal 2d6 + Spellcasting modifier⁠Radiant.
 Star Maps: 2
Used to cast Guiding Bolt without a spell slot. Replenished by a Long Rest.
Circle of the Stars spells
You gain additional spells from your circle. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Guiding Bolt ( + )
  •  Star Map: Guiding Bolt ( + )

Level 5[edit section | visual editor]

 Star Maps: 3
You gain an additional Star Map.
 Dazzling Breath
Damage increases to 3d6 + Spellcasting modifier⁠Radiant.

Level 6[edit section | visual editor]

Cosmic Omen
Whenever you Long Rest, you consult the stars for a Cosmic Omen of Weal or of Woe. The omen grants you the ability to increase (Weal) or decrease (Woe)  Attack Rolls or  Saving Throws, or (Weal only) to increase your allies' Ability Checks.
 Cosmic Omens: 3
Resource used for Omens. Replenished by a Long Rest.

Level 9[edit section | visual editor]

 Star Maps: 4
You gain an additional Star Map.
 Cosmic Omens: 4
You gain an additional Cosmic Omen.

Level 10[edit section | visual editor]

Twinkling Constellations

Your Starry Forms are improved. Once per turn, you can switch to a different Starry Form as a free action.
Starry Form: Archer
 Luminous Arrow increases to 2d8 + Spellcasting modifier⁠Radiant.
Starry Form: Chalice
 Chalice Healing increases to 2d8 + Wisdom modifier⁠Healing.
Starry Form: Dragon
 Dazzling Breath increases to 4d6 + Spellcasting modifier⁠Radiant, and you gain the ability to hover and  Fly at a movement speed of 6 m (20 ft).
