Arcane Archer is one of the subclasses of Fighter. It focuses on using magical arrows to damage and control enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025. [url 1]

“
You are capable of weaving magic into your attacks, giving your archery a supernatural quality - and your foes something to fear.
„

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Fighter, in addition to its unique features outlined below.
