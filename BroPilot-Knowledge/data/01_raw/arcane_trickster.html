Arcane Trickster is a subclass of Rogue. These Rogues are capable of spellcasting (from the Wizard spell list) and use their spells to attack enemies or support allies in combat. Arcane Tricksters use Intelligence as their spellcasting modifier.

“
As quick with a spell as you are with a lockpick, your illusions and enchantments keep opponents on the back foot.
„

Notes[edit section | visual editor]

  • Multiclassing prior to level 10 results in standard spellcaster progression with four Level 1 Spell Slots and three Level 2 Spell Slots, while multiclassing after level 10 results in retaining altered spellcaster progression with six Level 1 Spell Slots and two Level 2 Spell Slots. This is true even if the additional class is not a spellcaster.
