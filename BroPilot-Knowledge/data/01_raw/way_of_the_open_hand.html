Way of the Open Hand is one of the subclasses of Monk. It can be chosen at level 3. These monks are more specialized in using unarmed attacks to strike their opponents down.

“
You specialise in unarmed combat, using your hands and your control of ki to heal or inflict grievous hurt.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Monk, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

Gain unique, upgraded variants of  Flurry of Blows.

 Flurry of Blows: Topple ( + )
Punch twice in quick succession and potentially knock the target  Prone.
 Flurry of Blows: Stagger ( + )
Punch twice in quick succession and  Stagger the target, making it unable to take  Reactions.
 Flurry of Blows: Push ( + )
Punch twice in quick succession and potentially push your target 5 m / 17 ft away.

Level 6[edit section | visual editor]

Gain three toggleable passives that improve your unarmed damage. Only one of these passives can be active at once.

Manifestation of Body
Your hands sap the ki from your enemies' bodies. Your unarmed attacks deal an additional 1d4 + Wisdom Modifier⁠⁠Necrotic damage.
Manifestation of Mind
Your hands sap the ki from your enemies' bodies. Your unarmed attacks deal an additional 1d4 + Wisdom Modifier⁠⁠Psychic damage.
Manifestation of Soul
Your hands sap the ki from your enemies' bodies. Your unarmed attacks deal an additional 1d4 + Wisdom Modifier⁠⁠Radiant damage.
 Wholeness of Body ()
Regain half your  Ki Points and enter a temporary state of  Wholeness where you regain Ki Points and have an extra  Bonus Action. ( Recharge: Long rest.)

Level 9[edit section | visual editor]

 Ki Resonation: Punch ()
Hit a creature with your bare fists, making the ki in its body  Resonate with yours.
 Ki Resonation: Punch (Bonus Action) ()
Hit a creature with your bare fists, making the ki in its body  Resonate with yours.
 Ki Resonation: Blast ()
Detonate the ki of a creature  Resonating with you, hitting the creature and all creatures other than party members and summoned creatures within 5 m / 17 ft of it. ( Recharge: Per turn.)

Level 11[edit section | visual editor]

Tranquillity
Long Rests surround you with an aura of peace, granting you  Sanctuary permanently until you attack.
