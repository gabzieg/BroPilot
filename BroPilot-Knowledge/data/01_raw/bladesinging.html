Bladesinger is one of the subclasses of Wizard. It uses an elegant combination of sword and spell to defeat enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.[url 1]

“
A master of wizardry and swordplay, you combine the elegant manoeuvres of a warrior with the devastating magics of a mage.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Wizard, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 2[edit section | visual editor]

 Bladesong Power: 2
Used to cast Bladesong. Replenished on a Long Rest. Bladesinging is not possible in Medium or Heavy armor, even if proficient; you will receive the "Bladesong Impeded" Condition while wearing them.
 Bladesong ()
Invoke the Bladesong. You gain a +2 bonus to Armour Class and your movement speed increases by 3 m (10 ft). You also gain  Advantage in Acrobatics and a +2 to  Constitution Saving Throws.
Training in War and Song
You gain proficiency with  Light armour and in the Performance skill. You also gain proficiency with  Daggers,  Longswords,  Rapiers,  Scimitars,  Shortswords, and  Sickles.

Level 5[edit section | visual editor]

 Bladesong Power: 3
Gain an additional use of Bladesong.
 Bladesong ()
Bonus to AC and  Constitution Saving Throws increases to +3.

Level 6[edit section | visual editor]

Extra Attack
You can make an additional free attack after making an unarmed or weapon attack.

Level 9[edit section | visual editor]

 Bladesong Power: 4
Gain an additional use of Bladesong.
 Bladesong ()
Bonus to AC and  Constitution Saving Throws increases to +4.

Level 10[edit section | visual editor]

Song of Defence
When you take damage during your  Bladesong, you can expend a spell slot to subtract 5 damage per spell slot.

Bugs[edit section | visual editor]

Due to a coding error, characters who cast a spell that applies a condition (such as  Guidance) on a Bladesinger wizard may inadvertently have their casting animations changed to the ones used by – and unique to – Bladesinger wizards. This effect is permanent, and is only removed if the affected character is respecced with  Withers.
