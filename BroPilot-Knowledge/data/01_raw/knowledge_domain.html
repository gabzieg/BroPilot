Knowledge Domain is one of the Subclasses of Cleric. These Clerics are more specialized in arcane magic.

Player selectable Deities associated with this domain:  Moradin,  Mystra,  Oghma, and  Selûne.

“
Adaptable and adroit in all manner of languages and skills, your mind is an intellectual cup brimming with exquisite knowing.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Cleric, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 1[edit section | visual editor]

Blessings of Knowledge
Gain Expertise in two of the following Skills:  Arcana,  History,  Nature, or  Religion. Your Proficiency Bonus is doubled for Ability Checks made using these Skills.
Domain Spells
You gain spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Command
  •  Sleep

Level 2[edit section | visual editor]

 Knowledge of the Ages ( + )
Gain Proficiency in all Skills of a chosen Ability.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

Domain Spells
You gain spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Calm Emotions 
  •  Hold Person 

Level 5[edit section | visual editor]

Domain Spells
You gain spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Slow 
  •  Speak with Dead 

Level 6[edit section | visual editor]

 Read Thoughts ( + )
Read the thoughts of certain creatures while talking to them.
 Speak with Animals ( + )
Gain the ability to comprehend and communicate with beasts.

Level 7[edit section | visual editor]

Domain Spells
You gain spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Confusion 
  •  Otiluke's Resilient Sphere 

Level 8[edit section | visual editor]

Potent Spellcasting
Your god grants you even more intense power. You can add your Wisdom Modifier to the damage you deal with Cleric cantrips.

Level 9[edit section | visual editor]

Domain Spells
You gain spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Dominate Person 
  •  Telekinesis
