Gloom Stalker is a subclass of Ranger. Gloom Stalkers specialize in taking out foes swiftly and ruthlessly from the shadows, gaining damage bonuses and increased movement speed.

“
Emerging like a horrible gift from the envelope of darkness and shadow, you ambush and put down your foes before they can even scream.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Ranger, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

You gain the following spell. It is Always Prepared:

  •  Disguise Self 
 Umbral Shroud ()
Wrap yourself in shadows to become  Invisible if you are obscured. ( Recharge: Short rest.)
Superior Darkvision
Can see in the dark up to 24 m (80 ft).
Dread Ambusher
You specialise in taking out foes swiftly and ruthlessly. You gain a +3 bonus to Initiative. On the first turn of combat, your Movement Speed increases by 3 m (10 ft), and you can also make an extra weapon attack that deals an additional 1d8 damage
 Dread Ambusher (Melee)
On the first turn of each combat, ambush a target with an additional swift and precise attack. ( Recharge: Per combat.)
 Dread Ambusher (Ranged)
On the first turn of each combat, ambush a target with an additional swift and precise attack. ( Recharge: Per combat.)
 Dread Ambusher: Hide ()
Hide from enemies by succeeding at Stealth checks. Stick to the dark and avoid enemy sightlines. Attacking or casting a spell will reveal your location.

  •  Misty Step

Level 7[edit section | visual editor]

Iron Mind
You gain Proficiency in Wisdom and Intelligence  Saving Throws.

Level 9[edit section | visual editor]

  •  Fear 

Level 11[edit section | visual editor]

Stalker's Flurry
When you miss with a weapon attack, you can make another one for free.
