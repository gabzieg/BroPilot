Life Domain is one of the Subclasses of Cleric. These Clerics are more specialized in preserving life, and their healing spells are more potent.

Player-selectable deities associated with this domain include  Bahamut,  Corellon Larethian,  Eilistraee,  Helm,  Ilmater,  Lathander,  Selûne, and  Yondalla.

Tasked with the holy edict of preserving the body, mind, and soul, your god grants you a plethora of healing magics.
