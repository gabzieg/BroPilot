Tempest Domain is one of the cleric subclasses, with a focus on Thunder and Lightning damage, weather themed spells, and martial combat.

Player selectable Deities associated with this domain:  Gruumsh and  Talos.

“
Your faith has made you the very thunder that quakes the black firmament, the lightning coursing through the veins of a terrible storm.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Cleric, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 1[edit section | visual editor]

  • Gain  Heavy armour Proficiency
  • Gain Martial weapons Proficiency
Wrath of the Storm
As a  Reaction, strike back at an attacking creature, dealing 2d8⁠⁠Lightning or 2d8⁠⁠Thunder damage. The target takes half damage on a successful  Saving Throw.
 Wrath of the Storm: Lightning ()
If a nearby enemy attacks you, you can retaliate with a strike of lightning that deals 2d8⁠⁠Lightning damage.
 Wrath of the Storm: Thunder ()
If a nearby enemy attacks you, you can retaliate with a burst of thunder that deals 2d8⁠⁠Thunder damage.
Domain Spells
You gain additional spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Thunderwave
  •  Fog Cloud 

Level 2[edit section | visual editor]

 Destructive Wrath ()
When you roll Thunder or Lightning damage, you can use your Channel Divinity to deal maximum damage instead.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

Domain Spells
You gain additional spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Gust of Wind
  •  Shatter

Level 5[edit section | visual editor]

Domain Spells
You gain additional spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Call Lightning 
  •  Sleet Storm 

Level 6[edit section | visual editor]

Thunderbolt Strike
When you deal ⁠Thunder or ⁠Lightning damage to a creature that is Large or smaller, you can also push it up to 3 m (10 ft).

Level 7[edit section | visual editor]

Domain Spells
You gain additional spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Freedom of Movement
  •  Ice Storm

Level 8[edit section | visual editor]

 Divine Strike: Thunder ()
Once per turn deal 1d8⁠⁠Thunder in addition to your weapon's damage when you make a melee attack. ( Recharge: Per turn.)

Level 9[edit section | visual editor]

Domain Spells
You gain additional spells from your domain. They are Always Prepared.
  •  Destructive Wave
  •  Insect Plague
