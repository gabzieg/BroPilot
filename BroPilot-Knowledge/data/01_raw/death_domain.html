Death Domain is one of the subclasses of Cleric. It has potent features which boost necrotic spells and add necrotic damage to weapon attacks. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025. [url 1]

Player selectable Deities associated with this domain:  Kelemvor,  Laduguer, and  Shar (Shadowheart only).

You nourish your faith on the forces that create the dead and give rise to the undead.
