[![](/w/images/8/82/Icosahedron_D20_Smooth.webp)](/wiki/File:Icosahedron_D20_Smooth.webp)

This page describes any changes that have been made in *Baldur's Gate 3* (BG3) from the original rules of **Dungeons and Dragons 5th Edition 2014** (D&D 5e 2014).

The rule changes described on this and other change-related pages are written with the assumption that the reader understands the relevant D&D 5e rule. These pages do not explain any D&D 5e rules, only the changed or related BG3 rules. These pages also include some BG3 rules and features which are unchanged from D&D 5e to help a tabletop player quickly see which options have changed and which have not without having to do additional research. If the BG3 rule aligns with a rule from the 2024 5e revision or other material from future playtests, it is considered a rule change from D&D 5e.

## Fundamental changes

### Characters

See also: [D&D 5e class changes](/wiki/D%26D_5e_class_changes "D&D 5e class changes") and [D&D 5e race changes](/wiki/D%26D_5e_race_changes "D&D 5e race changes")

- The level cap is 12.
- Characters must use standard point buy. There is no rolling for [ability scores](/wiki/Ability_scores "Ability scores").
- All [races](/wiki/Races "Races") have a +2/+1 flexible ability score bonus – similar to the alternate rules from *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* – though the standard 5e racial ability score bonuses and the +1/+1/+1 options do not exist. [Half-elves](/wiki/Half-Elf "Half-Elf") and [humans](/wiki/Human "Human") have been granted additional features (chiefly, [![Civil Militia](/w/images/thumb/b/b5/Civil_Militia_Icon.webp/20px-Civil_Militia_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Civil_Militia "Civil Militia") [Civil Militia](/wiki/Civil_Militia "Civil Militia")) to make up for this change.
- Hit points gained on level-up are always fixed values; there is no option to roll a hit die.
- Multiclassing has no ability score requirements.
- Party members can be respecced after meeting [![Withers](/w/images/thumb/2/25/Portrait_Withers.png/25px-Portrait_Withers.png.webp)](/wiki/Withers "Withers") [Withers](/wiki/Withers "Withers") early in Act 1 of the game. Party members may respec their [class](/wiki/Class "Class"), subclass, [spells](/wiki/Spells "Spells"), any other class-related choices (e.g. [fighting style](/wiki/Fighting_style "Fighting style")), ability scores, and [skill](/wiki/Skill "Skill") proficiencies gained from class (though not from [background](/wiki/Background "Background")). It is not possible to change a character's [race](/wiki/Race "Race") or background.
  - If an [origin](/wiki/Origin "Origin") character or [companion](/wiki/Companion "Companion")'s starting class (or subclass) is changed, their unique dialogue and quests will still treat them as a member of their original class. They will still gain the standard class-based dialogue options from their new class.
  - This makes it possible to reach the level cap with one level from each of the 12 available classes, which grants an [achievement](/wiki/Achievements#Jack-of-all-Trades "Achievements") if done without ever respeccing.
- Tool, vehicle, and language proficiencies are not implemented; only proficiencies for weapons, armour, skills, and musical instruments.
  - As such, backgrounds only provide proficiency in two skills.
  - Rather than having proficiencies for individual instruments, there is only a single proficiency for all musical instruments. It is not part of any background, but can be granted by the [bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard") class, the [![Performer](/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Generic_Feature_Icon.webp/20px-Generic_Feature_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Performer "Performer") [Performer](/wiki/Performer "Performer") feat, or [a special dialogue interaction](/wiki/Alfira#Performing_together "Alfira").
- [Inspiration](/wiki/Inspiration "Inspiration") is granted automatically when a character in the active party does or sees certain things associated with their background. For example, if a character has the [![Charlatan](/w/images/thumb/2/29/Background_Charlatan_Icon.png/20px-Background_Charlatan_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Charlatan "Charlatan") [Charlatan](/wiki/Charlatan "Charlatan") background, they may gain inspiration if they deceive their way into a fortified enemy position. The entire party cannot collectively have more than four inspiration uses stored up at a time, which can be spent to reroll a failed skill check or saving throw in dialogue.
- Characters can carry a maximum of 40 kg (80 lbs) + 10 kg (20 lbs) per point of Strength. If a character is carrying more than 80% of their maximum [carrying capacity](/wiki/Carrying_capacity "Carrying capacity"), they are [![Encumbered](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Encumbered_(Condition) "Encumbered (Condition)") [Encumbered](/wiki/Encumbered_(Condition) "Encumbered (Condition)"). Above 93.33% carrying capacity, this becomes [![Heavily Encumbered](/w/images/thumb/e/ec/Heavily_Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Heavily_Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Heavily_Encumbered_(Condition) "Heavily Encumbered (Condition)") [Heavily Encumbered](/wiki/Heavily_Encumbered_(Condition) "Heavily Encumbered (Condition)").

### Death

- Most characters not in the party, including important NPCs, are killed outright when reduced to 0 hit points.
  - They do **not** make death saving throws and **cannot be resurrected**.
  - The exception to this is most children (excluding goblin children), who cannot be killed except in specific story events.
- Party members cannot instantly die from damage: a party member reduced to 0 hit points is [![Downed](/w/images/thumb/6/66/Downed_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Downed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)") [Downed](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)"), regardless of whether the remaining damage exceeds their hit point maximum, with the exception of [wildshaped](/wiki/Wild_Shape "Wild Shape") creatures. There are, however, other ways to instantly die, such as falling into a chasm or various story events.
  - Party members make [death saving throws](/wiki/Death_saving_throw "Death saving throw") once reduced to 0 hit points.
  - Dead party members can be resurrected using the [![Revivify](/w/images/thumb/5/59/Revivify_Icon.webp/20px-Revivify_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Revivify "Revivify") [Revivify](/wiki/Revivify "Revivify") spell, a [![](/w/images/thumb/6/62/Scroll_of_Revivify_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Scroll_of_Revivify_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Scroll_of_Revivify "Scroll of Revivify") [Scroll of Revivify](/wiki/Scroll_of_Revivify "Scroll of Revivify"), or by Withers for a fee of 200 gold. There is no time limit on resurrection, and Withers does not require a character's corpse to be intact or present.
  - If a party member takes damage that reduces their Wild Shape form's Hit Points below 0, the remaining damage is applied to their original form. If the remaining damage is also enough to reduce their original form to 0 Hit Points, they die instantly, skipping the usual death saving throws.

### Resting

- The party is limited to two [short rests](/wiki/Short_rest "Short rest") per [long rest](/wiki/Long_rest "Long rest"). Short rests restore each party member's hit points (including those left at [camp](/wiki/Camp "Camp")) by an amount equal to half their maximum HP (rounded down). There is no hit die rolling.
  - The [bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard")'s ability [![Song of Rest](/w/images/thumb/b/b1/Song_of_Rest_Icon.webp/20px-Song_of_Rest_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Song_of_Rest "Song of Rest") [Song of Rest](/wiki/Song_of_Rest "Song of Rest") provides an additional short rest.
- Long rests require [camp supplies](/wiki/Camp_Supplies "Camp Supplies"), which are food and drink items that must be looted or purchased. Taking a long rest without sufficient camp supplies results in only partial recovery of spell slots and hit points.

### Dice rolls

- There is a weighted dice setting in the game called [Karmic Dice](/wiki/Karmic_Dice "Karmic Dice"), which is enabled by default.
  - While enabled, the game mitigates bad luck streaks. Some players report that this can make fights feel quicker or more potent, but results can vary.
- Rolling a natural 1 on an ability check always results in failure, even if modifiers alone would exceed the DC. Rolling a natural 20 on a skill check always results in success, even if with modifiers it would not exceed the DC. There are no additional fumble or critical success features besides an automatic failure or pass of the ability check.
- When [saving throws](/wiki/Saving_throws "Saving throws") are used in dialogue, rolling a natural 1 or natural 20 always results in failure or success, regardless of the saving throw's DC or the character's modifiers. Regular saving throws do not have critical failures or critical successes when a 1 or 20 is rolled.
  - Death saving throws also do not have critical failures or critical successes. A roll of 1 gives only one fail rather than 2, and a roll of 20 results in a success, rather than restoring 1 HP.

### Combat

- [Initiative](/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") is rolled with a d4 instead of a d20, and is not treated as a Dexterity ability check. Features that grant a bonus to initiative still exist, but most grant a flat bonus to the roll instead of advantage or a bonus based on an [ability score](/wiki/Ability_score "Ability score").
  - This makes it more likely that multiple characters on the same side will roll identical or adjacent initiative values, allowing them to exploit the shared initiative feature described below.
  - If two or more player-controlled characters are next to each other in the initiative order, their initiative is shared. These characters can act in any order and can even blend their turns together.
- Combat areas tend to have a large amount of verticality, but are relatively small in horizontal size. This has led to the following mechanical changes:
  - The ranges of many [spells](/wiki/Spells "Spells") are reduced (as described on individual spell pages), often to 60ft.
  - The normal range of [ranged weapons](/wiki/Weapons#Weapon_properties_table "Weapons") has been reduced to 60ft, except hand crossbows, whose range has been increased to 50 ft.
  - [![Darkvision](/w/images/thumb/0/0d/Darkvision_Icon.webp/20px-Darkvision_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Darkvision "Darkvision") [Darkvision](/wiki/Darkvision "Darkvision") is reduced to 40ft, and [![Superior Darkvision](/w/images/thumb/3/38/Superior_Darkvision_Icon.webp/20px-Superior_Darkvision_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Superior_Darkvision "Superior Darkvision") [Superior Darkvision](/wiki/Superior_Darkvision "Superior Darkvision") to 80ft.
- Cover mechanics are not implemented, but a line of sight to the target is still required for ranged weapon attacks, [![Throw](/w/images/thumb/e/e3/Throw_Icon.webp/20px-Throw_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Throw "Throw") [Throw](/wiki/Throw "Throw") attacks, and projectile spells.
- When a creature at least 10ft above their target makes a ranged attack, they receive a +2 bonus to the attack roll due to [high ground](/wiki/High_ground "High ground"). When a creature at least 10ft below their target makes a ranged attack, they receive a -2 penalty to the attack roll due to low ground. The [Sharpshooter](/wiki/Sharpshooter "Sharpshooter") feat removes the low ground penalty.
- Rules for hearing-based detection are not implemented, and NPCs may only detect what they see. If a character [hides](/wiki/Hide "Hide") while not in any creature's sight cone, they automatically succeed. If they try to hide while in a creature's sight cone, or are already hidden and enter a creature's sight cone, then, depending on the creature's senses (such as Darkvision or [![Blindsight](/w/images/thumb/5/56/Generic_Darkness_Icon.webp/20px-Generic_Darkness_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Blindsight "Blindsight") [Blindsight](/wiki/Blindsight "Blindsight")) and the hider's level of [obscurity](/wiki/Obscurity "Obscurity"), they may be automatically detected or may roll a [Stealth](/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check against the creature's passive perception.
- When making a ranged attack with a weapon that has both the [Finesse](/wiki/Finesse "Finesse") and [Thrown](/wiki/Thrown "Thrown") properties, the attack roll uses the attacker's [Strength](/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier, instead of choosing the higher modifier between their Strength and [Dexterity](/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") (this is likely unintended and a developer oversight). The damage roll, however, uses the higher of the attacker's Strength or Dexterity modifiers.

### Spellcasting

See also: [D&D 5e spell changes](/wiki/D%26D_5e_spell_changes "D&D 5e spell changes")

- Prepared spellcasters may change their prepared spells at any time out of combat.
  - Some spells with an ongoing effect will end if the caster unprepares them, while others will not. It's not clear whether this inconsistency is intentional.
- Verbal, Somatic, and Material components are ignored. Being [![Silenced](/w/images/thumb/b/b6/Silenced_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Silenced_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Silenced_(Condition) "Silenced (Condition)") [Silenced](/wiki/Silenced_(Condition) "Silenced (Condition)") prevents casting of most spells; however, some spells that do not have verbal components in 5e do ignore this, such as [![Ice Knife](/w/images/thumb/1/17/Ice_Knife_Icon.webp/20px-Ice_Knife_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Ice_Knife "Ice Knife") [Ice Knife](/wiki/Ice_Knife "Ice Knife").
- Spellcasting focuses are not required for any class. Spellcasters can cast any spell, even with both hands full.
  - While casting [bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard") spells, bards will play an equipped [musical instrument](/wiki/Musical_instrument "Musical instrument") or whistle if they have none equipped. However, this is merely cosmetic.
- The game does not stop a character from casting a levelled spell with both an action and a bonus action in the same turn.
- All caster classes are capable of ritual casting, and the [Ritual Caster](/wiki/Ritual_Caster "Ritual Caster") feat instead allows the selection of two ritual spells. Ritual casting does not take any more time than normal casting, but cannot be done in combat.
  - Some spells that are not ritual spells in 5e have become so, such as [![Disguise Self](/w/images/thumb/8/8d/Disguise_Self_Icon.webp/20px-Disguise_Self_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Disguise_Self "Disguise Self") [Disguise Self](/wiki/Disguise_Self "Disguise Self"); see [D&D 5e spell changes](/wiki/D%26D_5e_spell_changes "D&D 5e spell changes") for more.
- Anyone can cast any [spell scroll](/wiki/Spell_scroll "Spell scroll") from any class list, even if they do not have the Spellcasting feature, and there is no [Arcana](/wiki/Arcana "Arcana") check for casting spells of a higher level than a character could normally cast. Wizards are still unable to copy a scroll into their spellbook if the spell is of a level higher than they have spell slots.
- Spell scrolls do not have a fixed DC or attack roll bonus. They use the caster's spellcasting ability (see [spellcasting ability](/wiki/Spellcasting_ability "Spellcasting ability") for how this is determined for multiclass characters and characters without the spellcasting feature).
- A character with at least one level in [wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard") can [transcribe](/wiki/Transcribing_scrolls "Transcribing scrolls") scrolls of any level up to which they have spell slots. For example, a character with 1 level in wizard and 2 levels in cleric can learn level 2 wizard spells by transcribing them from scrolls, since their [effective spellcaster level](/wiki/Spells#Spell_slots "Spells") is 3, granting them level 2 spell slots.
- Cantrips receive their second upgrade at character level 10, rather than level 11.
- Due to the level cap being 12, characters generally do not have access to spells of 7th level or higher (as 7th-level spell slots become available at class level 13).
  - The exceptions are a few spells that can only be used by NPCs that are technically classified as 9th level, and [![Power Word Kill](/w/images/thumb/1/1e/Power_Word_Kill_Icon.webp/20px-Power_Word_Kill_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Power_Word_Kill "Power Word Kill") [Power Word Kill](/wiki/Power_Word_Kill "Power Word Kill"), which is available as a one-time-use ability if specific choices are made in the story.

### Action economy

- A character can have one ranged weapon set equipped and one melee weapon set equipped and is able to freely switch between them an unlimited number of times per turn. For example, a character can start their turn wielding their melee weapon(s), switch to their bow for free, make a [![Ranged Attack](/w/images/thumb/c/c3/Ranged_Attack_Icon.webp/20px-Ranged_Attack_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") [Ranged Attack](/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), and then switch back to their melee set to allow them to make [![Opportunity Attack](/w/images/thumb/3/35/Opportunity_Attack_Icon.webp/20px-Opportunity_Attack_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Opportunity_Attack "Opportunity Attack") [Opportunity Attacks](/wiki/Opportunity_Attack "Opportunity Attack"). A character able to make multiple attacks with one action can switch weapons between attacks. Equipping a weapon from the ground, a container, or an inventory costs an action.
- [![Shove](/w/images/thumb/6/6b/Shove_Icon.webp/20px-Shove_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Shove "Shove") [Shove](/wiki/Shove "Shove") is not a part of the attack action. It is a bonus action available to all characters. Shove only pushes the target back an amount that depends on the shover's Strength and the target's weight. It usually does not knock a target prone unless they are shoved off a high ledge. (Some creatures are shown falling to the ground when shoved, but this is purely cosmetic as they get up again immediately without spending any movement, and this doesn't apply the [![Prone](/w/images/thumb/8/8e/Prone_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Prone_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Prone_(Condition) "Prone (Condition)") [Prone](/wiki/Prone_(Condition) "Prone (Condition)") condition at any point.)
- [Falling damage](/wiki/Falling_damage "Falling damage") is determined by fall height and deals consistent damage based on a percentage of a creature's max HP. It is also guaranteed to cause a creature to go Prone if a fall deals more than 25% of their max HP and will never cause a creature to go prone otherwise.
- Using the [![Help](/w/images/thumb/8/89/Help_Icon.webp/20px-Help_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Help "Help") [Help](/wiki/Help "Help") action on a [![Downed](/w/images/thumb/6/66/Downed_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Downed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)") [Downed](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)") ally brings them back to 1 hit point. This action can also be used to remove various other harmful conditions, such as [![Ensnared](/w/images/thumb/1/1e/Ensnaring_Strike_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Ensnaring_Strike_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Ensnared_(Ensnaring_Strike)_(Condition) "Ensnared (Ensnaring Strike) (Condition)") [Ensnared](/wiki/Ensnared_(Ensnaring_Strike)_(Condition) "Ensnared (Ensnaring Strike) (Condition)"), [![Enwebbed](/w/images/thumb/b/b9/Enwebbed_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Enwebbed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Enwebbed_(Condition) "Enwebbed (Condition)") [Enwebbed](/wiki/Enwebbed_(Condition) "Enwebbed (Condition)"), or [![Sleeping](/w/images/thumb/3/35/Sleeping_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Sleeping_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Sleeping_(Condition) "Sleeping (Condition)") [Sleeping](/wiki/Sleeping_(Condition) "Sleeping (Condition)"). It cannot be used to provide an ally advantage on an attack roll or ability check.
- [![Jumping](/w/images/thumb/2/28/Jump_Icon.webp/20px-Jump_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Jump "Jump") [Jumping](/wiki/Jump "Jump") is a bonus action which consumes 10ft of movement speed. With a Strength score of 10 or below, a creature can jump 15ft, and this increases by 3ft for every two points in Strength above 10. The jump always consumes 10 feet of movement regardless of the actual distance jumped, effectively increasing the creature's movement speed by up to 20 feet (at 20 Strength). Characters do not roll an [Athletics](/wiki/Athletics "Athletics") check to avoid being hit by an obstacle they are jumping over, or an [Acrobatics](/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check to avoid falling Prone when landing in difficult terrain.
- The Dodge action is not implemented, though the [monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk")'s [![Patient Defence](/w/images/thumb/8/81/Patient_Defence_Icon.webp/20px-Patient_Defence_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Patient_Defence "Patient Defence") [Patient Defence](/wiki/Patient_Defence "Patient Defence") and the [![Battle Master Fighter](/w/images/thumb/7/73/Class_Fighter_Battle_Master_Hotbar_Icon.png/20px-Class_Fighter_Battle_Master_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Battle_Master "Battle Master") [Battle Master Fighter](/wiki/Battle_Master "Battle Master")'s [![Evasive Footwork](/w/images/thumb/b/b6/Evasive_Footwork_Icon.webp/20px-Evasive_Footwork_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Evasive_Footwork "Evasive Footwork") [Evasive Footwork](/wiki/Evasive_Footwork "Evasive Footwork") manoeuvre function similarly.
- Readying an action is not implemented.
- Grappling is not implemented.
- Consuming a [potion](/wiki/Potions "Potions") only costs a bonus action. This greatly increases the utility of drinking potions while in combat.
- If a creature [![Throws](/w/images/thumb/e/e3/Throw_Icon.webp/20px-Throw_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Throw "Throw") [Throws](/wiki/Throw "Throw") a potion as an action, it will break and affect all targets in a small radius. This applies to many – but not all – types of potions.
- A character can Throw creatures which are more than one size larger. There is a minimum [Strength](/wiki/Strength "Strength") required, based on the target's weight, and the thrower must succeed on a contested check using Athletics against the target's passive Athletics or Acrobatics.
  - Contested ability checks have the acting character roll an ability check against a DC of the target's passive skill.
- Bonus action attack options, such as off-hand weapon attacks, do not require a character to first take the Attack action.
  - Exceptions include the attacks gained from the [Polearm Master](/wiki/Polearm_Master "Polearm Master") feat and the monk's [![Martial Arts: Bonus Unarmed Strike](/w/images/thumb/9/91/Martial_Arts_Bonus_Unarmed_Strike_Icon.webp/20px-Martial_Arts_Bonus_Unarmed_Strike_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Martial_Arts:_Bonus_Unarmed_Strike "Martial Arts: Bonus Unarmed Strike") [Martial Arts: Bonus Unarmed Strike](/wiki/Martial_Arts:_Bonus_Unarmed_Strike "Martial Arts: Bonus Unarmed Strike"), which are not available unless at least one attack was already made on the same turn.
- Donning and doffing armour only takes an action, regardless of armour type, making it feasible during combat.
- A character regains their reaction at the end of their turn, not at the start of it.
- Effects granting restricted extra actions generally lack those restrictions. For example, the extra action given by the [![Haste](/w/images/thumb/1/11/Haste_Icon.webp/20px-Haste_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Haste "Haste") [Haste](/wiki/Haste "Haste") spell is not limited to making a single attack and can be used to cast spells.
  - In [Honour Mode](/wiki/Honour_Mode "Honour Mode"), these features are heavily nerfed and mostly brought in line with their D&D counterparts.

### Deleted/Removed Actions

- There is no Dodge action, though monks retain [![Patient Defence](/w/images/thumb/8/81/Patient_Defence_Icon.webp/20px-Patient_Defence_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Patient_Defence "Patient Defence") [Patient Defence](/wiki/Patient_Defence "Patient Defence"), which allows them to dodge as a bonus action.
- There is no Ready action (a character cannot ready an action and wait for a target to trigger the action as a Reaction).
- The Help action cannot be used to give allies advantage on a skill check or attack roll. The Help action instead has an entirely different function, which is to heal [![Downed](/w/images/thumb/6/66/Downed_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Downed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)") [Downed](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)") allies for 1 hit point or remove certain negative conditions.

### Equipment

- [Weapons](/wiki/Weapons "Weapons") are given special [weapon actions](/wiki/Weapon_actions "Weapon actions") depending on the weapon type. These can only be used if the wielder is proficient with the weapon, and generally can only be used once each per [short rest](/wiki/Short_rest "Short rest"). Some magic weapons have additional weapon actions unique to them.
- When a character is standing on a [surface](/wiki/Surface "Surface") such as fire, acid, or poison, most weapons can be [![Dipped](/w/images/thumb/a/a8/Dip_Icon.webp/20px-Dip_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Dip "Dip") [Dipped](/wiki/Dip "Dip") in the surface as a bonus action. This causes them to deal additional damage of an appropriate type with each attack for a few turns.
- There is no magic item attunement system. All magic items can be equipped immediately, and it is possible to equip one in each equipment slot. There are two slots for [rings](/wiki/Rings "Rings").
- Ammunition does not exist for regular weapon attacks. Only [special arrows](/wiki/Arrows "Arrows") come in finite quantities.
- A character can have a melee weapon set and a ranged weapon set equipped that they can toggle between freely. If a shield is equipped in the melee set but the ranged weapon set is being actively wielded, a creature still benefits from the AC boost the shield provides.
- [![Opportunity Attacks](/w/images/thumb/3/35/Opportunity_Attack_Icon.webp/20px-Opportunity_Attack_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Opportunity_Attack "Opportunity Attack") [Opportunity Attacks](/wiki/Opportunity_Attack "Opportunity Attack") cannot be used without a melee weapon currently in hand. It is not possible to use a ranged weapon as an improvised weapon or use a melee weapon together with a hand crossbow.
  - Opportunity Attacks seem to be tied directly to the [![Threatened](/w/images/thumb/1/13/Threatened_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Threatened_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Threatened_(Condition) "Threatened (Condition)") [Threatened](/wiki/Threatened_(Condition) "Threatened (Condition)") condition, which only occurs in the reach of a melee-equipped hostile.
  - Opportunity Attacks can be enabled after making a ranged attack by manually switching back to a melee weapon before ending a character's turn.
- If an enemy moves out of reach of a character wielding two melee weapons who has Dual Wielding toggled on their hotbar, two separate opportunity attacks (one with each weapon) are made as part of the same reaction. The attack animation shows two separate strikes. As with a normal two-weapon attack, the off-hand attack's damage roll lacks the ability score modifier unless the character has the [![Two-Weapon Fighting](/w/images/thumb/5/54/Two-Weapon_Fighting_Icon.webp/20px-Two-Weapon_Fighting_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Two-Weapon_Fighting "Two-Weapon Fighting") [Two-Weapon Fighting](/wiki/Two-Weapon_Fighting "Two-Weapon Fighting") fighting style or a similar feature.
- Characters other than [monks](/wiki/Monk "Monk") can only make [![Unarmed Strikes](/w/images/thumb/4/40/Unarmed_Strike_Icon.webp/20px-Unarmed_Strike_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Unarmed_Strike "Unarmed Strike") [Unarmed Strikes](/wiki/Unarmed_Strike "Unarmed Strike") when they have no melee weapon equipped. Tabletop D&D generally allows unarmed strikes such as kicks and headbutts while one's hands are full.
- Some [equipment](/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), like gloves, boots, or helmets, may require armour proficiency to wear effectively. They don't grant a bonus to armour class, unless they have a magical effect that specifically says so, and a character will only be able to don them effectively with the correct proficiency. These items also count as armour for the purpose of effects that only apply when wearing armour (e.g. [![Defence](/w/images/thumb/e/e2/Defence_Icon.webp/20px-Defence_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Defence "Defence") [Defence](/wiki/Defence "Defence") fighting style) or when not wearing armour (e.g. [![Unarmoured Defence](/w/images/thumb/f/f6/Unarmoured_Defence_%28Barbarian%29_Icon.webp/20px-Unarmoured_Defence_%28Barbarian%29_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Unarmoured_Defence_(Barbarian) "Unarmoured Defence (Barbarian)") [Unarmoured Defence](/wiki/Unarmoured_Defence_(Barbarian) "Unarmoured Defence (Barbarian)")).
- [Heavy armour](/wiki/Heavy_armour "Heavy armour") does not have a Strength requirement, and therefore no movement speed penalty.
- [![](/w/images/thumb/9/98/Padded_Armour_3_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Padded_Armour_3_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Padded_Armour "Padded Armour") [Padded Armour](/wiki/Padded_Armour "Padded Armour") does not impose disadvantage on [Stealth](/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks, making it mechanically equivalent to [![](/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Leather_Armour_3_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Leather_Armour_3_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Leather_Armour "Leather Armour") [Leather Armour](/wiki/Leather_Armour "Leather Armour"), though it still weighs less and costs less.
- Starting equipment cannot be selected; it is given based only on a character's starting [class](/wiki/Class "Class"), not [background](/wiki/Background "Background").
- Ranged attacks have disadvantage if the target is within 10ft, rather than 5ft.
- Crossbows do not have the loading property restriction. Combined with the homogenisation of ranges, this leads to non-magical crossbows usually being superior to bows.
  - This also allows a character to dual-wield hand crossbows. It does not allow a character to wield a hand crossbow with a shield because the crossbow goes in the ranged set, while the shield goes in the melee set. But, as discussed above, the character gains the AC bonus of a shield equipped on their melee set even while the ranged set is active.
- The [heavy](/wiki/Heavy_(weapon_property) "Heavy (weapon property)") weapon property was initially included in the game, but it did not apply disadvantage to attack rolls made by small characters, and was subsequently removed from the game in a later update.
- [![Extra Reach](/w/images/thumb/8/83/Extra_Reach_Icon.png/25px-Extra_Reach_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Extra_Reach "Extra Reach") [Extra Reach](/wiki/Extra_Reach "Extra Reach") weapons have an effective range of 2.5m/8.3ft, rather than 10ft.
- Slings, darts, blowguns, lances, nets, and whips are not in the game.
  - The [Sling](/wiki/Sling "Sling") and [Dart](/wiki/Dart "Dart") exist in the code, but are inaccessible in normal gameplay. They can be accessed through third-party tools or mods.
- The weights of various pieces of equipment are changed. For example, [![](/w/images/thumb/e/e6/Chain_Mail_2_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Chain_Mail_2_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Chain_Mail "Chain Mail") [Chain Mail](/wiki/Chain_Mail "Chain Mail") weighs 36lbs instead of 55lbs.
- Thieves' tools are split into two items: [![](/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Thieves_Tools_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Thieves_Tools_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Thieves%27_Tools "Thieves' Tools") [Thieves' Tools](/wiki/Thieves%27_Tools "Thieves' Tools") for lockpicking and [![](/w/images/thumb/b/bf/Trap_Disarm_Toolkit_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Trap_Disarm_Toolkit_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Trap_Disarm_Toolkit "Trap Disarm Toolkit") [Trap Disarm Toolkits](/wiki/Trap_Disarm_Toolkit "Trap Disarm Toolkit") for disarming traps. These are consumed on each unsuccessful use. Because there are no tool proficiencies, the [Sleight of Hand](/wiki/Sleight_of_Hand "Sleight of Hand") skill is used for these checks.
- [![](/w/images/thumb/1/10/Soul_Coin_Unfaded_Icon.webp/20px-Soul_Coin_Unfaded_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Soul_Coin "Soul Coin") [Soul Coins](/wiki/Soul_Coin "Soul Coin") can be used to power infernal engines (specifically, the one inside [![Karlach](/w/images/thumb/b/b2/Portrait_Karlach.png/25px-Portrait_Karlach.png.webp)](/wiki/Karlach "Karlach") [Karlach](/wiki/Karlach "Karlach")), but the other uses defined for them in the adventure *Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus* – asking questions of the imprisoned soul or siphoning its essence for temporary hit points – are not implemented.

### Conditions

- [![Blinded](/w/images/thumb/7/70/Blinded_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Blinded_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Blinded_(Condition) "Blinded (Condition)") [Blinded](/wiki/Blinded_(Condition) "Blinded (Condition)"): In addition to its regular effects, ranged attacks are limited to a range of 10ft.
- Characters can still be targeted with attacks and spells while [Obscured](/wiki/Obscured "Obscured"), overcoming effects like [![Darkness](/w/images/thumb/b/bf/Darkness_Icon.webp/20px-Darkness_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Darkness "Darkness") [Darkness](/wiki/Darkness "Darkness") and [![Fog Cloud](/w/images/thumb/c/c0/Fog_Cloud_Icon.webp/20px-Fog_Cloud_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Fog_Cloud "Fog Cloud") [Fog Cloud](/wiki/Fog_Cloud "Fog Cloud"), provided the attacker gets close enough (as described above with the Blinded condition). The AI can and will abuse this to get advantage on characters Blinded by these effects or to target AoEs inside the areas.
  - Unlike with most spells in 5e, a spellcaster can target creatures they cannot see with spells.
- The Deafened condition is not implemented.
- [![Encumbered](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Encumbered_(Condition) "Encumbered (Condition)") [Encumbered](/wiki/Encumbered_(Condition) "Encumbered (Condition)"): A character becomes encumbered when carrying an amount greater than 80% of their maximum carrying capacity, rather than 5lbs times their Strength. Being encumbered also halves jump distance rather than just reducing speed.
  - [![Heavily Encumbered](/w/images/thumb/e/ec/Heavily_Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Heavily_Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Heavily_Encumbered_(Condition) "Heavily Encumbered (Condition)") [Heavily Encumbered](/wiki/Heavily_Encumbered_(Condition) "Heavily Encumbered (Condition)"): A character becomes heavily encumbered when carrying an amount greater than 93.33% of their maximum carrying capacity, rather than 10lbs times their Strength. It also prevents them from being able to climb or jump at all, in addition to its effects in 5e.
- Exhaustion is not implemented.
- [![Frightened](/w/images/thumb/7/75/Frightened_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Frightened_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Frightened_(Condition) "Frightened (Condition)") [Frightened](/wiki/Frightened_(Condition) "Frightened (Condition)"): Creatures which are frightened are unable to move at all, rather than being unable to move toward the source of their fear.
  - [![Fearful](/w/images/thumb/b/b5/Fearful_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Fearful_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Fearful_(Condition) "Fearful (Condition)") [Fearful](/wiki/Fearful_(Condition) "Fearful (Condition)"): Identical to Frightened, except instead of being unable to move, the target must run from the source of its fear and cannot take any additional actions.
- [![Grappled](/w/images/thumb/6/60/Grappled_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Grappled_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Grappled_(Condition) "Grappled (Condition)") [Grappled](/wiki/Grappled_(Condition) "Grappled (Condition)"): The only creatures that can grapple are the [![Tentacle](/w/images/thumb/0/0f/Portrait_Tentacle.png/25px-Portrait_Tentacle.png.webp)](/wiki/Tentacle "Tentacle") [Tentacle](/wiki/Tentacle "Tentacle") and the [![Shadow Creeper](/w/images/thumb/7/75/Portrait_Shadow_Creeper.png/25px-Portrait_Shadow_Creeper.png.webp)](/wiki/Shadow_Creeper "Shadow Creeper") [Shadow Creeper](/wiki/Shadow_Creeper "Shadow Creeper").
- [![Paralysed](/w/images/thumb/0/09/Paralysed_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Paralysed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Paralysed_(Condition) "Paralysed (Condition)") [Paralysed](/wiki/Paralysed_(Condition) "Paralysed (Condition)"): Attacks from within 10ft automatically hit and are always critical hits, rather than hits from within 5ft being automatic critical hits.
- [![Prone](/w/images/thumb/8/8e/Prone_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Prone_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Prone_(Condition) "Prone (Condition)") [Prone](/wiki/Prone_(Condition) "Prone (Condition)"): Being prone gives disadvantage on [Strength](/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Dexterity](/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") [saving throws](/wiki/Saving_throws "Saving throws"), attacks against a prone creature have [advantage](/wiki/Advantage "Advantage") out to a range of 10ft rather than 5ft, and ranged attacks against a prone creature do not have disadvantage. A creature cannot do anything while prone. Starting their turn while prone will cause a creature to automatically use half their movement to stand up; if a creature becomes prone during their own turn, the turn automatically ends. Becoming Prone will also make a creature stop concentrating on a spell or similar ability that requires concentration.
- [![Turned](/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Turned_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Turned_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Turned_(Condition) "Turned (Condition)") [Turned](/wiki/Turned_(Condition) "Turned (Condition)"): Turned creatures will not dodge, as it is not implemented as an action. If a Turned creature cannot move, it will just stay where it is and do nothing.
- [![Wet](/w/images/thumb/9/9d/Wet_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Wet_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Wet_(Condition) "Wet (Condition)") [Wet](/wiki/Wet_(Condition) "Wet (Condition)"): This is a new condition that prevents the character from [![Burning](/w/images/thumb/c/c8/Burning_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Burning_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Burning_(Condition) "Burning (Condition)") [Burning](/wiki/Burning_(Condition) "Burning (Condition)") and grants [resistance](/wiki/Resistance "Resistance") to [![fire](/w/images/thumb/8/84/Fire_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Fire_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Fire "Fire")⁠[fire](/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage, but also makes the creature [vulnerable](/wiki/Vulnerable "Vulnerable") to [![lightning](/w/images/thumb/3/31/Lightning_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Lightning_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Lightning "Lightning")⁠[lightning](/wiki/Lightning "Lightning") and [![cold](/w/images/thumb/0/05/Cold_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Cold_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Cold "Cold")⁠[cold](/wiki/Cold "Cold") damage.
- [![Threatened](/w/images/thumb/1/13/Threatened_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Threatened_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Threatened_(Condition) "Threatened (Condition)") [Threatened](/wiki/Threatened_(Condition) "Threatened (Condition)"): Imposes disadvantage to ranged attacks within 10ft of the target, unlike in 5e, where this penalty's range is 5ft.

### Miscellaneous

- The [![Great Weapon Fighting](/w/images/thumb/1/1f/Great_Weapon_Fighting_Icon.webp/20px-Great_Weapon_Fighting_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Great_Weapon_Fighting "Great Weapon Fighting") [Great Weapon Fighting](/wiki/Great_Weapon_Fighting "Great Weapon Fighting") fighting style rerolls all of an attack's damage dice on a 1 or 2, even if they are not part of the weapon's base damage.
- "Lifeberries," a.k.a. [![](/w/images/thumb/5/51/FOOD_Goodberry_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-FOOD_Goodberry_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Goodberry_(Item) "Goodberry (Item)") [Goodberries](/wiki/Goodberry_(Item) "Goodberry (Item)") that have been buffed by [![Disciple of Life](/w/images/thumb/9/9b/Disciple_of_Life_Icon.webp/20px-Disciple_of_Life_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Disciple_of_Life "Disciple of Life") [Disciple of Life](/wiki/Disciple_of_Life "Disciple of Life") from a [![Life Domain Cleric](/w/images/thumb/7/72/Class_Cleric_Life_Hotbar_Icon.png/20px-Class_Cleric_Life_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Life_Domain "Life Domain") [Life Domain Cleric](/wiki/Life_Domain "Life Domain"), do not work in BG3.
- The [![Haunted One](/w/images/thumb/8/8c/Background_Haunted_One_Icon.png/20px-Background_Haunted_One_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Haunted_One "Haunted One") [Haunted One](/wiki/Haunted_One "Haunted One") background grants proficiency in [Medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") and [Intimidation](/wiki/Intimidation "Intimidation"), neither of which is among the four skill options for the 5e version of this background as defined in *Curse of Strahd*.
- If a [background](/wiki/Background "Background") provides proficiency in an already chosen skill, there is no option to select another skill to replace it. Therefore, [wood elf](/wiki/Wood_elf "Wood elf") [![Criminal](/w/images/thumb/6/68/Background_Criminal_Icon.png/20px-Background_Criminal_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Criminal "Criminal") [Criminals](/wiki/Criminal "Criminal") and [half-orc](/wiki/Half-orc "Half-orc") [![Soldier](/w/images/thumb/b/ba/Background_Soldier_Icon.png/20px-Background_Soldier_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") [Soldiers](/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"), for example, lose a skill proficiency as [Stealth](/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") and Intimidation are provided by both race and background.
