# The Fiend

**The Fiend** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Warlock#Level_1 "Warlock") of [Warlock](/wiki/Warlock "Warlock"). These Warlocks are servants of chaotic and destructive beings such as demons or devils and traded their souls to these dark forces in exchange for power. They also have the ability to gain additional [Hit Points](/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") upon dealing the finishing blow to enemies.
