# Origins

Chosen during [character creation](/wiki/Character_creation "Character creation"), each of the seven origins comes with a predefined backstory, as well as their own unique [personal quest](/wiki/Quests#Origin_personal_quests "Quests") and interactions. As an alternative, a custom avatar is also available which does not come with its own quest. All origins except the Dark Urge are available in-game as potential [companions](/wiki/Companions "Companions"), who can join the chosen avatar's party.

Most origins also have a set name, [background](/wiki/Background "Background"), [race](/wiki/Race "Race"), gender and appearance that *cannot* be changed, while the name, gender and appearance of *Custom* and *Dark Urge* characters can be changed during character creation and in-game with the [Magic Mirror](/wiki/Magic_Mirror "Magic Mirror"). However, once character creation is completed and the game begins, the body type of Custom and Dark Urge characters is set and can no longer be changed.

The [background](/wiki/Background "Background") of Dark Urge characters is *always* [Haunted One](/wiki/Haunted_One "Haunted One"), while the background of the custom avatar can be changed during character creation. The default appearance of the other six *Origins*, with provided histories and backgrounds, cannot be altered in character creation or with the Magic Mirror, although certain items and spells may do so - usually on a temporary basis.
