# Way Of The Four Elements

**Way of the Four Elements** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Monk#Level_3 "Monk") of [monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk"). These monks are more specialized in using their ![Monk_Ki_Icon.png](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/25px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp) Ki to channel the elements to influence the battlefield. Rather than focusing on physical techniques that the other Monk subclasses focus on, Way of the Four Elements focuses on abilities that mimic [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard") spells, turning monks into a hybrid spellcaster.
