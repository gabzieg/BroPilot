# Circle Of The Moon

**Circle of the Moon** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Druid#Subclass "Druid") of [Druid](/wiki/Druid "Druid"). These Druids are more specialized in [Wild Shaping](/wiki/Wild_Shape "Wild Shape") into various creatures to attack enemies or support allies in combat. They also have the ability to regain [Hit Points](/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") while Wild Shaped.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Druid](/wiki/Druid "Druid"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*
