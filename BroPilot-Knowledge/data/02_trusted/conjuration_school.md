# Conjuration School

This page is about the Wizard subclass. For a list of all spells of this school, see [Conjuration](/wiki/Conjuration "Conjuration").

**Conjuration School** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Wizard#Level_2 "Wizard") of [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"). These Wizards specialize in summoning the elements and moving themselves and others from one place to another.

![](/w/images/thumb/b/bc/Class_Wizard_Conjuration_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Wizard_Conjuration_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> Compelled by shape and motion, your favoured arcane style involves summoning creatures and objects, as well as displacing them in space.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 2

Conjuration Savant
:   Halves the cost to learn Conjuration spells from [Scrolls](/wiki/Scrolls "Scrolls"). Learning these spells will only cost **![25 gp](/w/images/thumb/f/f5/Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png/23px-Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png.webp) 25 gp** per spell level.

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

:   Call forth rain. It extinguishes exposed flames and forms a [![Water](/w/images/thumb/1/1c/Water_surface_Icon.webp/25px-Water_surface_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Water_(surface) "Water (surface)") [Water](/wiki/Water_(surface) "Water (surface)") surface. (![Recharge: Short rest](/w/images/thumb/f/fd/Recharge_Icons.png/25px-Recharge_Icons.png.webp) Recharge: [Short rest](/wiki/Short_rest "Short rest").)

### Level 6

[![Benign Transposition: Teleport](/w/images/thumb/a/a6/Benign_Transposition_Teleport_Icon.webp/40px-Benign_Transposition_Teleport_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Benign_Transposition:_Teleport "Benign Transposition: Teleport") [Benign Transposition: Teleport](/wiki/Benign_Transposition:_Teleport "Benign Transposition: Teleport") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp))
