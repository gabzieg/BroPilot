# Arcane Archer

**Arcane Archer** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Fighter#Level_3 "Fighter") of [Fighter](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter"). It focuses on using magical arrows to damage and control enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

![](/w/images/thumb/c/c2/Class_Fighter_Arcane_Archer_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Fighter_Arcane_Archer_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> You are capable of weaving magic into your attacks, giving your archery a supernatural quality - and your foes something to fear.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Fighter](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*
