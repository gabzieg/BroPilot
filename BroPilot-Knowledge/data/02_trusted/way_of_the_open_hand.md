# Way Of The Open Hand

**Way of the Open Hand** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Monk#Level_3 "Monk") of [Monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk"). It can be chosen at level 3. These monks are more specialized in using unarmed attacks to strike their opponents down.

![](/w/images/thumb/c/c6/Class_Monk_Open_Hand_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Monk_Open_Hand_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> You specialise in unarmed combat, using your hands and your control of ki to heal or inflict grievous hurt.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 3

Gain unique, upgraded variants of [![Flurry of Blows](/w/images/thumb/e/ef/Flurry_of_Blows_Icon.webp/25px-Flurry_of_Blows_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Flurry_of_Blows "Flurry of Blows") [Flurry of Blows](/wiki/Flurry_of_Blows "Flurry of Blows").

[![Flurry of Blows: Topple](/w/images/thumb/8/81/Flurry_of_Blows_Topple_Icon.webp/40px-Flurry_of_Blows_Topple_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Flurry_of_Blows:_Topple "Flurry of Blows: Topple") [Flurry of Blows: Topple](/wiki/Flurry_of_Blows:_Topple "Flurry of Blows: Topple") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Ki Point](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp))
:   Punch twice in quick succession and potentially knock the target [![Prone](/w/images/thumb/8/8e/Prone_Condition_Icon.webp/25px-Prone_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Prone_(Condition) "Prone (Condition)") [Prone](/wiki/Prone_(Condition) "Prone (Condition)").

[![Flurry of Blows: Stagger](/w/images/thumb/4/43/Flurry_of_Blows_Stagger_Icon.webp/40px-Flurry_of_Blows_Stagger_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Flurry_of_Blows:_Stagger "Flurry of Blows: Stagger") [Flurry of Blows: Stagger](/wiki/Flurry_of_Blows:_Stagger "Flurry of Blows: Stagger") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Ki Point](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp))
