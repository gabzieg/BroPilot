# Death Domain

**Death Domain** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Cleric#Level_1 "Cleric") of [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"). It has potent features which boost necrotic spells and add necrotic damage to weapon attacks. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

Player selectable [Deities](/wiki/Deities "Deities") associated with this domain: [![Kelemvor](/w/images/thumb/2/2e/Kelemvor_Icon.webp/20px-Kelemvor_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Kelemvor "Kelemvor") [Kelemvor](/wiki/Kelemvor "Kelemvor"), [![Laduguer](/w/images/thumb/0/04/Laduguer_Icon.webp/18px-Laduguer_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Laduguer "Laduguer") [Laduguer](/wiki/Laduguer "Laduguer"), and [![Shar](/w/images/thumb/6/69/Shar_Icon.webp/20px-Shar_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Shar "Shar") [Shar](/wiki/Shar "Shar") (Shadowheart only).
