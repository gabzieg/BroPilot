# Hunter

**Hunter** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Ranger#Level_3 "Ranger") of [Ranger](/wiki/Ranger "Ranger"). These Rangers are more specialized in dealing high damage to enemies, using [Spellcasting](/wiki/Spells "Spells"), and area of effect and utility attacks to hunt down their prey.
