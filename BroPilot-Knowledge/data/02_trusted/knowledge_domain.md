# Knowledge Domain

**Knowledge Domain** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Cleric#Subclass "Cleric") of [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"). These Clerics are more specialized in arcane magic.

Player selectable [Deities](/wiki/Deities "Deities") associated with this domain: [![Moradin](/w/images/thumb/a/ac/Moradin_Icon.webp/16px-Moradin_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Moradin "Moradin") [Moradin](/wiki/Moradin "Moradin"), [![Mystra](/w/images/thumb/e/ec/Mystra_Icon.webp/20px-Mystra_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Mystra "Mystra") [Mystra](/wiki/Mystra "Mystra"), [![Oghma](/w/images/thumb/9/9b/Oghma_Icon.webp/18px-Oghma_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Oghma "Oghma") [Oghma](/wiki/Oghma "Oghma"), and [![Selûne](/w/images/thumb/e/e1/Sel%C3%BBne_Icon.webp/20px-Sel%C3%BBne_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Sel%C3%BBne "Selûne") [Selûne](/wiki/Sel%C3%BBne "Selûne").

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 1

[![Blessings of Knowledge](/w/images/thumb/c/c5/Blessings_of_Knowledge_Icon.webp/40px-Blessings_of_Knowledge_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Blessings_of_Knowledge "Blessings of Knowledge")[Blessings of Knowledge](/wiki/Blessings_of_Knowledge "Blessings of Knowledge")
:   Gain [Expertise](/wiki/Expertise "Expertise") in two of the following Skills: [![Arcana](/w/images/thumb/a/af/Arcana_Icon.png/25px-Arcana_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Arcana "Arcana") [Arcana](/wiki/Arcana "Arcana"), [![History](/w/images/thumb/c/c5/History_Icon.png/25px-History_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/History "History") [History](/wiki/History "History"), [![Nature](/w/images/thumb/f/fe/Nature_Icon.png/25px-Nature_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Nature "Nature") [Nature](/wiki/Nature "Nature"), or [![Religion](/w/images/thumb/1/1a/Religion_Icon.png/25px-Religion_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Religion "Religion") [Religion](/wiki/Religion "Religion"). Your [Proficiency Bonus](/wiki/Proficiency_Bonus "Proficiency Bonus") is doubled for [Ability Checks](/wiki/Ability_Checks "Ability Checks") made using these Skills.
