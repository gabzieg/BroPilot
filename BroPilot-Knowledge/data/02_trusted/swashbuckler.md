# Swashbuckler

**Swashbuckler** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Rogue#Level_3 "Rogue") of [Rogue](/wiki/Rogue "Rogue"). Unlike other rogues, they focus on fighting enemies head-to-head in single combat. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.
