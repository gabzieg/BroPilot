# Shadow Magic

**Shadow Magic** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Sorcerer#Level_1 "Sorcerer") of [Sorcerer](/wiki/Sorcerer "Sorcerer"). They command a shadowy mastiff which can attack and curse enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Subclass features

[![Sorcerer's default armour](/w/images/thumb/6/6b/Default_Sorcerer_Armor_Shadow.png/230px-Default_Sorcerer_Armor_Shadow.png.webp)](/wiki/File:Default_Sorcerer_Armor_Shadow.png "Sorcerer's default armour")

Sorcerer's default armour

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Sorcerer](/wiki/Sorcerer "Sorcerer"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 1

[![Eyes of the Dark](/w/images/thumb/4/4f/Eyes_of_the_Dark_Icon.webp/40px-Eyes_of_the_Dark_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Eyes_of_the_Dark "Eyes of the Dark")[Eyes of the Dark](/wiki/Eyes_of_the_Dark "Eyes of the Dark")
:   You can see in the dark up to 24 m (80 ft).

[![Strength of the Grave](/w/images/thumb/9/9e/Strength_of_the_Grave_Icon.webp/40px-Strength_of_the_Grave_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Strength_of_the_Grave "Strength of the Grave")[Strength of the Grave](/wiki/Strength_of_the_Grave "Strength of the Grave")
:   After reaching 0 [Hit Points](/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you regain 1 instead of becoming [downed](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)").

### Level 3

[![Darkness](/w/images/thumb/b/bf/Darkness_Icon.webp/40px-Darkness_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Darkness "Darkness") [Darkness](/wiki/Darkness "Darkness") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Level 2 Spell Slot](/w/images/thumb/e/ef/Spell_Slot_Icon.png/20px-Spell_Slot_Icon.png.webp))
:   Create a cloud of magical darkness that [![Heavily Obscures](/w/images/thumb/b/b8/Heavily_Obscured_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Heavily_Obscured_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Heavily_Obscured_(Condition) "Heavily Obscured (Condition)") [Heavily Obscures](/wiki/Heavily_Obscured_(Condition) "Heavily Obscured (Condition)") and inflicts [![Blind](/w/images/thumb/7/70/Blinded_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Blinded_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Blinded_(Condition) "Blinded (Condition)") [Blind](/wiki/Blinded_(Condition) "Blinded (Condition)") to creatures within. Creatures cannot make ranged attacks into or out of it.

[![Eyes of the Dark: Darkness](/w/images/thumb/a/aa/Eyes_of_the_Dark_Darkness_Icon.webp/40px-Eyes_of_the_Dark_Darkness_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Eyes_of_the_Dark:_Darkness "Eyes of the Dark: Darkness") [Eyes of the Dark: Darkness](/wiki/Eyes_of_the_Dark:_Darkness "Eyes of the Dark: Darkness") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp) + 2 ![Sorcery Point](/w/images/thumb/b/be/Sorcery_Points_Icons.png/20px-Sorcery_Points_Icons.png.webp))
:   Create a dark shroud that [Heavily Obscures](/wiki/Heavily_Obscured_(Condition) "Heavily Obscured (Condition)") and [Blinds](/wiki/Blinded_(Condition) "Blinded (Condition)") creatures within. Creatures cannot make ranged attacks into or out of it.

### Level 11

[![Shadow Walk](/w/images/thumb/7/7e/Shadow_Walk_Icon.webp/40px-Shadow_Walk_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Shadow_Walk "Shadow Walk") [Shadow Walk](/wiki/Shadow_Walk "Shadow Walk") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp))

The next spell you cast that turn will benefit from [![Distant Spell](/w/images/thumb/c/ca/Metamagic_Distant_Spell_Icon.webp/20px-Metamagic_Distant_Spell_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Metamagic:_Distant_Spell "Metamagic: Distant Spell") [Distant Spell](/wiki/Metamagic:_Distant_Spell "Metamagic: Distant Spell") without costing any Sorcery Points.
