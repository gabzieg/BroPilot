# Berserker

**Berserker** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Barbarian#Level_3 "Barbarian") of [Barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"). These Barbarians are more specialized in using their anger to gain more abilities while raging in combat.

![](/w/images/thumb/6/62/Class_Barbarian_Berserker_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Barbarian_Berserker_Badge_Icon.png.webp)

## Notes

- The passive feature ***Rage Becomes Frenzy*** is not an available (bonus) action to select. Instead, it refers to a Berserker's ability to automatically enter a state of Frenzy, instead of Rage. Barbarians with the Berserker subclass have this passive listed under the Character Summary of *Notable Features*.
