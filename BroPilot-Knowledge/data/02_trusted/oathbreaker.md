# Oathbreaker

**Oathbreaker** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Paladin#Becoming_an_Oathbreaker "Paladin") of [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"). These Paladins draw power from the darkness within to destroy their foes.

![](/w/images/thumb/c/cc/Class_Paladin_Oathbreaker_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Paladin_Oathbreaker_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> You have broken your sacred Oath in pursuit of power and ambition. Only darkness remains to fuel you now.
>
> „

## Oath

[![](/w/images/thumb/1/13/Portrait_Oathbreaker_Knight.png/70px-Portrait_Oathbreaker_Knight.png.webp)](/wiki/Oathbreaker_Knight "Oathbreaker Knight")
> “
>
> To know the reason for your fall is, perhaps, to know the shape of things to come.
>
> „

— [Oathbreaker Knight](/wiki/Oathbreaker_Knight "Oathbreaker Knight") talking to the fallen Paladin

Unlike other Paladins, Oathbreakers have broken their original oath. For more details, see [Oaths and Oathbreaking](/wiki/Paladin#Oaths_and_Oathbreaking "Paladin").

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 1

[![Spiteful Suffering](/w/images/thumb/e/e6/Spiteful_Suffering_Icon.webp/40px-Spiteful_Suffering_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Spiteful_Suffering "Spiteful Suffering") [Spiteful Suffering](/wiki/Spiteful_Suffering "Spiteful Suffering") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Channel Oath Charge](/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Channel_Oath_Icon.png/20px-Channel_Oath_Icon.png.webp))
:   Steep an enemy in the darkness that churns within you. The target takes 1d4 + [Charisma Modifier](/wiki/Ability_Score_Modifier "Ability Score Modifier")[![Necrotic](/w/images/thumb/3/39/Necrotic_Damage_Icon.png/25px-Necrotic_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Necrotic "Necrotic")[Necrotic](/wiki/Necrotic "Necrotic") damage each turn and [Attack Rolls](/wiki/Attack_Rolls "Attack Rolls") against it have ![Advantage Icon.png](/w/images/thumb/f/fc/Advantage_Icon.png/35px-Advantage_Icon.png.webp) [Advantage](/wiki/Advantage "Advantage").
