# Swarmkeeper

**Swarmkeeper** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Ranger#Level_3 "Ranger") of [Ranger](/wiki/Ranger "Ranger"). They command swarms of fey creatures to assist them in battle. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

![](/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Class_Ranger_Swarmkeeper_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Ranger_Swarmkeeper_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> You have forged a deep connection with your natural surroundings and formed a magical bond with a swarm of nature spirits. This swarm serves as a helpful companion and a potent force in battle.
>
> „

### Level 3

[![Prey's Scent](/w/images/thumb/9/94/Prey%27s_Scent_Icon.webp/40px-Prey%27s_Scent_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Prey%27s_Scent_(Swarmkeeper) "Prey's Scent (Swarmkeeper)")[Prey's Scent](/wiki/Prey%27s_Scent_(Swarmkeeper) "Prey's Scent (Swarmkeeper)")

Gathered Swarm
:   Choose the swarm of nature spirits you've formed a bond with. You can change your swarm whenever you level up. An ability for the chosen swarm can be used once per round after landing an attack.
:   [![Cloud of Jellyfish](/w/images/thumb/d/d4/Cloud_of_Jellyfish_Icon.webp/40px-Cloud_of_Jellyfish_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Cloud_of_Jellyfish "Cloud of Jellyfish")[Cloud of Jellyfish](/wiki/Cloud_of_Jellyfish "Cloud of Jellyfish")
    :   You gain a swarm of jellyfish that fights on your side. Once per round, the swarm can assist you in one of three ways after you attack an enemy: it can attack and deal additional [![Lightning](/w/images/thumb/3/31/Lightning_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Lightning_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Lightning "Lightning")⁠[Lightning](/wiki/Lightning "Lightning") damage, [![Shock](/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Shocked_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Shocked_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Shocked_(Condition) "Shocked (Condition)") [Shock](/wiki/Shocked_(Condition) "Shocked (Condition)") your enemy, or teleport you to a safer location.

    [![Flurry of Moths](/w/images/thumb/f/fa/Flurry_of_Moths_Icon.webp/40px-Flurry_of_Moths_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Flurry_of_Moths "Flurry of Moths")[Flurry of Moths](/wiki/Flurry_of_Moths "Flurry of Moths")
    :   You gain a swarm of moths that fights on your side. Once per round, the swarm can assist you in one of three ways after you attack an enemy: it can attack and deal additional [![Psychic](/w/images/thumb/0/04/Psychic_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Psychic_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Psychic "Psychic")⁠[Psychic](/wiki/Psychic "Psychic") damage, [![Blind](/w/images/thumb/7/70/Blinded_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Blinded_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Blinded_(Condition) "Blinded (Condition)") [Blind](/wiki/Blinded_(Condition) "Blinded (Condition)") your enemy, or teleport you to a safer location.

    [![Legion of Bees](/w/images/thumb/8/86/Legion_of_Bees_Icon.webp/40px-Legion_of_Bees_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Legion_of_Bees "Legion of Bees")[Legion of Bees](/wiki/Legion_of_Bees "Legion of Bees")
    :   You gain a swarm of bees that fights on your side. Once per round, the swarm can assist you in one of three ways after you attack an enemy: it can attack and deal additional [![Piercing](/w/images/thumb/0/0b/Piercing_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Piercing_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Piercing "Piercing")⁠[Piercing](/wiki/Piercing "Piercing") damage, push the enemy away from you, or teleport you to a safer location.
