# The Great Old One

**The Great Old One** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Warlock#Level_1 "Warlock") of [Warlock](/wiki/Warlock "Warlock"). These Warlocks are acolytes of eldritch entities that are incomprehensible to regular mortals. They are given the ability to channel magic through pacts with these ancient beings in order to control the fabric of reality itself.
