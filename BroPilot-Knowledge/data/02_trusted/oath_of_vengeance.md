# Oath Of Vengeance

**Oath of Vengeance** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Paladin#Level_1 "Paladin") of [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin") that focuses on righting wrongs and delivering justice, usually by any means necessary.

![](/w/images/thumb/4/41/Class_Paladin_Vengeance_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Paladin_Vengeance_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> You have set aside even your own purity to right wrongs and deliver justice to those who have committed the most grievous sins.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 1

[![Oath of Vengeance Tenets](/w/images/thumb/1/14/Divine_Intervention_Arm_Thy_Servant_Icon.webp/40px-Divine_Intervention_Arm_Thy_Servant_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Oath_of_Vengeance_Tenets "Oath of Vengeance Tenets")[Oath of Vengeance Tenets](/wiki/Oath_of_Vengeance_Tenets "Oath of Vengeance Tenets")
:   Oath of Vengeance paladins abide by the following tenets:
    > **Fight the Greater Evil.** Exerting your wisdom, identify the higher morality in any given instance, and fight for it.
    >
    > **No Mercy for the Wicked.** Chasten those who dole out their villainy by wiping their blight from the world forever.

[![Inquisitor's Might](/w/images/thumb/2/2a/Inquisitor%27s_Might_Icon.webp/40px-Inquisitor%27s_Might_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Inquisitor%27s_Might "Inquisitor's Might") [Inquisitor's Might](/wiki/Inquisitor%27s_Might "Inquisitor's Might") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Channel Oath Charge](/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Channel_Oath_Icon.png/20px-Channel_Oath_Icon.png.webp))
:   You or an ally's weapon attacks deal an additional Charisma modifier⁠[![Radiant](/w/images/thumb/7/78/Radiant_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Radiant_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Radiant "Radiant")⁠[Radiant](/wiki/Radiant "Radiant") damage and can [![Daze](/w/images/thumb/a/a2/Dazed_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Dazed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Dazed_(Condition) "Dazed (Condition)") [Daze](/wiki/Dazed_(Condition) "Dazed (Condition)") enemies for 1 turn.

## Oathbreaking actions

This section details known actions which cause Paladins to break their Oath of Vengeance.

### Act One

[Roadside Cliffs](/wiki/Roadside_Cliffs "Roadside Cliffs")

- Attacking [Damays](/wiki/Damays "Damays") and [Nymessa](/wiki/Nymessa "Nymessa") (tieflings who guard trapped [Lae'zel](/wiki/Lae%27zel "Lae'zel")) via any dialogue option. Attacking them after they are convinced to leave does not break the oath.
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), chopping off [Gale](/wiki/Gale "Gale")'s hand at the point of his possible recruitment.

[Emerald Grove](/wiki/Emerald_Grove "Emerald Grove")

- Freeing [Sazza](/wiki/Sazza "Sazza") from her cage.
  - Only if the Paladin is the one to free her; if a companion does it, the Oath will not be broken.
- When the [Shadow Druids](/wiki/Shadow_Druids "Shadow Druids") reveal themselves, telling [Kagha](/wiki/Kagha "Kagha") the character won't stand in her way.
- Prodding the wing of the [Exhausted Bird](/wiki/Exhausted_Bird "Exhausted Bird"), or, as [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), selecting the option to "tear her wings off".
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), breaking [Pandirna](/wiki/Pandirna "Pandirna")'s legs.

[Forest](/wiki/Forest "Forest")

- Killing [Scratch](/wiki/Scratch "Scratch") near the [Owlbear Cave](/wiki/Owlbear_Cave "Owlbear Cave").

[Sunlit Wetlands](/wiki/Sunlit_Wetlands "Sunlit Wetlands")/[Putrid Bog](/wiki/Putrid_Bog "Putrid Bog")

- Succumbing to [Auntie Ethel's](/wiki/Ethel "Ethel") will after putting on a [Whispering Mask](/wiki/Whispering_Mask "Whispering Mask").
- Letting Auntie Ethel get away from the fight alive in exchange for her boon, even if [Mayrina](/wiki/Mayrina "Mayrina") is allowed to go free.
  - As of Patch 8, this has been reported not to trigger oathbreaking if the paladin is not the character who speaks to Auntie Ethel.

[Goblin Camp](/wiki/Goblin_Camp "Goblin Camp")

- Torturing [Liam](/wiki/Liam "Liam") in the [Shattered Sanctum](/wiki/Shattered_Sanctum "Shattered Sanctum").
- Revealing the location of the [Emerald Grove](/wiki/Emerald_Grove "Emerald Grove") to [Minthara](/wiki/Minthara "Minthara"), either by showing her or allowing her to read your thoughts.

[Rosymorn Monastery](/wiki/Rosymorn_Monastery "Rosymorn Monastery")/[Creche Y'llek](/wiki/Creche_Y%27llek "Creche Y'llek")

- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), kicking the [githyanki egg](/wiki/Githyanki_egg "Githyanki egg") off the cliffside when talking to [Lady Esther](/wiki/Lady_Esther "Lady Esther").

[Underdark](/wiki/Underdark "Underdark")

- Allowing [Nere](/wiki/Nere "Nere") to kill the enslaved deep gnomes after he is freed from the cave-in.
- Siding with Nere against [Elder Brithvar](/wiki/Brithvar "Brithvar") and his duergar rebels.
- In [Ebonlake Grotto](/wiki/Ebonlake_Grotto "Ebonlake Grotto"), siding with [Sovereign Glut](/wiki/Glut "Glut") and helping it defeat [Sovereign Spaw](/wiki/Spaw "Spaw").
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), throwing [Baelen](/wiki/Baelen "Baelen")'s bag in the midst of spores at the bibberbang field.

[Campsite](/wiki/Campsite "Campsite")

- At the camp celebration with the tieflings, trying to stop [Komira](/wiki/Komira "Komira") from killing [Kagha](/wiki/Kagha "Kagha"), if [Arabella](/wiki/Arabella "Arabella") has died during her judgement.
  - Merely making the attempt is necessary to break the oath, regardless of whether it succeeds.
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), after killing [Alfira](/wiki/Alfira "Alfira"), choosing to "admire this beautiful death".

### Act Two

[Shadow-Cursed Lands](/wiki/Shadow-Cursed_Lands "Shadow-Cursed Lands")

- Attacking [He Who Was](/wiki/He_Who_Was "He Who Was") (through the corresponding dialogue option) instead of judging [Madeline](/wiki/Madeline "Madeline"), or forgiving Madeline, or preventing her from going through with stabbing herself.
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), crushing [Dolly Thrice](/wiki/Dolly_Thrice "Dolly Thrice") after releasing her from the [Moonlantern](/wiki/Moonlantern "Moonlantern").

[Last Light Inn](/wiki/Last_Light_Inn "Last Light Inn")

- Encouraging [Mol](/wiki/Mol "Mol") to sign a contract with [Raphael](/wiki/Raphael "Raphael"), or praising her ambitiousness.
- Siding with [Marcus](/wiki/Marcus_Falgor "Marcus Falgor") when he attempts to kidnap [Isobel](/wiki/Isobel_Thorm "Isobel Thorm").
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), revealing the player character's depravity to the [Strange Ox](/wiki/Strange_Ox "Strange Ox").

[Moonrise Towers](/wiki/Moonrise_Towers "Moonrise Towers")

- On the first visit to Moonrise Towers, killing the goblins on [Disciple Z'rell](/wiki/Z%27rell "Z'rell") orders.
- Helping [Linsella](/wiki/Linsella "Linsella") take back control of [Barnabus](/wiki/Barnabus "Barnabus") the gnoll.
- Letting Questioners [Jasin](/wiki/Jasin "Jasin") and [Sumera](/wiki/Sumera "Sumera") wipe [Minthara](/wiki/Minthara "Minthara")'s mind, or taking part in the execution and erasing her mind (as [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), "Make her NOTHING" option).
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), speaking with [Steelclaw](/wiki/Steelclaw "Steelclaw") and trying to remember what the player character did previously.

[Gauntlet of Shar](/wiki/Gauntlet_of_Shar "Gauntlet of Shar")

- In the [Shadowfell](/wiki/Shadowfell "Shadowfell"), letting [Balthazar](/wiki/Balthazar "Balthazar") take [Aylin](/wiki/Aylin "Aylin") to deliver her to [Ketheric Thorm](/wiki/Ketheric_Thorm "Ketheric Thorm"), or attacking and killing Aylin.

### Act Three

[Rivington](/wiki/Rivington "Rivington")

- Providing the newborn mind flayer in the [Abandoned Windmill](/wiki/Abandoned_Windmill "Abandoned Windmill") basement with a dead body to feed on.
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), revealing the player character's depravity to the [Strange Ox](/wiki/Strange_Ox "Strange Ox").

[Wyrm's Crossing](/wiki/Wyrm%27s_Crossing "Wyrm's Crossing")/[Wyrm's Rock Fortress](/wiki/Wyrm%27s_Rock_Fortress "Wyrm's Rock Fortress")

- Agreeing to take gold from [Arfur Gregorio](/wiki/Arfur_Gregorio "Arfur Gregorio") to 'forget' about rigged toys he donated for refugees.
- Bringing [Wulbren](/wiki/Wulbren "Wulbren")'s head to [Manip Falcäo](/wiki/Manip_Falc%C3%A4o "Manip Falcäo").
- Accepting [Gortash](/wiki/Gortash "Gortash")'s proposal of alliance at the ordaining ceremony.

[Lower City](/wiki/Lower_City "Lower City")

- Talking to [Aradin](/wiki/Aradin_Beno "Aradin Beno") in front of [Sorcerous Sundries](/wiki/Sorcerous_Sundries "Sorcerous Sundries"), and inviting him to try and kidnap [Aylin](/wiki/Aylin "Aylin") from the player's camp.
- Letting [Viconia DeVir](/wiki/Viconia_DeVir "Viconia DeVir") live after defeating her in the [Cloister of Sombre Embrace](/wiki/Cloister_of_Sombre_Embrace "Cloister of Sombre Embrace").
- Refusing to help [Allandra Grey](/wiki/Allandra_Grey "Allandra Grey") with investigating and avenging [Holli Dylford](/wiki/Holli_Dylford "Holli Dylford")'s death.
- Letting [Churg Elvek](/wiki/Churg_Elvek "Churg Elvek") and the crowd execute [Volo](/wiki/Volo "Volo").
- Allowing [Astarion](/wiki/Astarion "Astarion") to complete the [Rite of Profane Ascension](/wiki/The_Pale_Elf#Outcomes "The Pale Elf"), or leaving the imprisoned spawn in their cells after convincing him not to ascend.
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), burying [Bugthimble](/wiki/Bugthimble "Bugthimble") alive at the [Graveyard](/wiki/Graveyard "Graveyard").
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), defeating [Orin](/wiki/Orin "Orin") and accepting [Bhaal](/wiki/Bhaal "Bhaal")'s gift and will.
- Dropping and activating the Motivator in the [Steel Watch Foundry](/wiki/Steel_Watch_Foundry "Steel Watch Foundry").
- During [Impress the Murder Tribunal](/wiki/Impress_the_Murder_Tribunal "Impress the Murder Tribunal"), becoming the Unholy Assassin of Bhaal.
