# Life Domain

**Life Domain** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Cleric#Subclass "Cleric") of [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"). These Clerics are more specialized in preserving life, and their healing spells are more potent.

Player-selectable [deities](/wiki/Deities "Deities") associated with this domain include [![Bahamut](/w/images/thumb/f/fd/Bahamut_Icon.webp/17px-Bahamut_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Bahamut "Bahamut") [Bahamut](/wiki/Bahamut "Bahamut"), [![Corellon Larethian](/w/images/thumb/b/b3/Corellon_Larethian_Icon.webp/20px-Corellon_Larethian_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Corellon_Larethian "Corellon Larethian") [Corellon Larethian](/wiki/Corellon_Larethian "Corellon Larethian"), [![Eilistraee](/w/images/thumb/e/e2/Eilistraee_Icon.webp/14px-Eilistraee_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Eilistraee "Eilistraee") [Eilistraee](/wiki/Eilistraee "Eilistraee"), [![Helm](/w/images/thumb/b/b5/Helm_Icon.webp/11px-Helm_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Helm_(deity) "Helm (deity)") [Helm](/wiki/Helm_(deity) "Helm (deity)"), [![Ilmater](/w/images/thumb/0/05/Ilmater_Icon.webp/18px-Ilmater_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Ilmater "Ilmater") [Ilmater](/wiki/Ilmater "Ilmater"), [![Lathander](/w/images/thumb/9/95/Lathander_Icon.webp/20px-Lathander_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Lathander "Lathander") [Lathander](/wiki/Lathander "Lathander"), [![Selûne](/w/images/thumb/e/e1/Sel%C3%BBne_Icon.webp/20px-Sel%C3%BBne_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Sel%C3%BBne "Selûne") [Selûne](/wiki/Sel%C3%BBne "Selûne"), and [![Yondalla](/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Yondalla_Icon.webp/16px-Yondalla_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Yondalla "Yondalla") [Yondalla](/wiki/Yondalla "Yondalla").
