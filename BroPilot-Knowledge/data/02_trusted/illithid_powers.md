# Illithid Powers

**Illithid powers** are special abilities tied to the [mind flayer](/wiki/Mind_flayer "Mind flayer") tadpole infecting the player character, their [Origin](/wiki/Origin "Origin") companions and certain non-Origin [companions](/wiki/Companions "Companions").

[![](/w/images/thumb/7/7e/Portrait_Shadowheart.png/70px-Portrait_Shadowheart.png.webp)](/wiki/Shadowheart "Shadowheart")
> “
>
> On one hand, they seem useful. But on the other hand - or perhaps tentacle, before long - we know where they're coming from, and what lies at the end of that path...
>
> „

— [Shadowheart](/wiki/Shadowheart "Shadowheart")

## Overview

[![](/w/images/thumb/b/b6/Tadpole_Pool.png/200px-Tadpole_Pool.png.webp)](/wiki/Mind_Flayer_Parasite_Specimen "Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen")

Upon first crossing the bridge leading to the [Goblin Camp](/wiki/Goblin_Camp "Goblin Camp") in [Act One](/wiki/Act_One "Act One"), a cutscene involving the [![](/w/images/thumb/1/1d/Mysterious_Artefact_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Mysterious_Artefact_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Mysterious_Artefact "Mysterious Artefact") [Mysterious Artefact](/wiki/Mysterious_Artefact "Mysterious Artefact") triggers. During the next [Long Rest](/wiki/Long_Rest "Long Rest"), the player character and their companions are visited by the [![Dream Guardian](/w/images/thumb/9/96/Portrait_Dream_Guardian_1.png/25px-Portrait_Dream_Guardian_1.png.webp)](/wiki/Dream_Guardian "Dream Guardian") [Dream Guardian](/wiki/Dream_Guardian "Dream Guardian") who encourages them to accept and wield the powers the tadpoles give. This dream starts the [![Embrace Your Potential](/w/images/thumb/6/69/Quest_Map_Icon.png/22px-Quest_Map_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Embrace_Your_Potential "Embrace Your Potential")⁠[Embrace Your Potential](/wiki/Embrace_Your_Potential "Embrace Your Potential") quest, enabling the party to consume tadpoles in exchange for illithid powers.

Once started, consuming a [![](/w/images/thumb/9/91/Mind_Flayer_Parasite_Specimen_Item_Icon.png/20px-Mind_Flayer_Parasite_Specimen_Item_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Mind_Flayer_Parasite_Specimen "Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen") [parasite](/wiki/Mind_Flayer_Parasite_Specimen "Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen") or recruiting Minthara unlocks the Illithid Powers skill tree. Once unlocked, all Mind Flayer Parasite Specimens in the inventories of the player character and their companions are removed and added to the skill tree interface, where they can be used to unlock new Illithid Powers.

### Elite powers

See also: [Partial-illithid](/wiki/Partial-illithid "Partial-illithid")

Upon first gaining access to illithid powers, the outer ring of abilities are locked. At the very end of [Act Two](/wiki/Act_Two "Act Two"), on the road to [Baldur's Gate](/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate "Baldur's Gate"), the party is given the opportunity to unlock these powers by agreeing to absorb an [![](/w/images/thumb/c/cc/Astral_Tadpole_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Astral_Tadpole_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Astral-Touched_Tadpole "Astral-Touched Tadpole") [Astral-Touched Tadpole](/wiki/Astral-Touched_Tadpole "Astral-Touched Tadpole") and become a [partial-illithid](/wiki/Partial-illithid "Partial-illithid"). Doing this automatically unlocks [Fly (Illithid Power)](/wiki/Fly_(Illithid_Power) "Fly (Illithid Power)") and the 5 powers of the innermost ring:

1. [![Concentrated Blast](/w/images/thumb/8/83/Concentrated_Blast_Icon.webp/25px-Concentrated_Blast_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Concentrated_Blast "Concentrated Blast") [Concentrated Blast](/wiki/Concentrated_Blast "Concentrated Blast")
2. [![Favourable Beginnings](/w/images/thumb/3/3e/Favourable_Beginnings_Icon.webp/25px-Favourable_Beginnings_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Favourable_Beginnings "Favourable Beginnings") [Favourable Beginnings](/wiki/Favourable_Beginnings "Favourable Beginnings")
3. [![Force Tunnel](/w/images/thumb/1/19/Force_Tunnel_Icon.webp/25px-Force_Tunnel_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Force_Tunnel "Force Tunnel") [Force Tunnel](/wiki/Force_Tunnel "Force Tunnel")
4. [![Psionic Overload](/w/images/thumb/a/af/Psionic_Overload_Icon.webp/25px-Psionic_Overload_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Psionic_Overload "Psionic Overload") [Psionic Overload](/wiki/Psionic_Overload "Psionic Overload"), and
5. [![Transfuse Health](/w/images/thumb/5/5e/Transfuse_Health_Icon.webp/25px-Transfuse_Health_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Transfuse_Health "Transfuse Health") [Transfuse Health](/wiki/Transfuse_Health "Transfuse Health").

Any regular [Tadpoles](/wiki/Tadpoles "Tadpoles") the character might have already spent to unlock these abilities will be refunded. Absorbing the Astral-Touched Tadpole causes the character to undergo [![Partial Ceremorphosis](/w/images/thumb/2/21/Partial_Ceremorphosis_Icon.webp/25px-Partial_Ceremorphosis_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Partial_Ceremorphosis "Partial Ceremorphosis") [Partial Ceremorphosis](/wiki/Partial_Ceremorphosis "Partial Ceremorphosis") and irrevocably changes their appearance: their eyes darken and their entire body covers with dark veins.

## Spellcasting ability modifier

Main article: [Spellcasting ability](/wiki/Spells#Spellcasting_ability "Spells")

Some illithid powers involve [saving throws](/wiki/Saving_throws "Saving throws"). For these powers, the [difficulty class](/wiki/Difficulty_class "Difficulty class") is determined by your [spellcasting ability modifier](/wiki/Spells#Spellcasting_ability "Spells"), even for classes that do not typically have spells. All bonuses to spell save DC such as [Arcane Acuity](/wiki/Arcane_Acuity_(Condition) "Arcane Acuity (Condition)") or [Arcane Enchantment](/wiki/Arcane_Enchantment_(%2B1) "Arcane Enchantment (+1)") will also apply to illithid powers.

If multiclassing, the ability score used by this modifier is determined by the latest class added. In other words, taking level 1 in a new class will switch the spellcasting ability modifier used by illthid powers (and items or scrolls) to the modifier of the new class.
