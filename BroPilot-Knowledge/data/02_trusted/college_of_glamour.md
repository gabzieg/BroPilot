# College Of Glamour

**College of Glamour** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Bard#Level_3 "Bard") of [Bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard"). It can inspire multiple allies simultaneously with temporary hit points and influence enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 3

[![Mantle of Inspiration](/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Mantle_of_Inspiration_Icon.webp/40px-Mantle_of_Inspiration_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Mantle_of_Inspiration "Mantle of Inspiration") [Mantle of Inspiration](/wiki/Mantle_of_Inspiration "Mantle of Inspiration") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Bardic Inspiration](/w/images/thumb/b/be/Bardic_Inspiration_Resource_Icon.png/20px-Bardic_Inspiration_Resource_Icon.png.webp))
:   Imbue 2 allies with your vigour and grant them ![5 temporary hit points](/w/images/thumb/2/25/HP_Icon.png/25px-HP_Icon.png.webp) 5 [temporary hit points](/wiki/Temporary_Hit_Points "Temporary Hit Points"). If they're hit with a melee attack, the attacker becomes [![Charmed](/w/images/thumb/4/47/Charmed_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Charmed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Charmed_(Condition) "Charmed (Condition)") [Charmed](/wiki/Charmed_(Condition) "Charmed (Condition)").

### Level 6

[![Mantle of Majesty: Command](/w/images/thumb/b/bf/Mantle_of_Majesty_Icon.webp/40px-Mantle_of_Majesty_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Mantle_of_Majesty:_Command "Mantle of Majesty: Command") [Mantle of Majesty: Command](/wiki/Mantle_of_Majesty:_Command "Mantle of Majesty: Command") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp))
:   Command a creature to flee, move closer, freeze, drop to the ground or drop its weapon.
    Targets that are [![Charmed](/w/images/thumb/4/47/Charmed_Condition_Icon.webp/25px-Charmed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Charmed_(Condition) "Charmed (Condition)") [Charmed](/wiki/Charmed_(Condition) "Charmed (Condition)") automatically fail their Saving Throw. (![Recharge: Long rest](/w/images/thumb/f/fd/Recharge_Icons.png/25px-Recharge_Icons.png.webp) Recharge: [Long rest](/wiki/Long_rest "Long rest").)

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

Mantle of Inspiration
:   Bonus increases to ![8 temporary hit points](/w/images/thumb/2/25/HP_Icon.png/25px-HP_Icon.png.webp) 8 [temporary hit points](/wiki/Temporary_Hit_Points "Temporary Hit Points").
:   Number of targets increases to 3.

### Level 10

Mantle of Inspiration
:   Bonus increases to ![11 temporary hit points](/w/images/thumb/2/25/HP_Icon.png/25px-HP_Icon.png.webp) 11 [temporary hit points](/wiki/Temporary_Hit_Points "Temporary Hit Points").
