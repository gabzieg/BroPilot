**Weapons** are a type of [equipment](/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") used in [attacks](/wiki/Attack_rolls "Attack rolls") that deal damage. Weapons also give [proficient](/wiki/Proficiency "Proficiency") wielders access to special [weapon actions](/wiki/Weapon_Actions "Weapon Actions"), and are an essential tool for any adventurer.

## Overview

Each weapon is either [*melee*](/wiki/List_of_melee_weapons "List of melee weapons") or [*ranged*](/wiki/List_of_ranged_weapons "List of ranged weapons"), and deals a specific type of physical damage: [![Bludgeoning](/w/images/thumb/7/7d/Bludgeoning_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Bludgeoning_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Bludgeoning "Bludgeoning")⁠[Bludgeoning](/wiki/Bludgeoning "Bludgeoning"), [![Piercing](/w/images/thumb/0/0b/Piercing_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Piercing_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Piercing "Piercing")⁠[Piercing](/wiki/Piercing "Piercing"), or [![Slashing](/w/images/thumb/f/f0/Slashing_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Slashing_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Slashing "Slashing")⁠[Slashing](/wiki/Slashing "Slashing").

Weapons are further categorised as either *martial* or *simple*. Martial weapons deal more damage than simple weapons, but require more specialised training to properly use.

Additionally, each weapon has a set of *properties* which determine how the weapon may be used.

Finally, for multiclassed characters, additional proficiencies are not retroactively applied to include lower tier weapons. For example, if a character starts as a Wizard at level 1, they gain proficiency with Daggers, Quarterstaves, and Light Crossbows. If at level 2 a Death Domain Cleric is chosen, Martial weapon proficiency is gained, but this character will still not be proficient with all simple weapons, such as clubs and handaxes.

### Weapon slots

Each character has a *main hand* slot and an *off-hand* slot, both of which can equip weapons.

Weapons are classified as either *one-handed*, *two-handed* or *versatile*. One-handed weapons only take up one weapon slot, whereas two-handed take up both. Versatile weapons can be wielded as either one-handed or two-handed weapons, the latter of which deals greater damage.

One-handed weapons allow the wielder to equip a [shield](/wiki/Shields "Shields") in their off-hand, and can also be *dual wielded* if both weapons have the [light](/wiki/Light_(weapon_property) "Light (weapon property)") property.

### Proficiency

Classes give proficiency with weapons or category of weapons, allowing the wielder to add their proficiency bonus to their attack rolls.

Proficiency in the equipped main hand weapon unlocks its weapon actions. Off-hand weapons do not grant weapon actions.

See also: [List of simple weapons](/wiki/List_of_simple_weapons "List of simple weapons")

## Improvised weapons

Objects that are not actually weapons can nevertheless be used as [Improvised Weapons](/wiki/Improvised_Melee_Weapon "Improvised Melee Weapon"). Note that this is not the same as using a non-weapon item that resembles a weapon strongly enough to allow it to be used as such. For example, when [Salami](/wiki/Salami "Salami") is used as a weapon, it actually functions as a [Club](/wiki/Clubs "Clubs"), not as an Improvised Weapon.
