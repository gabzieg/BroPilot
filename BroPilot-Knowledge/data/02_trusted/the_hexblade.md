# The Hexblade

**Hexblade** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Warlock#Level_1 "Warlock") of [Warlock](/wiki/Warlock "Warlock"). They can curse enemies with their hexed weapons and raise the souls of defeated enemies as spectres. Released as part of Patch 8 on April 15, 2025.

![](/w/images/thumb/a/a5/Class_Warlock_Hexblade_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Warlock_Hexblade_Badge_Icon.png.webp)

>
> You have made your pact with a mysterious entity from the Shadowfell - a force that manifests in sentient magic weapons carved from the stuff of shadow.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Warlock](/wiki/Warlock "Warlock"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 1

[![Hex Warrior](/w/images/thumb/4/45/Hex_Warrior_Icon.webp/40px-Hex_Warrior_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Hex_Warrior "Hex Warrior")[Hex Warrior](/wiki/Hex_Warrior "Hex Warrior")
:   You gain proficiency with [![Medium armour](/w/images/thumb/7/73/Medium_Armour_Icon.png/25px-Medium_Armour_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Armour#List_of_medium_armour "Armour") [Medium armour](/wiki/Armour#List_of_medium_armour "Armour"), [![Shields](/w/images/thumb/a/af/Shields_Icon.png/25px-Shields_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Shields "Shields") [Shields](/wiki/Shields "Shields"), and ![Martial Weapon Prof Icon.png](/w/images/thumb/1/17/Martial_Weapon_Prof_Icon.png/25px-Martial_Weapon_Prof_Icon.png.webp) [Martial weapons](/wiki/Martial_weapons "Martial weapons").
    You gain [![Bind Hexed Weapon](/w/images/thumb/3/35/Bind_Pact_Weapon_Icon.webp/25px-Bind_Pact_Weapon_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Bind_Hexed_Weapon "Bind Hexed Weapon") [Bind Hexed Weapon](/wiki/Bind_Hexed_Weapon "Bind Hexed Weapon"), which lets you use your [Charisma](/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity when attacking with weapons that you are proficient with.

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

[![Bind Hexed Weapon](/w/images/thumb/3/35/Bind_Pact_Weapon_Icon.webp/40px-Bind_Pact_Weapon_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Bind_Hexed_Weapon "Bind Hexed Weapon") [Bind Hexed Weapon](/wiki/Bind_Hexed_Weapon "Bind Hexed Weapon") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp))
:   Bind to your main hand weapon. Its damage becomes magical, you cannot drop or throw it, and you become [Proficient](/wiki/Proficient "Proficient") with it if you weren't already. When you attack with this weapon, there is a chance*[[See Notes](/wiki/Bind_Hexed_Weapon#Notes "Bind Hexed Weapon")]* that [![Hexblade's Curse](/w/images/thumb/4/47/Generic_Ethereal_Condition_Icon.webp/25px-Generic_Ethereal_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Hexblade%27s_Curse_(Condition) "Hexblade's Curse (Condition)") [Hexblade's Curse](/wiki/Hexblade%27s_Curse_(Condition) "Hexblade's Curse (Condition)") will be applied to the target.

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

[![Hexblade's Curse](/w/images/thumb/3/3f/Hexblade%27s_Curse_Icon.webp/40px-Hexblade%27s_Curse_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Hexblade%27s_Curse "Hexblade's Curse") [Hexblade's Curse](/wiki/Hexblade%27s_Curse "Hexblade's Curse") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp))
:   Curse a target. Your damage rolls against the target gain a bonus equal to your [Proficiency bonus](/wiki/Proficiency_bonus "Proficiency bonus"). The number you need to roll a [Critical Hit](/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") while attacking the target is also reduced by 1.
    If the target dies, you regain ![HP Icon.png](/w/images/thumb/2/25/HP_Icon.png/25px-HP_Icon.png.webp) Warlock level + Charisma modifier [hit points](/wiki/Hit_points "Hit points"). (![Recharge: Short rest](/w/images/thumb/f/fd/Recharge_Icons.png/25px-Recharge_Icons.png.webp) Recharge: [Short rest](/wiki/Short_rest "Short rest").)
