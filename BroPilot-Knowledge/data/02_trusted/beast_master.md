# Beast Master

**Beast Master** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Ranger#Level_3 "Ranger") of [Ranger](/wiki/Ranger "Ranger"). These Rangers are able to summon an animal companion to aid them in combat and exploration. The companions also gain new features and abilities as the Ranger level increases.

![](/w/images/thumb/2/26/Class_Ranger_Beast_Master_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Ranger_Beast_Master_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> You foster an intelligent bond with a powerful beast that can aid you in and out of combat.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Ranger](/wiki/Ranger "Ranger"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*
