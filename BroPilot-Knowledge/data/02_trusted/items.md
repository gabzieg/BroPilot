# Items

There are different types of items in the game. Items can be of different rarities which are: [Common](/wiki/Category:Common_Items "Category:Common Items"), [Uncommon](/wiki/Category:Uncommon_Items "Category:Uncommon Items"), [Rare](/wiki/Category:Rare_Items "Category:Rare Items"), [Very rare](/wiki/Category:Very_Rare_Items "Category:Very Rare Items"), [Legendary](/wiki/Category:Legendary_Items "Category:Legendary Items"), and [Story Item](/wiki/Category:Story_Items "Category:Story Items").
