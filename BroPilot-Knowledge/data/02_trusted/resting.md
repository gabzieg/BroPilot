# Resting

**Resting** is a gameplay mechanic used to restore lost [hit points](/wiki/Hit_points "Hit points") and depleted [resources](/wiki/Resources "Resources").

## Overview

Rests can be either long or short. **Long rests** restore all lost hit points and spent resources, whereas **short rests** restore a limited amount of hit points, and a smaller variety of resources.

## Long rest

[![](/w/images/thumb/0/06/Long_Rest.webp/300px-Long_Rest.webp)](/wiki/File:Long_Rest.webp)

Starting a long rest at camp

Long rests are primarily taken [at camp](/wiki/Campsite "Campsite"). Taking a long rest passes the night, and costs 40 (80 in tactician or honour difficulty, up to 120 in custom difficulty) [camp supplies](/wiki/Camp_supplies "Camp supplies").

### Full rest

If possessing the required [supplies](/wiki/Camp_supplies "Camp supplies"), the party can take a long rest, which restores the following:

- [Hit points](/wiki/Hit_points "Hit points") - all
- [Spell slots](/wiki/Spells#Spell_Slots "Spells") - all
- [Resources](/wiki/Resources "Resources") - all
- Use of certain item actions
- [Illithid power](/wiki/Illithid_power "Illithid power") dialogue options
- Both Short Rest slots

### Partial rest

If lacking the required supplies, the party can instead take a *partial rest*, which restores the following:

- Hit points - up to half of their maximum, rounded down
- Resources - up to half of the character's maximum, rounded down
  - Including class-specific resources such as spell slots, ![](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp) [Ki Points](/wiki/Ki_Point "Ki Point"), and ![](/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Superiority_Die_d8_Icon.png/20px-Superiority_Die_d8_Icon.png.webp) [Superiority Dice](/wiki/Superiority_Die "Superiority Die")
- Refreshing of cooldowns, including items and class features like [Song of Rest](/wiki/Song_of_Rest "Song of Rest"), [Portent](/wiki/Portent "Portent") and most [illithid powers](/wiki/Illithid_powers "Illithid powers")
  - [Luck of the Far Realms](/wiki/Luck_of_the_Far_Realms "Luck of the Far Realms") is not restored as it costs a hidden resource which is a maximum of 1, and is rounded from one-half down to zero (0).

Short rests are *not* restored when performing a partial long rest.

### Consequences of long resting

Many [origin](/wiki/Origin "Origin"), [companion](/wiki/Companions "Companions") and [camp follower](/wiki/Camp_followers "Camp followers") interactions occur on long resting. Long rests can also advance certain [time-sensitive content](/wiki/Time-sensitive_activities "Time-sensitive activities"); additionally each act has a handful of [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") which advance upon long resting.

Any [Temporary bonuses](/wiki/Temporary_bonuses "Temporary bonuses") with "Duration: Until Long rest" end.

Any effects of [Elixirs](/wiki/Elixirs "Elixirs") end.

## Short rest

Twice per long rest, the party can take a short rest without travelling to the camp. Characters who are not in the active party do not benefit from short rests and do not regain hit points or resources. Short rests restore the following:

- Hit points - up to half of their maximum, rounded down
- Resources:
  - [Bards](/wiki/Bard "Bard") of level 5 or higher regain their ![](/w/images/thumb/b/be/Bardic_Inspiration_Resource_Icon.png/20px-Bardic_Inspiration_Resource_Icon.png.webp) [Bardic Inspiration](/wiki/Bardic_Inspiration_(resource) "Bardic Inspiration (resource)") charges
  - [Clerics](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric") regain their ![](/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Channel_Divinity_Charges_Icon.png/20px-Channel_Divinity_Charges_Icon.png.webp) [Channel Divinity Charges](/wiki/Channel_Divinity_Charge "Channel Divinity Charge")
  - [Druids](/wiki/Druid "Druid") regain their ![](/w/images/thumb/b/b6/Wild_Shape_Charges_Icon.png/20px-Wild_Shape_Charges_Icon.png.webp) [Wild Shape Charges](/wiki/Wild_Shape_Charge "Wild Shape Charge")
  - [Fighters](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter") regain the use of their [![Action Surge](/w/images/thumb/4/4d/Action_Surge_Icon.webp/20px-Action_Surge_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Action_Surge "Action Surge") [Action Surge](/wiki/Action_Surge "Action Surge") and [![Second Wind](/w/images/thumb/9/95/Second_Wind_Icon.webp/20px-Second_Wind_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") [Second Wind](/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") abilities, as well as their ![](/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Superiority_Die_d8_Icon.png/20px-Superiority_Die_d8_Icon.png.webp) [Superiority Dice](/wiki/Superiority_Die "Superiority Die") and ![](/w/images/thumb/5/51/Arcane_Arrow_Resource_Icon.png/20px-Arcane_Arrow_Resource_Icon.png.webp) [Arcane Arrows](/wiki/Arcane_Arrow "Arcane Arrow").
  - [Monks](/wiki/Monk "Monk") regain their ![](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp) [Ki Points](/wiki/Ki_Point "Ki Point")
  - [Paladins](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin") regain their ![](/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Channel_Oath_Icon.png/20px-Channel_Oath_Icon.png.webp) [Channel Oath Charges](/wiki/Channel_Oath_Charge "Channel Oath Charge")
  - [Warlocks](/wiki/Warlock "Warlock") regain their ![](/w/images/thumb/6/6c/Warlock_Spell_Slot_Icon.webp/20px-Warlock_Spell_Slot_Icon.webp) [Warlock Spell Slots](/wiki/Spells#Pact_Magic "Spells")
- Uses of [weapon actions](/wiki/Weapon_actions "Weapon actions")
- [Illithid Powers](/wiki/Illithid_Powers "Illithid Powers"):
  - [Psionic Overload](/wiki/Psionic_Overload "Psionic Overload")
  - [Force Tunnel](/wiki/Force_Tunnel "Force Tunnel")
  - [Stage Fright](/wiki/Stage_Fright "Stage Fright")
  - [Repulsor](/wiki/Repulsor "Repulsor")
  - [Shield of Thralls](/wiki/Shield_of_Thralls "Shield of Thralls")
  - [Black Hole](/wiki/Black_Hole "Black Hole")
  - [Absorb Intellect](/wiki/Absorb_Intellect "Absorb Intellect")
  - [Fracture Psyche](/wiki/Fracture_Psyche "Fracture Psyche")

## Additional Types

There are additional features in-game which function similarly to taking a rest;

- The level 2 [Bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard") feature [![Song of Rest](/w/images/thumb/b/b1/Song_of_Rest_Icon.webp/25px-Song_of_Rest_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Song_of_Rest "Song of Rest") [Song of Rest](/wiki/Song_of_Rest "Song of Rest") functions identically to a short rest.
- [![](/w/images/thumb/5/5a/POT_Potion_of_Angelic_Reprieve_Unfaded_Icon.png/25px-POT_Potion_of_Angelic_Reprieve_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Potion_of_Angelic_Reprieve "Potion of Angelic Reprieve") [Potion of Angelic Reprieve](/wiki/Potion_of_Angelic_Reprieve "Potion of Angelic Reprieve") functions as a short rest (in addition to recovering all expended ![1 Level 1 Spell Slots.png](/w/images/thumb/0/04/1_Level_1_Spell_Slots.png/22px-1_Level_1_Spell_Slots.png.webp) and ![1 Level 2 Spell Slots.png](/w/images/thumb/a/aa/1_Level_2_Spell_Slots.png/22px-1_Level_2_Spell_Slots.png.webp) [Spell Slots](/wiki/Spell_Slot "Spell Slot")), if the character consuming it is left asleep and uninterrupted, for 2 turns.
- [![](/w/images/thumb/6/62/POT_Potion_of_Angelic_Slumber_Unfaded_Icon.png/25px-POT_Potion_of_Angelic_Slumber_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Potion_of_Angelic_Slumber "Potion of Angelic Slumber") [Potion of Angelic Slumber](/wiki/Potion_of_Angelic_Slumber "Potion of Angelic Slumber") functions as a long rest, if the character consuming it is left asleep and uninterrupted, for 2 turns.
- [![Divine Intervention: Opulent Revival](/w/images/thumb/f/f9/Divine_Intervention_Opulent_Revival_Icon.webp/25px-Divine_Intervention_Opulent_Revival_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Divine_Intervention:_Opulent_Revival "Divine Intervention: Opulent Revival") [Divine Intervention: Opulent Revival](/wiki/Divine_Intervention:_Opulent_Revival "Divine Intervention: Opulent Revival") restores the party as if they took a long rest, revives any downed party members, and can even be used in combat. However, any form of Divine Intervention can only be used once per character in the entire game.
- Using a [Restoration](/wiki/Restoration "Restoration") Pod found aboard the [Nautiloid](/wiki/Nautiloid "Nautiloid") in the prologue or the [Mind Flayer Colony](/wiki/Mind_Flayer_Colony "Mind Flayer Colony") in [Act Two](/wiki/Act_Two "Act Two"), the boudoir in the [House of Hope](/wiki/House_of_Hope "House of Hope") in [Act Three](/wiki/Act_Three "Act Three"), or in [High Hall](/wiki/High_Hall "High Hall"), also in Act Three, restores the party as if they took a long rest, *without* ending relevant effects like [![Goodberry](/w/images/thumb/4/42/Goodberry_Icon.webp/25px-Goodberry_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Goodberry "Goodberry") [Goodberry](/wiki/Goodberry "Goodberry") or [Elixirs](/wiki/Elixirs "Elixirs").
