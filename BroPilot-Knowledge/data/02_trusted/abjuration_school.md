# Abjuration School

This page is about the Wizard subclass. For a list of all spells of this school, see [Abjuration](/wiki/Abjuration "Abjuration").

**Abjuration School** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Wizard#Level_2 "Wizard") of [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"). These Wizards are specialized in magic that can protect themselves and allies from harm. They also have the ability to reduce the amount of [damage](/wiki/Damage "Damage") dealt to them on subsequent turns whenever they cast an Abjuration spell.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 2

Abjuration Savant
:   Halves the cost to learn Abjuration spells from [Scrolls](/wiki/Scrolls "Scrolls"). Learning these spells will only cost **![25 gp](/w/images/thumb/f/f5/Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png/23px-Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png.webp) 25 gp** per spell level.

[![Arcane Ward](/w/images/thumb/7/79/Arcane_Ward_Icon.webp/40px-Arcane_Ward_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Arcane_Ward "Arcane Ward")[Arcane Ward](/wiki/Arcane_Ward "Arcane Ward")
:   The residual magic of your spells forms a ward around you that protects you from harm.
:   Each time you cast an Abjuration spell, the intensity of the ward increases by the amount of the spell's Level, up to a maximum of 2x your Wizard level.
      
    Each time you take damage, the ward blocks an amount of damage equal to its intensity, and its intensity decreases by 1.
      
    After each [Long Rest](/wiki/Long_Rest "Long Rest"), the ward's intensity resets, and becomes the same as your Wizard level.

### Level 6

[![Projected Ward](/w/images/thumb/7/79/Projected_Ward_Icon.webp/40px-Projected_Ward_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Projected_Ward "Projected Ward") [Projected Ward](/wiki/Projected_Ward "Projected Ward") (![Reaction](/w/images/thumb/c/c1/Reaction_Icon.png/23px-Reaction_Icon.png.webp))
:   When a nearby ally takes damage and you have an active [![Arcane Ward](/w/images/thumb/7/79/Arcane_Ward_Icon.webp/25px-Arcane_Ward_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Arcane_Ward "Arcane Ward") [Arcane Ward](/wiki/Arcane_Ward "Arcane Ward"), you can sacrifice your ward to reduce the damage they take equal to its charges and then lose 1 charge.

### Level 10

[![Improved Abjuration](/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Improved_Abjuration_Icon.webp/40px-Improved_Abjuration_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Improved_Abjuration "Improved Abjuration")[Improved Abjuration](/wiki/Improved_Abjuration "Improved Abjuration")
:   Each time you take a [Short Rest](/wiki/Short_Rest "Short Rest"), the intensity of your [![Arcane Ward](/w/images/thumb/7/79/Arcane_Ward_Icon.webp/25px-Arcane_Ward_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Arcane_Ward "Arcane Ward") [Arcane Ward](/wiki/Arcane_Ward "Arcane Ward") increases by an amount equal to your wizard level.
