# Light Domain

**Light Domain** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Cleric#Subclasses "Cleric") of [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"). These Clerics are charged with chasing away lies and burning away darkness.

Player selectable [Deities](/wiki/Deities "Deities") associated with this domain: [![Corellon Larethian](/w/images/thumb/b/b3/Corellon_Larethian_Icon.webp/20px-Corellon_Larethian_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Corellon_Larethian "Corellon Larethian") [Corellon Larethian](/wiki/Corellon_Larethian "Corellon Larethian"), [![Eilistraee](/w/images/thumb/e/e2/Eilistraee_Icon.webp/14px-Eilistraee_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Eilistraee "Eilistraee") [Eilistraee](/wiki/Eilistraee "Eilistraee"), [![Helm](/w/images/thumb/b/b5/Helm_Icon.webp/11px-Helm_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Helm_(deity) "Helm (deity)") [Helm](/wiki/Helm_(deity) "Helm (deity)"), and [![Lathander](/w/images/thumb/9/95/Lathander_Icon.webp/20px-Lathander_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Lathander "Lathander") [Lathander](/wiki/Lathander "Lathander").

### Level 2

[![Radiance of the Dawn](/w/images/thumb/c/c8/Radiance_of_the_Dawn_Icon.webp/40px-Radiance_of_the_Dawn_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Radiance_of_the_Dawn "Radiance of the Dawn") [Radiance of the Dawn](/wiki/Radiance_of_the_Dawn "Radiance of the Dawn") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Channel Divinity Charge](/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Channel_Divinity_Charges_Icon.png/20px-Channel_Divinity_Charges_Icon.png.webp))

This ability allows the Cleric to channel divine power and deal 2d10 + character level Radiant damage to enemies.
