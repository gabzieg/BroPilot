# Tempest Domain

**Tempest Domain** is one of the [cleric subclasses](/wiki/Cleric#Subclass "Cleric"), with a focus on [Thunder](/wiki/Damage_Types#Thunder "Damage Types") and [Lightning](/wiki/Damage_Types#Lightning "Damage Types") damage, weather themed spells, and martial combat.

Player selectable [Deities](/wiki/Deities "Deities") associated with this domain: [![Gruumsh](/w/images/thumb/c/c3/Gruumsh_Icon.webp/19px-Gruumsh_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Gruumsh "Gruumsh") [Gruumsh](/wiki/Gruumsh "Gruumsh") and [![Talos](/w/images/thumb/4/4d/Talos_Icon.webp/20px-Talos_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Talos "Talos") [Talos](/wiki/Talos "Talos").

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

- Gain [![Heavy armour](/w/images/thumb/b/b7/Heavy_Armour_Icon.png/25px-Heavy_Armour_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Armour#List_of_heavy_armour "Armour") [Heavy armour](/wiki/Armour#List_of_heavy_armour "Armour") Proficiency
- Gain ![Martial Weapon Prof Icon.png](/w/images/thumb/1/17/Martial_Weapon_Prof_Icon.png/25px-Martial_Weapon_Prof_Icon.png.webp) [Martial weapons](/wiki/Martial_weapons "Martial weapons") Proficiency

The target takes half damage on a successful ![Saving Throw](/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Saving_Throw_Icon.png/25px-Saving_Throw_Icon.png.webp) [Saving Throw](/wiki/Saving_Throw "Saving Throw").
:   [![Wrath of the Storm: Lightning](/w/images/thumb/6/61/Wrath_of_the_Storm_Lightning_Icon.webp/30px-Wrath_of_the_Storm_Lightning_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Wrath_of_the_Storm:_Lightning "Wrath of the Storm: Lightning") [Wrath of the Storm: Lightning](/wiki/Wrath_of_the_Storm:_Lightning "Wrath of the Storm: Lightning") (![Reaction](/w/images/thumb/c/c1/Reaction_Icon.png/23px-Reaction_Icon.png.webp))
    :   If a nearby enemy attacks you, you can retaliate with a strike of lightning that deals 2d8⁠[![Lightning](/w/images/thumb/3/31/Lightning_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Lightning_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Lightning "Lightning")⁠[Lightning](/wiki/Lightning "Lightning") damage.

    [![Wrath of the Storm: Thunder](/w/images/thumb/b/b6/Wrath_of_the_Storm_Thunder_Icon.webp/30px-Wrath_of_the_Storm_Thunder_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Wrath_of_the_Storm:_Thunder "Wrath of the Storm: Thunder") [Wrath of the Storm: Thunder](/wiki/Wrath_of_the_Storm:_Thunder "Wrath of the Storm: Thunder") (![Reaction](/w/images/thumb/c/c1/Reaction_Icon.png/23px-Reaction_Icon.png.webp))
    :   If a nearby enemy attacks you, you can retaliate with a burst of thunder that deals 2d8⁠[![Thunder](/w/images/thumb/8/87/Thunder_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Thunder_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Thunder "Thunder")⁠[Thunder](/wiki/Thunder "Thunder") damage.
