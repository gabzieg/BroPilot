# Weapon Actions

**Weapon actions** are [actions](/wiki/Action "Action") granted to a character based on the weapon type they are wielding. Usually, these actions are special attacks that are superior in some way to normal attacks. Each weapon type has its own set of weapon actions which helps distinguish weapon categories. For example, [Longswords](/wiki/Longswords "Longswords") grant the [![Lacerate](/w/images/thumb/a/af/Lacerate_Icon.webp/20px-Lacerate_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Lacerate "Lacerate") [Lacerate](/wiki/Lacerate "Lacerate"), [![Rush Attack](/w/images/thumb/1/1a/Rush_Attack_Icon.webp/20px-Rush_Attack_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Rush_Attack "Rush Attack") [Rush Attack](/wiki/Rush_Attack "Rush Attack"), and [![Pommel Strike](/w/images/thumb/1/1e/Pommel_Strike_Icon.webp/20px-Pommel_Strike_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Pommel_Strike "Pommel Strike") [Pommel Strike](/wiki/Pommel_Strike "Pommel Strike") actions. Additionally, many special weapons have unique weapon actions on top of the ones granted by the weapon type.

To access *weapon actions* from a weapon, the wielder must be [proficient](/wiki/Proficient "Proficient") in that weapon type and have it equipped in their main hand or both hands. Off-hand weapons do not grant weapon actions.

Unless otherwise specified, each weapon action can be used once per [short rest](/wiki/Short_rest "Short rest"). This limitation is character-specific, not weapon-specific. Therefore, a consumed weapon action cannot be restored by equipping a new weapon, but the same weapon action from a given weapon type can be used by different characters without taking a short rest. A weapon action is consumed even if the attack misses.

## Weapon action DC

Many weapon actions are attacks that can inflict debilitating conditions on the target. For these attacks, the target can make a [saving throw](/wiki/Saving_throw "Saving throw") to avoid suffering the condition. The [Difficulty Class](/wiki/Difficulty_Class "Difficulty Class") (or DC) for saving throws is calculated similarly to [spell save DC](/wiki/Spells#Spell_saves "Spells") except for two differences: First, the [spellcasting ability modifier](/wiki/Spells#Spellcasting_ability "Spells") is replaced with the [ability score modifier](/wiki/Abilities#Ability_score_modifiers "Abilities") of either Strength or Dexterity, whichever is higher. Second, each weapon action can grant its own inherent bonus DC that is not listed anywhere, but most frequently is +2. To express all this mathematically:

Weapon action DC = 8 + [proficiency bonus](/wiki/Proficiency_bonus "Proficiency bonus") + [Strength](/wiki/Strength "Strength") or [Dexterity](/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier + inherent weapon action bonus DC

Certain weapon action instead use a **Hybrid DC** which allows a user to either use their spell save DC or weapon action DC with a +2 bonus, whichever is higher. Other unique weapon actions always use the spell save DC. In all cases, the type of DC and any inherent bonus is listed in the tables below.

Prior to Patch 5, weapon actions instead had a base DC of 10 but did not add the wielder's proficiency bonus to the DC, leading to an overall lower DC after proficiency bonus increases at level 5. This was changed to the above formula after patch 5.
