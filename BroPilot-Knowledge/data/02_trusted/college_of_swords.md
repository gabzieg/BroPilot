# College Of Swords

**College of Swords** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Bard#Level_3 "Bard") of [Bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard"). These Bards focus entirely on improving their own combat prowess by combining the [Extra Attack](/wiki/Extra_Attack "Extra Attack") passive, being able to use [Bardic Inspiration](/wiki/Bardic_Inspiration_(resource) "Bardic Inspiration (resource)") charges for utility and multi-attacks, and being the only Bard Subclass able to have a [Fighting Style](/wiki/Fighting_Style "Fighting Style").
