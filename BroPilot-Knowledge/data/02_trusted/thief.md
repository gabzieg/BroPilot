# Thief

**Thief** is a [subclass](/wiki/Rogue#Level_3 "Rogue") of [Rogue](/wiki/Rogue "Rogue"). Most notably, its [![Fast Hands](/w/images/thumb/d/dc/Fast_Hands_Icon.webp/20px-Fast_Hands_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Fast_Hands "Fast Hands") [Fast Hands](/wiki/Fast_Hands "Fast Hands") passive grants a second ![](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp) [Bonus Action](/wiki/Actions#Resources "Actions"). Together with the cunning actions the rogue gains at level 2, this subclass allows for exceptional mobility and enhanced versatility.

The extra bonus action from Fast Hands allows thieves to use [![Cunning Action: Dash](/w/images/thumb/8/8e/Dash_bonus_action_Icon.webp/20px-Dash_bonus_action_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Cunning_Action:_Dash "Cunning Action: Dash") [Cunning Action: Dash](/wiki/Cunning_Action:_Dash "Cunning Action: Dash") twice to triple their [Movement Speed](/wiki/Movement_Speed "Movement Speed") without sacrificing an action. Cunning Action: Dash combined with [![Cunning Action: Disengage](/w/images/thumb/4/4b/Disengage_Bonus_Action_Icon.webp/20px-Disengage_Bonus_Action_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Cunning_Action:_Disengage "Cunning Action: Disengage") [Cunning Action: Disengage](/wiki/Cunning_Action:_Disengage "Cunning Action: Disengage") allows for moving between enemies without triggering [![Opportunity Attacks](/w/images/thumb/3/35/Opportunity_Attack_Icon.webp/20px-Opportunity_Attack_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Opportunity_Attack "Opportunity Attack") [Opportunity Attacks](/wiki/Opportunity_Attack "Opportunity Attack"). This extra bonus action can also be paired with the same from [items](/wiki/Sorrowful_Lash "Sorrowful Lash"), [weapon actions](/wiki/Off-Hand_Attack_(Melee) "Off-Hand Attack (Melee)"), and [other classes](/wiki/War_Magic "War Magic").
