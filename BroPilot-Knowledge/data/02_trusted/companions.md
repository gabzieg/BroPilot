# Companions

See also: [Camp followers](/wiki/Camp_followers "Camp followers").

**Companions** are playable characters who can join and fight alongside the player character. Each has their own motivations, personality, and [approval](/wiki/Approval "Approval"), which increases or decreases according to choices made throughout the game. At high approval levels, many companions can be [romanced](/wiki/Romance "Romance"). Conversely, a companion may leave the party or even try to kill the player character if their approval becomes too low.

The default [class](/wiki/Class "Class") of companions can be changed. However, for story purposes, the game acts as if they still have their original class.

## Origin companions

[Origin](/wiki/Origin "Origin") companions are playable characters with rich backgrounds and ties to the world and the events of the game. Origin companions can be recruited shortly after completing the [prologue](/wiki/Prologue "Prologue"). Each origin can also be selected as a player character as an alternative to creating a custom character.

## Recruitment locations

### Origin companions

- [![Astarion](/w/images/thumb/1/1b/Portrait_Astarion.png/25px-Portrait_Astarion.png.webp)](/wiki/Astarion "Astarion") [Astarion](/wiki/Astarion "Astarion") is standing on the trail west of the crashed [Nautiloid](/wiki/Nautiloid "Nautiloid") on the [Ravaged Beach](/wiki/Ravaged_Beach "Ravaged Beach").
- [![Gale](/w/images/thumb/1/11/Portrait_Gale.png/25px-Portrait_Gale.png.webp)](/wiki/Gale "Gale") [Gale](/wiki/Gale "Gale") is trapped within the [Ancient Sigil Circle](/wiki/Ancient_Sigil_Circle "Ancient Sigil Circle") east of the Nautiloid.
- [![Karlach](/w/images/thumb/b/b2/Portrait_Karlach.png/25px-Portrait_Karlach.png.webp)](/wiki/Karlach "Karlach") [Karlach](/wiki/Karlach "Karlach") is on the [Risen Road](/wiki/Risen_Road "Risen Road"), south of the [Toll House](/wiki/Risen_Road_Toll_House "Risen Road Toll House") at the end of a trail of blood leading towards the river.
- [![Lae'zel](/w/images/thumb/8/86/Portrait_Lae%27zel.png/25px-Portrait_Lae%27zel.png.webp)](/wiki/Lae%27zel "Lae'zel") [Lae'zel](/wiki/Lae%27zel "Lae'zel") is a temporary companion in the [prologue](/wiki/Prologue "Prologue"). She can be recruited at the following locations where she is:
  - Trapped in a cage by two [tieflings](/wiki/Tiefling "Tiefling"), south of the [Emerald Grove](/wiki/Emerald_Grove "Emerald Grove")
  - Confronting the Githyanki patrol as part of the [Find the Githyanki Crèche](/wiki/Find_the_Githyanki_Cr%C3%A8che "Find the Githyanki Crèche") quest
  - Dead, in the [Shadow-Cursed Lands](/wiki/Shadow-Cursed_Lands "Shadow-Cursed Lands"). Her corpse is too damaged to be spoken to, but she can be resurrected using [![Revivify](/w/images/thumb/5/59/Revivify_Icon.webp/20px-Revivify_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Revivify "Revivify") [Revivify](/wiki/Revivify "Revivify") or its scroll version.
- [![Shadowheart](/w/images/thumb/7/7e/Portrait_Shadowheart.png/25px-Portrait_Shadowheart.png.webp)](/wiki/Shadowheart "Shadowheart") [Shadowheart](/wiki/Shadowheart "Shadowheart") is an optional temporary companion during the prologue. She can be recruited at the following locations where she is:
  - Unconscious on the [Ravaged Beach](/wiki/Ravaged_Beach "Ravaged Beach"), close to where the player character landed – if freed from her pod on the Nautiloid
  - Unsuccessfully trying to enter the [Dank Crypt](/wiki/Dank_Crypt "Dank Crypt") – if left in her pod on the Nautiloid
  - Inside [the Hollow](/wiki/The_Hollow "The Hollow") of the [Emerald Grove](/wiki/Emerald_Grove "Emerald Grove")
  - If not previously recruited, Shadowheart approaches the party at:
    - The [Goblin Camp](/wiki/Goblin_Camp "Goblin Camp")
    - The [Mountain Pass](/wiki/Mountain_Pass "Mountain Pass")
- [![Wyll](/w/images/thumb/6/6d/Portrait_Wyll.png/25px-Portrait_Wyll.png.webp)](/wiki/Wyll "Wyll") [Wyll](/wiki/Wyll "Wyll") is inside the Emerald Grove, training young tieflings.
  - He appears in camp if Karlach joins the party before him. While initially hostile, he can be talked down and recruited.

## Permanent removal

Once they join the party, companions remain by the player character's side in most circumstances and, if killed during battle, can be brought back with [![Revivify](/w/images/thumb/5/59/Revivify_Icon.webp/20px-Revivify_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Revivify "Revivify") [Revivify](/wiki/Revivify "Revivify"), its scroll, or through [![Withers](/w/images/thumb/2/25/Portrait_Withers.png/25px-Portrait_Withers.png.webp)](/wiki/Withers "Withers") [Withers](/wiki/Withers "Withers"). However, there are several instances where a companion may leave, become hostile, or be killed during a cutscene – depending on the player character's choices. In these instances, they remain permanently [dead](/wiki/Dead_(Condition) "Dead (Condition)") and cannot be brought back under any circumstances.
