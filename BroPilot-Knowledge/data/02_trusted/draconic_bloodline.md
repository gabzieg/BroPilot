# Draconic Bloodline

**Draconic Bloodline** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Sorcerer#Subclass "Sorcerer") of [Sorcerer](/wiki/Sorcerer "Sorcerer"). These Sorcerers are harder to hit than their counterparts. They also have the ability to gain resistance and deal more damage with certain [types of damage](/wiki/Damage_Types "Damage Types") based on their bloodline.

### Level 1

[![Draconic Resilience](/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Draconic_Resilience_Icon.webp/40px-Draconic_Resilience_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Draconic_Resilience "Draconic Resilience")[Draconic Resilience](/wiki/Draconic_Resilience "Draconic Resilience")
:   Dragon-like scales cover parts of your skin. When you aren't wearing armour, your base [![Armour Class](/w/images/thumb/a/ae/Armour_Class_Icon.png/25px-Armour_Class_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Armour_Class "Armour Class") [Armour Class](/wiki/Armour_Class "Armour Class") is 13.

This bonus does not stack with *[Mage Armour](/wiki/Mage_Armour "Mage Armour")*. You may also choose an alternate [appearance](#Appearance).

## Appearance

It is possible however to change the scale colour to any other using the [Magic Mirror](/wiki/Magic_Mirror "Magic Mirror") at [camp](/wiki/Campsite "Campsite") or during [character creation](/wiki/Character_creation "Character creation").
