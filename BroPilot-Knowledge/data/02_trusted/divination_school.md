# Divination School

This page is about the Wizard subclass. For a list of all spells of this school, see [Divination](/wiki/Divination "Divination").

**Divination School** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Wizard#Level_2 "Wizard") of [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"). These Wizards specialize in fates and knowledge. They can manipulate random outcomes and see the unseen.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 2

Divination Savant
:   Halves the cost to learn Divination spells from [Scrolls](/wiki/Scrolls "Scrolls"). Learning these spells will only cost **![25 gp](/w/images/thumb/f/f5/Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png/23px-Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png.webp) 25 gp** per spell level.

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

[![Portent](/w/images/thumb/1/1d/Portent_Icon.webp/40px-Portent_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Portent "Portent")[Portent](/wiki/Portent "Portent")
:   Your dreams grant you glimpses that let you influence the future.
    After each [Long Rest](/wiki/Long_Rest "Long Rest"), the user gains two random Portent Dice. During the day, they can use their reaction to change the die of an [Attack Roll](/wiki/Attack_Roll "Attack Roll") or [Saving Throw](/wiki/Saving_Throw "Saving Throw") rolled near them to one of their Portent Dice.
    Each Portent Die can only be used once; unused Portent Dice are lost at the end of the day.

### Level 6

[![Expert Divination](/w/images/thumb/f/f4/Expert_Divination_Icon.webp/40px-Expert_Divination_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Expert_Divination "Expert Divination")[Expert Divination](/wiki/Expert_Divination "Expert Divination")
:   You gain an additional [Portent Die](/wiki/Portent "Portent"). When taking a [Short Rest](/wiki/Short_Rest "Short Rest") you receive a set of [Prophecies](/wiki/Prophecy "Prophecy"). Complete them to regain missing Portent Dice.

### Level 10

Both Third Eye bonuses can be active at the same time.

[![Third Eye: Darkvision](/w/images/thumb/b/b0/Third_Eye_Darkvision_Icon.webp/40px-Third_Eye_Darkvision_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Third_Eye:_Darkvision "Third Eye: Darkvision") [Third Eye: Darkvision](/wiki/Third_Eye:_Darkvision "Third Eye: Darkvision") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp))
:   You gain the ability to see in the dark out to a range of 24 m (80 ft) (![Recharge: Long rest](/w/images/thumb/f/fd/Recharge_Icons.png/25px-Recharge_Icons.png.webp) Recharge: [Long rest](/wiki/Long_rest "Long rest").)

[![Third Eye: See Invisibility](/w/images/thumb/b/bd/Third_Eye_See_Invisibility_Icon.webp/40px-Third_Eye_See_Invisibility_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Third_Eye:_See_Invisibility "Third Eye: See Invisibility") [Third Eye: See Invisibility](/wiki/Third_Eye:_See_Invisibility "Third Eye: See Invisibility") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp))
:   Become able to see [![Invisible](/w/images/thumb/d/d7/Invisible_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Invisible_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Invisible_(Condition) "Invisible (Condition)") [Invisible](/wiki/Invisible_(Condition) "Invisible (Condition)") creatures, and possibly reveal them to others. (![Recharge: Long rest](/w/images/thumb/f/fd/Recharge_Icons.png/25px-Recharge_Icons.png.webp) Recharge: [Long rest](/wiki/Long_rest "Long rest").)
