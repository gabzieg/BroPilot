# College Of Lore

**College of Lore** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Bard#Level_3 "Bard") of [Bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard"). These Bards gain the ability to hinder their foes with their performances, 3 more Skill Proficiencies, and additional pick of [Magical Secrets](/wiki/Magical_Secrets "Magical Secrets") spells.

![](/w/images/thumb/5/50/Class_Bard_Lore_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Bard_Lore_Badge_Icon.png.webp)

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 3

- Gain [Proficiency](/wiki/Proficiency "Proficiency") in 3 [Skills](/wiki/Skill "Skill").

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

[![Cutting Words](/w/images/thumb/5/5c/Cutting_Words_Icon.webp/40px-Cutting_Words_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Cutting_Words "Cutting Words") [Cutting Words](/wiki/Cutting_Words "Cutting Words") (![Reaction](/w/images/thumb/c/c1/Reaction_Icon.png/23px-Reaction_Icon.png.webp) + ![Bardic Inspiration](/w/images/thumb/b/be/Bardic_Inspiration_Resource_Icon.png/20px-Bardic_Inspiration_Resource_Icon.png.webp))
:   Use your wit to distract a creature and sap its confidence. It receives a ![](/w/images/thumb/2/2b/D6_Physical.png/27px-D6_Physical.png.webp) -1d6 penalty to ![Attack Roll](/w/images/thumb/b/ba/Attack_Roll_Icon.png/25px-Attack_Roll_Icon.png.webp) [Attack Roll](/wiki/Attack_Roll "Attack Roll"), ![Ability Check](/w/images/thumb/1/13/D20.png/23px-D20.png.webp) [Ability Check](/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check"), or ![Saving Throw](/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Saving_Throw_Icon.png/25px-Saving_Throw_Icon.png.webp) [Saving Throw](/wiki/Saving_Throw "Saving Throw").

### Level 6

[Magical Secrets](/wiki/Magical_Secrets "Magical Secrets")
:   Learn 2 non-Bard [Spells](/wiki/Spells "Spells") from the **[Magical Secrets](/wiki/Magical_Secrets "Magical Secrets")** list of level 3 or lower.
