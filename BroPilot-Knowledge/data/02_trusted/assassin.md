# Assassin

**Assassin** is a [subclass](/wiki/Rogue#Level_3 "Rogue") of [Rogue](/wiki/Rogue "Rogue"). It's focused on dealing higher and more reliable damage to enemies at the start of battle, especially if you can [surprise](/wiki/Initiative#Surprise "Initiative") them.

![](/w/images/thumb/d/df/Class_Rogue_Assassin_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Rogue_Assassin_Badge_Icon.png.webp)

>
> You prefer to deal sublime punishment to a single foe at a time - not in a duel, mind, because a duel implies chivalry, and you're too busy getting the job done for honour.
