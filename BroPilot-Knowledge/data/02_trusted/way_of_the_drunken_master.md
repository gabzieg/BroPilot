# Way Of The Drunken Master

**Drunken Master** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Monk#Level_3 "Monk") of [Monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk"). They have significant bonuses against drunk targets and can intoxicate enemies in combat. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

![](/w/images/thumb/e/e0/Class_Monk_Drunken_Master_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Monk_Drunken_Master_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> With the unpredictable lurches of a tippler, you hiccup your way through battle, frustrating foes with carefully executed movements concealed beneath a façade of incompetence.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 3

[![Cheeky Tipple](/w/images/thumb/e/e0/Cheeky_Tipple_Icon.webp/40px-Cheeky_Tipple_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Cheeky_Tipple "Cheeky Tipple")[Cheeky Tipple](/wiki/Cheeky_Tipple "Cheeky Tipple")
:   You are Immune to the negative effects of being [![Drunk](/w/images/thumb/0/0a/Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Drunk_(Condition) "Drunk (Condition)") [Drunk](/wiki/Drunk_(Condition) "Drunk (Condition)"). Once per day, when you drink alcohol, you regain half your ![](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp) [Ki Points](/wiki/Ki_Point "Ki Point") (rounded down).

[![Drunken Performance](/w/images/thumb/e/e3/Drunken_Performance_Icon.webp/40px-Drunken_Performance_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Drunken_Performance "Drunken Performance")[Drunken Performance](/wiki/Drunken_Performance "Drunken Performance")
:   You gain [Proficiency](/wiki/Proficiency "Proficiency") in [![Performance](/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Performance_Icon.png/25px-Performance_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Performance "Performance") [Performance](/wiki/Performance "Performance").

[![Drunken Technique](/w/images/thumb/e/ed/Drunken_Technique_Icon.webp/40px-Drunken_Technique_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Drunken_Technique "Drunken Technique") [Drunken Technique](/wiki/Drunken_Technique "Drunken Technique") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Ki Point](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp))
:   Punch twice in quick succession and grant yourself an additional 3 m / 10 ft movement and the benefits of [![Disengage](/w/images/thumb/9/94/Disengage_Icon.webp/20px-Disengage_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Disengage "Disengage") [Disengage](/wiki/Disengage "Disengage").

:   Flurry of Blows is replaced by Drunken Technique.

### Level 4

[![Intoxicating Strike](/w/images/thumb/6/6f/Intoxicating_Strike_Icon.webp/40px-Intoxicating_Strike_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Intoxicating_Strike "Intoxicating Strike") [Intoxicating Strike](/wiki/Intoxicating_Strike "Intoxicating Strike") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Ki Point](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp))
:   Hit a creature with your bare fists and possibly get them [![Drunk](/w/images/thumb/0/0a/Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp/25px-Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Drunk_(Condition) "Drunk (Condition)") [Drunk](/wiki/Drunk_(Condition) "Drunk (Condition)").

[![Life of the Party](/w/images/thumb/7/73/Life_of_the_Party_Icon.webp/40px-Life_of_the_Party_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Life_of_the_Party "Life of the Party")[Life of the Party](/wiki/Life_of_the_Party "Life of the Party")
:   Every time [![Intoxicating Strike](/w/images/thumb/6/6f/Intoxicating_Strike_Icon.webp/20px-Intoxicating_Strike_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Intoxicating_Strike "Intoxicating Strike") [Intoxicating Strike](/wiki/Intoxicating_Strike "Intoxicating Strike") hits, the monk gains [![Life of the Party](/w/images/thumb/1/19/Life_of_the_Party_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Life_of_the_Party_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Life_of_the_Party_(Condition) "Life of the Party (Condition)") [Life of the Party](/wiki/Life_of_the_Party_(Condition) "Life of the Party (Condition)"), which increases [Armour Class](/wiki/Armour_Class "Armour Class") and [Attack Rolls](/wiki/Attack_Rolls "Attack Rolls") against [![Drunk](/w/images/thumb/0/0a/Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Drunk_(Condition) "Drunk (Condition)") [Drunk](/wiki/Drunk_(Condition) "Drunk (Condition)") targets.

### Level 6

[![Leap to Your Feet](/w/images/thumb/5/5b/Leap_to_Your_Feet_Icon.webp/40px-Leap_to_Your_Feet_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Leap_to_Your_Feet "Leap to Your Feet")[Leap to Your Feet](/wiki/Leap_to_Your_Feet "Leap to Your Feet")
:   When you are [![Prone](/w/images/thumb/8/8e/Prone_Condition_Icon.webp/25px-Prone_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Prone_(Condition) "Prone (Condition)") [Prone](/wiki/Prone_(Condition) "Prone (Condition)"), standing up uses only 1.5 m (5 ft) of your ![](/w/images/thumb/9/97/Movement_Speed_Icon.png/23px-Movement_Speed_Icon.png.webp) [Movement Speed](/wiki/Resources#Movement_speed "Resources") speed.

[![Redirect Attack](/w/images/thumb/c/c7/Redirect_Attack_Icon.webp/40px-Redirect_Attack_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Redirect_Attack "Redirect Attack")[Redirect Attack](/wiki/Redirect_Attack "Redirect Attack")
:   When a creature misses you with a melee attack, you can spend 1 ![](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp) [Ki Point](/wiki/Ki_Point "Ki Point") as a [reaction](/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to make an unarmed attack against the attacker.

### Level 9

[![Sobering Realisation](/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Sobering_Realisation_Icon.webp/40px-Sobering_Realisation_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Sobering_Realisation "Sobering Realisation") [Sobering Realisation](/wiki/Sobering_Realisation "Sobering Realisation") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Ki Point](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp))
:   Hit a [![Drunk](/w/images/thumb/0/0a/Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Drunk_(Condition) "Drunk (Condition)") [Drunk](/wiki/Drunk_(Condition) "Drunk (Condition)") target and sober them up by dealing bonus [![Psychic](/w/images/thumb/0/04/Psychic_Damage_Icon.png/25px-Psychic_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Psychic "Psychic")⁠[Psychic](/wiki/Psychic "Psychic") damage. On a successful hit, the monk also loses [![Life of the Party](/w/images/thumb/1/19/Life_of_the_Party_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Life_of_the_Party_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Life_of_the_Party_(Condition) "Life of the Party (Condition)") [Life of the Party](/wiki/Life_of_the_Party_(Condition) "Life of the Party (Condition)").

### Level 11

[![Drunkard's Luck](/w/images/thumb/d/d3/Drunkard%27s_Luck_Icon.webp/40px-Drunkard%27s_Luck_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Drunkard%27s_Luck "Drunkard's Luck") [Drunkard's Luck](/wiki/Drunkard%27s_Luck "Drunkard's Luck") (2 ![Ki Point](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp))
:   When you make an [Ability Check](/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check"), [Attack Roll](/wiki/Attack_Roll "Attack Roll"), or [Saving Throw](/wiki/Saving_Throw "Saving Throw") and have [![Disadvantage](/w/images/thumb/b/b9/Disadvantage_Icon.png/27px-Disadvantage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Disadvantage "Disadvantage") [Disadvantage](/wiki/Disadvantage "Disadvantage") on the roll, you can spend ![](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Monk_Ki_Icon.png/20px-Monk_Ki_Icon.png.webp) 2 [Ki Points](/wiki/Ki_Point "Ki Point") to negate the Disadvantage for that roll.

## Notes

- This subclass synergies well with the [Drunk set](/wiki/Drunk_set "Drunk set") equipment set.
- [![](/w/images/thumb/5/54/Wine_Goblet_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Wine_Goblet_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Wine_Goblet "Wine Goblet") [Wine Goblets](/wiki/Wine_Goblet "Wine Goblet") can induce the [![Alcohol](/w/images/thumb/0/0a/Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Alcohol_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Alcohol_(Condition) "Alcohol (Condition)") [Alcohol](/wiki/Alcohol_(Condition) "Alcohol (Condition)") condition in combat without costing a bonus action.
