# Wildheart

**Wildheart** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Barbarian#Level_3 "Barbarian") of [Barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"). These Barbarians are attuned with nature and the beasts that live in it. Their rages resemble the animals that inspired them.

![](/w/images/thumb/2/2a/Class_Barbarian_Wildheart_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Barbarian_Wildheart_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> Your attunement with nature and its beasts inspires your rage, empowering you with supernatural might.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*
