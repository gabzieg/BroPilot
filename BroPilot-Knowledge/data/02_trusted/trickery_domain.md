# Trickery Domain

**Trickery Domain** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Cleric#Subclass "Cleric") of [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"). These Clerics are more specialised in stealth and illusion magic.

Player selectable [Deities](/wiki/Deities "Deities") associated with this domain: [![Garl Glittergold](/w/images/thumb/c/ce/Garl_Glittergold_Icon.webp/20px-Garl_Glittergold_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Garl_Glittergold "Garl Glittergold") [Garl Glittergold](/wiki/Garl_Glittergold "Garl Glittergold"), [![Lolth](/w/images/thumb/0/01/Lolth_Icon.webp/15px-Lolth_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Lolth "Lolth") [Lolth](/wiki/Lolth "Lolth"), [![Shar](/w/images/thumb/6/69/Shar_Icon.webp/20px-Shar_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Shar "Shar") [Shar](/wiki/Shar "Shar") (Shadowheart only), [![Tiamat](/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Tiamat_Icon.webp/20px-Tiamat_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Tiamat "Tiamat") [Tiamat](/wiki/Tiamat "Tiamat"), and [![Tymora](/w/images/thumb/0/03/Tymora_Icon.webp/20px-Tymora_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Tymora "Tymora") [Tymora](/wiki/Tymora "Tymora").
