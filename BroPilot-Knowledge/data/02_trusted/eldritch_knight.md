# Eldritch Knight

**Eldritch Knight** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Fighter#Level_3 "Fighter") of [Fighter](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter"). These Fighters specialize in [spellcasting](/wiki/Spells "Spells"), using their martial prowess and magic to attack enemies or support allies in combat. Eldritch Knights use [Intelligence](/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") as their [Spellcasting Ability](/wiki/Spells#Spellcasting_Ability_and_Proficiency "Spells"), and learn spells from the [Wizard spell list](/wiki/List_of_Wizard_spells "List of Wizard spells"), favouring the schools of [Evocation](/wiki/Evocation "Evocation") and [Abjuration](/wiki/Abjuration "Abjuration").
