# Wild Magic (Sorcerer Subclass)

See also: For the Barbarian version, see [Wild Magic (barbarian subclass)](/wiki/Wild_Magic_(barbarian_subclass) "Wild Magic (barbarian subclass)").

**Wild Magic** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Sorcerer#Subclass "Sorcerer") of [Sorcerer](/wiki/Sorcerer "Sorcerer"). These Sorcerers are capable of using the chaos of their magic to unleash various unexpected effects that can be helpful or chaotic. They also have the ability to gain [Advantage](/wiki/Advantage "Advantage") on rolls, in exchange for their magic becoming more erratic and surging more often.
