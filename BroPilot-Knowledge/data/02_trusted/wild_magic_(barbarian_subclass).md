# Wild Magic (Barbarian Subclass)

For the sorcerer subclass of the same name, see [Wild Magic (sorcerer subclass)](/wiki/Wild_Magic_(sorcerer_subclass) "Wild Magic (sorcerer subclass)").

**Wild Magic** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Barbarian#Level_3 "Barbarian") of [barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"). These barbarians manifest otherworldy magic to bolster their martial abilities.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*
