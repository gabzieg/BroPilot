A single attack, spell, trap, or other harmful effect can often result in multiple [Damage Rolls](/wiki/Damage_Rolls "Damage Rolls"). Determining the resulting damage from these various rolls can be difficult due to hidden mechanics which are commonly referred to by the community as **damage "sources"** and **damage "riders".** The term damage "instances" is sometimes used in place of damage "sources," but for the purpose of this page the only terms to be used are damage source(s) and damage rider(s). The game does not inform players what is a damage source vs. what is a damage rider, or how these two interact with each other. This page describes the mechanical differences between damage sources and damage riders, how they interact, and lists which effects and abilities fall into each category.

## Damage Sources (DS) vs. Damage Riders (DR)

A **damage source** is an attack or other effect that directly deals damage to one or more targets. Examples include striking a target with a [weapon](/wiki/Weapons "Weapons"), many [spells](/wiki/Spells "Spells"), or thrown items such as an [![](/w/images/thumb/2/29/GRN_Alchemist%27s_Fire_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-GRN_Alchemist%27s_Fire_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Alchemist%27s_Fire "Alchemist's Fire") [Alchemist's Fire](/wiki/Alchemist%27s_Fire "Alchemist's Fire"). Damage sources usually require the use of an [action](/wiki/Action "Action") (e.g. throwing an object such as a [grenade](/wiki/Grenades "Grenades")), [bonus action](/wiki/Bonus_action "Bonus action") (e.g. making an attack with your offhand weapon while dual wielding) or [reaction](/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") (e.g. the [![Hellish Rebuke](/w/images/thumb/8/82/Hellish_Rebuke_Icon.webp/25px-Hellish_Rebuke_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Hellish_Rebuke "Hellish Rebuke") [Hellish Rebuke](/wiki/Hellish_Rebuke "Hellish Rebuke") spell).

A **damage rider** is something which adds to the damage dealt by a source. A rider cannot deal its damage independently - there must be an applicable damage source for it to "ride". Examples of damage riders include the [![Hex](/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Hex_Icon.webp/25px-Hex_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Hex "Hex") [Hex](/wiki/Hex "Hex") spell adding damage to the damage from a Warlock's [![Eldritch Blast](/w/images/thumb/d/db/Eldritch_Blast_Icon.webp/25px-Eldritch_Blast_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Eldritch_Blast "Eldritch Blast") [Eldritch Blast](/wiki/Eldritch_Blast "Eldritch Blast") or weapon attacks, [coatings](/wiki/Coatings "Coatings") applied to weapons providing additional damage to a weapon attack, a 6th level [![Draconic Bloodline](/w/images/thumb/2/27/Class_Sorcerer_Draconic_Bloodline_Hotbar_Icon.png/25px-Class_Sorcerer_Draconic_Bloodline_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Draconic_Bloodline "Draconic Bloodline") [Draconic Bloodline](/wiki/Draconic_Bloodline "Draconic Bloodline") applying their [elemental affinity](/wiki/Elemental_Affinity:_Damage "Elemental Affinity: Damage") to a spell of the appropriate [damage type](/wiki/Damage_Types "Damage Types"), or a raging [![Barbarian](/w/images/thumb/8/8b/Barbarian_Icon_Small.webp/25px-Barbarian_Icon_Small.webp)](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian") [Barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian") adding bonus rage damage to their weapon attacks. Many damage riders are gained from passive features or other kinds of buffs. Any eligible damage riders from a damaging effect will be applied to a damage source which is a part of that damaging effect.

## Damage Riders treated as Damage Sources (DRS)

There are some damage riders which defy the typical trends described above. They still must "ride along" with a damage source in order to be applied, but when the damage is calculated they are treated as damage sources instead of damage riders. This means that these "**damage riders which are treated as damage sources**" can then cause normal damage riders to be applied once again as part of the same ability (e.g. attack or spell) which caused the damage.

**Nearly all DRS effects are treated like normal Damage Riders when playing on Honour mode, making much of the following information irrelevant for those players. See the [Undocumented Patch 5 updates](/wiki/Guide:Undocumented_Patch_5_updates#Honour_mode "Guide:Undocumented Patch 5 updates") for more details.**

### Example Calculation

A character with 20 Strength, the [Tavern Brawler](/wiki/Tavern_Brawler "Tavern Brawler") Feat, [![Lightning Charges](/w/images/thumb/4/47/Lightning_Charges_Condition_Icon.webp/25px-Lightning_Charges_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Lightning_Charges_(Condition) "Lightning Charges (Condition)") [Lightning Charges](/wiki/Lightning_Charges_(Condition) "Lightning Charges (Condition)"), and the [![](/w/images/thumb/5/5a/Ring_C_Silver_A_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Ring_C_Silver_A_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Ring_of_Flinging "Ring of Flinging") [Ring of Flinging](/wiki/Ring_of_Flinging "Ring of Flinging") uses the [![](/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Kuotoa_Javelin_PlusOne_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Kuotoa_Javelin_PlusOne_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Lightning_Jabber "Lightning Jabber") [Lightning Jabber](/wiki/Lightning_Jabber "Lightning Jabber") to make a thrown weapon attack against a target which is affected by [![Hex](/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Hex_Icon.webp/25px-Hex_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Hex "Hex") [Hex](/wiki/Hex "Hex").

**Damage Sources (DS):**

- Thrown weapon attack using the [![](/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Kuotoa_Javelin_PlusOne_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Kuotoa_Javelin_PlusOne_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Lightning_Jabber "Lightning Jabber") [Lightning Jabber](/wiki/Lightning_Jabber "Lightning Jabber") applies 1d6 (damage die) + 1 (weapon enchantment) + 5 (Strength modifier) piercing damage

**Damage Riders (DR):**

- [![Lightning Charges](/w/images/thumb/4/47/Lightning_Charges_Condition_Icon.webp/25px-Lightning_Charges_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Lightning_Charges_(Condition) "Lightning Charges (Condition)") [Lightning Charges](/wiki/Lightning_Charges_(Condition) "Lightning Charges (Condition)") add +1 Lightning Damage to each damage source
- The [![](/w/images/thumb/5/5a/Ring_C_Silver_A_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Ring_C_Silver_A_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Ring_of_Flinging "Ring of Flinging") [Ring of Flinging](/wiki/Ring_of_Flinging "Ring of Flinging") adds 1d4 damage which is of the same type as the weapon used (piercing in this example) to each damage source.
- [![Hex](/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Hex_Icon.webp/25px-Hex_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Hex "Hex") [Hex](/wiki/Hex "Hex") adds 1d6 necrotic damage to each damage source.
- [Tavern Brawler](/wiki/Tavern_Brawler "Tavern Brawler") adds the character's Strength modifier to thrown weapon attacks an additional time

**Damage Riders treated as Damage Sources (DRS):**

- The additional 1d4 damage from [![](/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Kuotoa_Javelin_PlusOne_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Kuotoa_Javelin_PlusOne_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Lightning_Jabber "Lightning Jabber") [Lightning Jabber](/wiki/Lightning_Jabber "Lightning Jabber")'s passive, [Throwing: Lightning Damage](/wiki/Throwing:_Lightning_Damage "Throwing: Lightning Damage"), is a damage rider treated as a damage source. This means it will cause all of the Damage Riders listed above to activate a second time.

**Calculating total damage:**

To determine the total damage applied take each DS and DRS. Then apply the damage riders onto each of these. In this example that means one should evaluate the normal damage source of throwing a spear plus all damage riders, followed by the additional lightning damage from the [![](/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Kuotoa_Javelin_PlusOne_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Kuotoa_Javelin_PlusOne_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Lightning_Jabber "Lightning Jabber") [Lightning Jabber](/wiki/Lightning_Jabber "Lightning Jabber") with all damage riders applied again.

## Technical details

The scripts defining all the effects in the game are found in `.txt` files inside `Gustav.pak` and `Shared.pak`. (See [Modding:Unpacking and converting files](/wiki/Modding:Unpacking_and_converting_files "Modding:Unpacking and converting files")). In these scripts, damage rider effects are implemented with one of several damage functions. These are:

- `WeaponDamage()`, `CharacterWeaponDamage()`, and `CharacterUnarmedDamage()`:  
  The basic, well-behaved damage bonuses are implemented with this function. The bonus will apply *only* to the triggering attack, and nothing else.  
  Example effect: 2⁠[![Acid](/w/images/thumb/5/5f/Acid_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Acid_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Acid "Acid")⁠[Acid](/wiki/Acid "Acid") from [Caustic Band](/wiki/Caustic_Band "Caustic Band").
- `DamageBonus()`:  
  Damage riders using this function will apply their bonus damage to each independent damage instance. When combined with damage riders using `DealDamage()` (see below), a single attack can create numerous independent damage instances. In these cases, `DamageBonus()` will apply its bonus damage multiple times, but `WeaponDamage()` only once.  
  Example effect: 2⁠[![Radiant](/w/images/thumb/7/78/Radiant_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Radiant_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Radiant "Radiant")⁠[Radiant](/wiki/Radiant "Radiant") from [Callous Glow Ring](/wiki/Callous_Glow_Ring "Callous Glow Ring").
- `DealDamage()`:  
  This function is used by all damaging attacks, spells, and status effects to apply their damage. It is also used by "**damage riders treated as damage sources**" to apply their bonus damage. It creates a separate damage instance that can be independently boosted by applicable `DamageBonus()` effects. Furthermore, damage riders using `DealDamage()` can trigger each other in some cases, creating far more damage instances than might be expected.  
  Example effect: 1d8⁠[![Physical](/w/images/thumb/f/f0/Slashing_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Slashing_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Physical "Physical")⁠[Physical](/wiki/Physical "Physical") from [Colossus Slayer](/wiki/Colossus_Slayer "Colossus Slayer").

Each of these functions can optionally specify the damage type. If not specified, the damage type will be inherited from the triggering `DealDamage()` instance.

## Potential bugs

The following items compose a non-exhaustive list which indicates that the "Damage Riders" as compared to "Damage Riders treated as Damage Sources" may include several bugs and therefore many of these abilities may change in future patches:

- [Patch 3](/wiki/Patch_Notes "Patch Notes") changed [Lightning Charges](/wiki/Lightning_Charges_(Condition) "Lightning Charges (Condition)") from a "Damage Rider treated as a Damage Source" to a "Damage Rider."
- [Patch 5](/wiki/Patch_Notes "Patch Notes")'s patch-notes mention a change to [Tavern Brawler](/wiki/Tavern_Brawler "Tavern Brawler") in regards to thrown weapons, likely intending to change it from a "Damage Rider treated as a Damage Source" to a "Damage Rider" - in fact no change was made. Instead, Dwarven Thrower, Lightning Jabber, and Hunter's Mark were updated in a comparable manner, leaving them still able to trigger certain other DRS-effects while not applying Damage Riders themselves.
- [Sneak Attack](/wiki/Sneak_Attack_(Disambiguation) "Sneak Attack (Disambiguation)") is treated as a Damage Rider when a character uses the Sneak Attack action, but as a "Damage Rider treated as a Damage Source" when Sneak Attack is applied by the automatic reaction system.
