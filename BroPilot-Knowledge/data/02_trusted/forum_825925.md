# Where to find a normal sickle?

Fonte: https://forums.larian.com/ubbthreads.php?ubb=showflat&Number=825925 | Board: Help Tips and Tricks

### Post original

I fancied making a sussur sickle this playthrough, but can’t find a basic sickle to start from. I thought redcaps might drop them, but while they’re animated with sickles there are none on their bodies. Then I wondered about the kua toa, but only their priest drops a magical sickle.  
  
Does anyone know where I might be able to find one, ideally without theft or murder?

---

### Resposta #1

Not without theft, unfortunately.  
  
The only one I know of is in the room with Nettie. It's super easy to get when you first walk in because she's looking at the exhausted bird.  
  
The sickle is on the table to the right of the entrance into the room where you first see her. It's by, I think, a couple of carrots and maybe a potion. Holding down "alt" will show it.
