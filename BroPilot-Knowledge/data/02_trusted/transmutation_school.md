# Transmutation School

This page is about the Wizard subclass. For a list of all spells of this school, see [Transmutation](/wiki/Transmutation "Transmutation").

**Transmutation School** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Wizard#Level_2 "Wizard") of [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"). These Wizards become accomplished [Alchemists](/wiki/Alchemy "Alchemy"), able to pass benefits to companions. At higher levels they can turn into a bird without using a spell slot.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 2

Transmutation Savant
:   Halves the cost to learn Transmutation spells from [Scrolls](/wiki/Scrolls "Scrolls"). Learning these spells will only cost **![25 gp](/w/images/thumb/f/f5/Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png/23px-Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png.webp) 25 gp** per spell level.

[![Experimental Alchemy](/w/images/thumb/e/e8/Experimental_Alchemy_Icon.webp/40px-Experimental_Alchemy_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Experimental_Alchemy "Experimental Alchemy")[Experimental Alchemy](/wiki/Experimental_Alchemy "Experimental Alchemy")
:   You brew two [Alchemical Solutions](/wiki/Alchemy "Alchemy") instead of one when combining extracts, if you succeed a [Difficulty Class](/wiki/Difficulty_Class "Difficulty Class") 15 [Medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") [Check](/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check").

### Level 10

[![Shapechanger](/w/images/thumb/9/9d/Shapechanger_Icon.webp/40px-Shapechanger_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Shapechanger "Shapechanger") [Shapechanger](/wiki/Shapechanger "Shapechanger") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp))

    If the blue jay's [hit points](/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") drop to 0, the target reverts to its original form with its original hit points.
