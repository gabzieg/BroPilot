# Skills

This article is about the in-game attributes. You may be looking for [Actions](/wiki/Actions "Actions").

**Abilities** are representations of a creature's physical and mental attributes in *Baldur's Gate 3*. There are six abilities in *Baldur's Gate 3*, and every creature has a set of six **ability scores** – numerical values which represent how well they perform at each ability.

Each ability score translates directly into an **ability score modifier** – modifiers which function as bonuses or penalties that are added to most [dice rolls](/wiki/Dice_rolls "Dice rolls") in the game.

Ability scores are bought with points during [character creation](/wiki/Character_creation "Character creation"). The minimum ability score during character creation is 8, and the maximum is 15, before any of the ability score increases are applied.

### Ability score modifiers

Each ability has an ability score modifier, derived from its ability score. Ability score modifiers are added to nearly all ability checks, [saving throws](/wiki/Saving_throws "Saving throws"), and [attacks](/wiki/Attacks "Attacks") a creature makes associated with that ability, as well as to the [Difficulty Class](/wiki/Difficulty_Class "Difficulty Class") (DC) of any spells they cast.

Creatures also add an ability score modifier to some [damage rolls](/wiki/Damage "Damage") they make. Some [features](/wiki/Features "Features") allow creatures to add additional or alternative ability score modifiers to specific rolls. Creatures add their dexterity modifier to [initiative rolls](/wiki/Initiative "Initiative")

An ability's modifier is decreased by *1* at every odd score below 10, and is increased by *1* at every even score above 10. Alternatively, it can be calculated by subtracting 10 from the ability score, then dividing by 2 and rounding down.

## Ability checks

This section is an excerpt from [Dice rolls § Ability checks](/wiki/Dice_rolls#Ability_checks "Dice rolls").

Ability checks are dice rolls made to determine whether a creature succeeds or fails at a task. They are rolled against the task's DC. The DC of these checks are generally predetermined by the game. Each ability check is made using one of the six abilities in the game, and creatures add an ability's corresponding ability score modifier to the results of ability checks they make.

### Skills

Ability checks are usually made using a specified skill. Skills are specific areas of expertise, each associated with an ability that characters can be proficient in.

Characters add their proficiency bonus to any ability checks they make using skills they are proficient in.

Custom characters gain proficiency in two skills based on their chosen [background](/wiki/Background "Background") during character creation, with [origin](/wiki/Origin "Origin") characters having predetermined backgrounds. All characters can choose 2 more skills to be proficient in from a list of skills determined by their [class](/wiki/Class "Class") ([bards](/wiki/Bard "Bard") and [rangers](/wiki/Ranger "Ranger") choose 3, while [rogues](/wiki/Rogue "Rogue") choose 4).

Additionally, some [races](/wiki/Races "Races"), subclasses, and [feats](/wiki/Feats "Feats") also grant proficiency in specific skills, and [bards](/wiki/Bards "Bards") receive the class feature [![Jack of All Trades](/w/images/thumb/1/19/Jack_of_All_Trades_Icon.webp/20px-Jack_of_All_Trades_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Jack_of_All_Trades "Jack of All Trades") [Jack of All Trades](/wiki/Jack_of_All_Trades "Jack of All Trades") at level 2, allowing them to add half their proficiency bonus (rounded down) to ability checks they make using skills they *are not* proficient in.

Proficiency does not stack – there is no benefit to having multiple sources of proficiency for a skill.

#### Expertise

[![](/w/images/b/b8/Expertise.png)](/wiki/File:Expertise.png)

Characters can also have expertise in a skill, which allows them to add *double* their proficiency bonus when making a corresponding ability check. While it is possible to have both proficiency and expertise in a skill, they do not stack. However, some sources of expertise do require the character to already be proficient in a skill.

Sources of expertise that require prior proficiency in the respective skill include:

- [Bards](/wiki/Bard "Bard") gain expertise in any two skills they are proficient in at both levels 3 and 10.
- [Rogues](/wiki/Rogue "Rogue") gain expertise in any two skills they are proficient in at both levels 1 and 6.

Sources of expertise that do not require prior proficiency in the respective skill include:

- A [Rock gnome's](/wiki/Gnome#Rock_gnome "Gnome") expertise in [History](/wiki/History "History")
- A [Knowledge Domain](/wiki/Knowledge_Domain "Knowledge Domain") [Cleric's](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric") selection of expertise in two of the following skills at level 1: [Arcana](/wiki/Arcana "Arcana"), [History](/wiki/History "History"), [Nature](/wiki/Nature "Nature"), or [Religion](/wiki/Religion "Religion")
- Expertise in [Deception](/wiki/Deception "Deception") and [Performance](/wiki/Performance "Performance") from the [![Actor](/w/images/thumb/3/3f/Perform_Whistle_Icon.webp/20px-Perform_Whistle_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Actor "Actor") [Actor](/wiki/Actor "Actor") feat
- Expertise in [Deception](/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Intimidation](/wiki/Intimidation "Intimidation"), and [Persuasion](/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") from the [![Illithid Expertise](/w/images/thumb/a/ae/Illithid_Expertise_Icon.webp/20px-Illithid_Expertise_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Illithid_Expertise "Illithid Expertise") [Illithid Expertise](/wiki/Illithid_Expertise "Illithid Expertise") feature

### Types of ability checks

Automatic rolls
:   Some ability checks are automatic. For example, when a creature approaches a trap, a [Perception](/wiki/Perception "Perception") check is rolled to determine whether they notice it. Perception is a Wisdom skill, so the creature adds their Wisdom modifier and, if proficient in Perception, their proficiency bonus to the ability check.

During dialogue
:   Ability checks are also common during dialogue, where some responses require an ability check to determine the outcome. Examples include using Charisma skills like [Persuasion](/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion"), [Deception](/wiki/Deception "Deception"), or [Intimidation](/wiki/Intimidation "Intimidation") to influence others, or Intelligence skills like [Arcana](/wiki/Arcana "Arcana"), [History](/wiki/History "History"), or [Religion](/wiki/Religion "Religion") to determine or remember facts. Before these checks, a button labelled "Add Bonus" appears along the bottom of the screen, allowing the player character and other party members to use certain spells and / or items to grant bonuses to the check. This is also possible when making ability checks while interacting with objects, such as when disarming traps or picking locks.

Contests
:   A contest is a situation in which two creatures both make a roll, and the one with the better roll wins. One could also say that one creature makes a roll to determine the [Difficulty Class](/wiki/Difficulty_Class "Difficulty Class") for the other creature's roll. An example is attacking while [hidden](/wiki/Hide "Hide"), in which case the target rolls Perception and the attacker rolls Stealth to contest it.
:   This may also involve a [passive check](/wiki/Passive_check "Passive check") instead of an active roll. For example, a hiding player character in the line of sight of a non-allied creature must make a Stealth check contesting the other creature's passive Perception score. Another example is attempting to [![Shove](/w/images/thumb/6/6b/Shove_Icon.webp/20px-Shove_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Shove "Shove") [Shove](/wiki/Shove "Shove") or [![Throw](/w/images/thumb/e/e3/Throw_Icon.webp/20px-Throw_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Throw "Throw") [Throw](/wiki/Throw "Throw") a creature, in which case the creature making the attempt rolls an [Athletics](/wiki/Athletics "Athletics") check, which contests the other creature's passive Athletics or [Acrobatics](/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") score (whichever is higher).

#### Frequency of checks

Perception is among the most commonly rolled skills in the game, as it automatically triggers around traps, hidden objects, and ambushes.

Of the 3 skills that only appear in dialogue, Persuasion, Deception, and Intimidation, Persuasion is the most common (appearing in 293 dialogs), followed by Intimidation (appearing in 209 dialogs), and Deception (147 dialogs).[[*verify*](/wiki/bg3wiki:Verification "bg3wiki:Verification")]

Of the two skills that are used in melee combat, Athletics and Acrobatics, Athletics is generally more useful, as Athletics is checked both when shoving, and being shoved – Acrobatics is only checked on being shoved. Athletics also has more checks outside of combat than Acrobatics (which only has 2 non-combats checks in the whole game).[[*verify*](/wiki/bg3wiki:Verification "bg3wiki:Verification")]

Arcana is among the most common lore checks, and often enables options for using magical devices. Animal Handling, Medicine, and Performance rolls are less common, and Animal Handling does not appear as an option if [![Speak with Animals](/w/images/thumb/2/24/Speak_with_Animals_Icon.webp/20px-Speak_with_Animals_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Speak_with_Animals "Speak with Animals") [Speak with Animals](/wiki/Speak_with_Animals "Speak with Animals") is available.

## Saving throws

This section is an excerpt from [Dice rolls § Saving throws](/wiki/Dice_rolls#Saving_throws "Dice rolls").

Saving throws represent a creature's attempt to "save" themself from harm. Spells and actions taken by other creatures frequently allow their targets to attempt a save, as do hazards like [traps](/wiki/Traps "Traps") and [surfaces](/wiki/Surface "Surface"). Each save has an associated ability and a save DC that creatures attempting to 'save' roll against. When attempting a save, a creature adds the ability score modifier of that save's associated ability; if they are proficient in saves made using that ability, they add their proficiency bonus as well.

While the result of an attempted saving throw is always binary – either a success or a failure – the exact outcome of a successful save depends on the effect in question. Typically, the damage or conditions inflicted by the associated effect will be reduced in severity, or sometimes negated entirely.

As stated above, saving throws do not automatically fail or succeed on natural 1s and natural 20s, except when made to maintain [concentration](/wiki/Concentration "Concentration") or during dialogue.

A [number of features](/wiki/List_of_features_and_items_that_affect_saving_throws "List of features and items that affect saving throws") affect saving throws, and some [races](/wiki/Races "Races") have advantage on certain types of saves.

### Save proficiency

Each class grants proficiency in saving throws with two abilities. Though when multiclassing, only the first class taken grants saving throw proficiencies (unless they come from a separate feature, like [![Iron Mind](/w/images/thumb/8/8c/Iron_Mind_Icon.webp/20px-Iron_Mind_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Iron_Mind "Iron Mind") [Iron Mind](/wiki/Iron_Mind "Iron Mind")). An additional save proficiency can be gained by taking the [Resilient](/wiki/Resilient "Resilient") feat.

### Save DCs

The Difficulty Class rolled against when attempting to save is called the save DC. A successful save can mean completely avoiding negative effects, reducing the damage received (usually by half), or both. For example, successfully saving against a spike trap could mean that a creature takes no damage because it successfully evades the spikes. On the other hand, if caught in the area of effect of a [![Fireball](/w/images/thumb/c/cb/Fireball_Icon.webp/20px-Fireball_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Fireball "Fireball") [Fireball](/wiki/Fireball "Fireball") spell, a successful save merely halves the damage. Saving against a [![Thunderwave](/w/images/thumb/0/04/Thunderwave_Icon.webp/20px-Thunderwave_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Thunderwave "Thunderwave") [Thunderwave](/wiki/Thunderwave "Thunderwave") spell both halves the damage taken and prevents a creature from being pushed by the spell.

Different mechanics calculate save DC differently:

Spell save DC
:   The Difficulty Class of a spell that can be saved against is determined through the following formula:

    :   8 + proficiency bonus + spellcasting ability modifier

:   Each class, except for fighter and rogue, has a spellcasting ability which is used for its class features (features that appear under a class's tab in the spellbook) and spells that call for a spellcasting ability modifier. Some non-class features also use this spellcasting ability, such as spells cast through items (including [scrolls](/wiki/Scrolls "Scrolls")), free recasts of [recastable spells](/wiki/Category:Recastable_spells "Category:Recastable spells") (regardless of source), and [illithid powers](/wiki/Illithid_powers "Illithid powers"). A multiclassed character uses the spellcasting ability of their newest class (the class in which they most recently reached level 1) for non-class features. There are some class features which are bugged and will incorrectly use the newest class's spellcasting ability, as noted on those features' pages.

:   Fighter and rogue do not have a spellcasting ability properly defined, and so use the caster's default spellcasting ability. The default spellcasting ability is Intelligence for custom characters, as well as the Origin characters [![Astarion](/w/images/thumb/1/1b/Portrait_Astarion.png/25px-Portrait_Astarion.png.webp)](/wiki/Astarion "Astarion") [Astarion](/wiki/Astarion "Astarion"), [![The Dark Urge](/w/images/thumb/8/8c/Portrait_The_Dark_Urge.png/25px-Portrait_The_Dark_Urge.png.webp)](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge") [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), [![Gale](/w/images/thumb/1/11/Portrait_Gale.png/25px-Portrait_Gale.png.webp)](/wiki/Gale "Gale") [Gale](/wiki/Gale "Gale"), [![Karlach](/w/images/thumb/b/b2/Portrait_Karlach.png/25px-Portrait_Karlach.png.webp)](/wiki/Karlach "Karlach") [Karlach](/wiki/Karlach "Karlach"), and [![Lae'zel](/w/images/thumb/8/86/Portrait_Lae%27zel.png/25px-Portrait_Lae%27zel.png.webp)](/wiki/Lae%27zel "Lae'zel") [Lae'zel](/wiki/Lae%27zel "Lae'zel"); Wisdom is used by [![Halsin](/w/images/thumb/f/fe/Portrait_Halsin.png/25px-Portrait_Halsin.png.webp)](/wiki/Halsin "Halsin") [Halsin](/wiki/Halsin "Halsin"), [![Jaheira](/w/images/thumb/a/a8/Portrait_Jaheira.png/25px-Portrait_Jaheira.png.webp)](/wiki/Jaheira "Jaheira") [Jaheira](/wiki/Jaheira "Jaheira"), [![Minsc](/w/images/thumb/d/d6/Portrait_Minsc.png/25px-Portrait_Minsc.png.webp)](/wiki/Minsc "Minsc") [Minsc](/wiki/Minsc "Minsc"), [![Minthara](/w/images/thumb/8/8e/Portrait_Minthara_%28263%29.png/25px-Portrait_Minthara_%28263%29.png.webp)](/wiki/Minthara "Minthara") [Minthara](/wiki/Minthara "Minthara"), and [![Shadowheart](/w/images/thumb/7/7e/Portrait_Shadowheart.png/25px-Portrait_Shadowheart.png.webp)](/wiki/Shadowheart "Shadowheart") [Shadowheart](/wiki/Shadowheart "Shadowheart"); and finally, [![Wyll](/w/images/thumb/6/6d/Portrait_Wyll.png/25px-Portrait_Wyll.png.webp)](/wiki/Wyll "Wyll") [Wyll](/wiki/Wyll "Wyll") uses Charisma. Weapon action DC
:   Most weapons allow proficient users to perform special "[weapon actions](/wiki/Weapon_actions "Weapon actions")", which are typically limited to once per short rest (e.g., [![Backbreaker](/w/images/thumb/e/e0/Backbreaker_Icon.webp/20px-Backbreaker_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Backbreaker "Backbreaker") [Backbreaker](/wiki/Backbreaker "Backbreaker")). These actions often include the chance to inflict a condition on the target, and these conditions require the target to attempt a save to avoid them. Each weapon action can grant its own inherent bonus to DC that is not listed anywhere, but is frequently +2. The Difficulty Class of saves against weapon actions is calculated as follows:

    :   Weapon Action DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + [Strength](/wiki/Strength "Strength") or [Dexterity](/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier (whichever is higher) + inherent weapon action bonus DC
:   Equipment other than weapons may have features that use weapon action DC. Some class features also use weapon action DC, notably most manoeuvres of a [![Battle Master](/w/images/thumb/7/73/Class_Fighter_Battle_Master_Hotbar_Icon.png/20px-Class_Fighter_Battle_Master_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Battle_Master "Battle Master") [Battle Master](/wiki/Battle_Master "Battle Master"), and many [![Monk](/w/images/thumb/3/36/Monk_Icon_Small.webp/20px-Monk_Icon_Small.webp)](/wiki/Monk "Monk") [Monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk") abilities, such as [![Stunning Strike](/w/images/thumb/b/b5/Stunning_Strike_Unarmed_Icon.webp/20px-Stunning_Strike_Unarmed_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Stunning_Strike_(Unarmed) "Stunning Strike (Unarmed)") [Stunning Strike](/wiki/Stunning_Strike_(Unarmed) "Stunning Strike (Unarmed)").
:   Some [unique weapons actions](/wiki/Weapon_actions#Unique_weapon_actions "Weapon actions") instead use spell save DC.

Hybrid save DC
:   Certain weapon actions, notably [![Concussive Smash](/w/images/thumb/5/57/Concussive_Smash_Icon.webp/20px-Concussive_Smash_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Concussive_Smash "Concussive Smash") [Concussive Smash](/wiki/Concussive_Smash "Concussive Smash"), instead allow the acting creature to either use their spell save DC or weapon action DC with a +2 bonus, whichever is higher.

Fixed Save DC
:   In the case of threats which do not originate from a spellcaster, such as a trap or poisonous apple, the game sets the DC based on how serious the threat is intended to be. For example, a slightly spoiled tart could impose a DC 5 Constitution save when eaten, whereas a potent snake venom could impose a DC 15 Constitution save on the victim. Many consumable items, such as elemental [arrows](/wiki/Arrows "Arrows"), [throwables](/wiki/Grenades "Grenades"), and [coatings](/wiki/Coatings "Coatings"), use fixed save DCs.

### Death saving throws

Death saving throws are a special type of saving throw made by playable characters after they have been [![Downed](/w/images/thumb/6/66/Downed_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Downed_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)") [Downed](/wiki/Downed_(Condition) "Downed (Condition)"). Death saves are made once per turn while the character remains Downed. Death saves are always DC 10. If a Downed character receives damage from any source that is not a critical hit, they automatically fail one death saving throw. A critical hit against a Downed character results in 2 failed saves. Melee attacks against a Downed target are always classified as critical hits.

Playable characters [die](/wiki/Dead "Dead") when three failures are accumulated or stabilise when three successes are accumulated, whichever happens first.

Death saving throws are not associated with an ability score and so do not get any ability score modifiers, nor do they benefit from a proficiency bonus. They only benefit from bonuses which apply to all saving throws (such as [![Bless](/w/images/thumb/c/c4/Bless_Icon.webp/20px-Bless_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Bless "Bless") [Bless](/wiki/Bless "Bless")) or specifically to death saves (such as from the [![](/w/images/thumb/6/61/Ring_A_Gem_A_Gold_1_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Ring_A_Gem_A_Gold_1_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Family_Ring "Family Ring") [Family Ring](/wiki/Family_Ring "Family Ring")).

## Spellcasting ability

For more information, see [Spellcasting ability](/wiki/Spellcasting_ability "Spellcasting ability").

Every class has a spellcasting ability whose modifier is added to the [attack rolls](/wiki/Attack_rolls "Attack rolls") and [spell save DCs](/wiki/Spell_save_DC "Spell save DC") of their known spells.

Intelligence
:   [Fighter](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter"), [Rogue](/wiki/Rogue "Rogue"), [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard").

Wisdom
:   [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"), [Druid](/wiki/Druid "Druid"), [Monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk"), [Ranger](/wiki/Ranger "Ranger").

Charisma
:   [Barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"), [Bard](/wiki/Bard "Bard"), [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"), [Sorcerer](/wiki/Sorcerer "Sorcerer"), [Warlock](/wiki/Warlock "Warlock").

Spells from sources other than class instead use the spellcasting ability of the class the caster most recently took a *first* level in.

## Interactions with classes

Each [class](/wiki/Class "Class") has designated primary and secondary abilities. While these designations have no particular mechanical significance, they are mentioned in character creation, and serve as recommendations for players unfamiliar with the rules of the game. Classes do, however, frequently have features that rely on these abilities.

## Overview of abilities and skills

### Strength

Main article: [Strength](/wiki/Strength "Strength")

[![](/w/images/thumb/2/2c/Strength_Score_Icon.png/120px-Strength_Score_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Strength "Strength")
> “
>
> Muscles and physical power. Affects your effectiveness with melee weapons. Also determines how far you can jump and how much you can carry.
>
> „

— In-game ability description

Strength represents a creature's ability to exert physical force, and is the game's recommended primary ability for [barbarians](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian") and [fighters](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter").

Attack and damage rolls
:   Creatures add their Strength modifier to the results of [attack](/wiki/Attack "Attack") and [damage](/wiki/Damage "Damage") rolls for most melee weapons.

Jump distance
:   The distance which a creature can [jump](/wiki/Jump "Jump") is determined by their Strength score.

Carrying capacity
:   [Carrying capacity](/wiki/Carrying_capacity "Carrying capacity") uses a creature's Strength score to determine how much weight it can carry before becoming [![Encumbered](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Encumbered_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Encumbered_(Condition) "Encumbered (Condition)") [Encumbered](/wiki/Encumbered_(Condition) "Encumbered (Condition)").

Throws
:   The maximum [weight](/wiki/Weight "Weight") a creature can [![Throw](/w/images/thumb/e/e3/Throw_Icon.webp/20px-Throw_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Throw "Throw") [Throw](/wiki/Throw "Throw") is determined by their Strength score.

Strength checks
:   Strength checks are made when physical might is required, such as when attempting to push over a heavy object or when attempting to force open locked doors or containers during dialogue.

Athletics
:   [Athletics](/wiki/Athletics "Athletics") is used during attempts to [![Shove](/w/images/thumb/6/6b/Shove_Icon.webp/20px-Shove_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Shove "Shove") [Shove](/wiki/Shove "Shove") other creatures, as well as when attempting to resist being shoved.

#### Strength saves

Strength saves are rare, and primarily called for when attempting to resist being [knocked prone](/wiki/Prone_(condition) "Prone (condition)").

### Dexterity

Main article: [Dexterity](/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity")

[![](/w/images/thumb/2/27/Dexterity_Score_Icon.png/120px-Dexterity_Score_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity")
> “
>
> Agility, reflexes, and balance. Affects your effectiveness with ranged and Finesse weapons. Also affects your Initiative and Armour Class.
>
> „

— In-game ability description

Dexterity represents a creature's agility and reflexes, and is the game's recommended primary ability for [monks](/wiki/Monk "Monk"), [rangers](/wiki/Ranger "Ranger"), and [rogues](/wiki/Rogue "Rogue").

Attack and damage rolls
:   Creatures add their Dexterity modifier to the results of attack and damage rolls of ranged weapons and, when the creature's Dexterity score is higher than their Strength score, melee weapons with the [Finesse](/wiki/Finesse "Finesse") property.

Armour Class
:   Creatures also add their Dexterity modifier to their [Armour Class](/wiki/Armour_Class "Armour Class") (AC) – making them more difficult to hit. This bonus goes up to a maximum of *+2* when wearing medium [armour](/wiki/Armour "Armour") (excluding armour with the [![Exotic Material](/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Generic_Feature_Icon.webp/20px-Generic_Feature_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Exotic_Material "Exotic Material") [Exotic Material](/wiki/Exotic_Material "Exotic Material") feature), and is ignored when wearing heavy armour.

Initiative
:   Creatures add their Dexterity modifier when rolling for [initiative](/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"), which determines the [turn order](/wiki/Turn_order "Turn order") during combat.

Dexterity checks
:   Dexterity checks are made when attempting to be nimble.

Acrobatics
:   [Acrobatics](/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") is used to resist attempts at being shoved.

Sleight of Hand
:   [Sleight of Hand](/wiki/Sleight_of_Hand "Sleight of Hand") is used when attempting to pick locks, disarm [traps](/wiki/Traps "Traps"), pickpocket NPCs, or steal items without being noticed.

Stealth
:   [Stealth](/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") is used to [![Hide](/w/images/thumb/3/32/Hide_Icon.webp/20px-Hide_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Hide "Hide") [Hide](/wiki/Hide "Hide") from other characters and when attempting to remain undetected with [![Greater Invisibility](/w/images/thumb/8/86/Greater_Invisibility_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Greater_Invisibility_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Greater_Invisibility_(Condition) "Greater Invisibility (Condition)") [Greater Invisibility](/wiki/Greater_Invisibility_(Condition) "Greater Invisibility (Condition)").

#### Dexterity saves

Dexterity saves are commonly called for by spells and traps that deal area of effect damage, usually halving the damage of these effects.

### Constitution

Main article: [Constitution](/wiki/Constitution "Constitution")

[![](/w/images/thumb/a/a0/Constitution_Score_Icon.png/120px-Constitution_Score_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Constitution "Constitution")
> “
>
> Stamina and physical endurance. Affects your hit point maximum.
>
> „

— In-game ability description

Constitution represents a creature's health and endurance.

Hit points
:   Creatures add their Constitution modifier to the amount of [hit points](/wiki/Hit_points "Hit points") they gain on level up. This increase is applied retroactively.

Constitution checks
:   Constitution checks are made when a creature's resilience is tested, but more commonly a Constitution save is attempted instead. Constitution checks are rare and have no associated skills.

#### Constitution saves

Constitution saves are made to maintain [concentration](/wiki/Concentration "Concentration") on certain spells, and are used when saving against [poisons](/wiki/Poisons "Poisons") or spells that test a target's endurance or resilience.

### Intelligence

Main article: [Intelligence](/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence")

[![](/w/images/thumb/c/c7/Intelligence_Score_Icon.png/120px-Intelligence_Score_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence")
> “
>
> Memory and mental power. Improves spellcasting for wizards.
>
> „

— In-game ability description

Intelligence represents a creature's recall, as well as their ability to reason and think quickly. It is the game's recommended primary ability for [wizards](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"), and serves as the spellcasting ability of wizards, [Eldritch Knights](/wiki/Eldritch_Knight "Eldritch Knight"), and [Arcane Tricksters](/wiki/Arcane_Trickster "Arcane Trickster").

Intelligence checks
:   Intelligence checks are used when a creature attempts to apply logic or reason, or when attempting to remember something specific.

Arcana
:   [Arcana](/wiki/Arcana "Arcana") is used when attempting to remember facts about the Weave, the source of magic and spells.

History
:   [History](/wiki/History "History") is used when attempting to remember specific historical dates or events.

Investigation
:   [Investigation](/wiki/Investigation "Investigation") is used by creatures to discover unusual things in their surroundings, and is used during attempts at deduction during dialogue.

Nature
:   [Nature](/wiki/Nature "Nature") is used when attempting to remember facts about nature.

Religion
:   [Religion](/wiki/Religion "Religion") is used when attempting to remember facts about deities and the planes of existence.

#### Intelligence saves

Intelligence saves are used against psionic spells and features, such as those used by [illithids](/wiki/Illithids "Illithids").

### Wisdom

Main article: [Wisdom](/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom")

[![](/w/images/thumb/8/88/Wisdom_Score_Icon.png/120px-Wisdom_Score_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom")
> “
>
> Senses and intuition. Improves spellcasting for clerics, druids, and rangers.
>
> „

— In-game ability description

Wisdom represents a creature's intuition and awareness of their surroundings. It is the game's recommended primary ability for [clerics](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric") and [druids](/wiki/Druid "Druid"), and serves as the spellcasting ability of clerics, druids, monks, and rangers. Several of a monk's class features benefit from Wisdom.

Wisdom checks
:   Wisdom checks are attempts to discern situations and read between the lines.

Animal Handling
:   [Animal Handling](/wiki/Animal_Handling "Animal Handling") is used when interacting with animals.

Insight
:   [Insight](/wiki/Insight "Insight") is used to read situations and when attempting to get a greater understanding of someone's intentions.

Medicine
:   [Medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") is used during dialogue to heal and aid others, as well as by [Transmutation School](/wiki/Transmutation_School "Transmutation School") wizards to [craft](/wiki/Alchemy "Alchemy") extra materials with the [![Experimental Alchemy](/w/images/thumb/e/e8/Experimental_Alchemy_Icon.webp/20px-Experimental_Alchemy_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Experimental_Alchemy "Experimental Alchemy") [Experimental Alchemy](/wiki/Experimental_Alchemy "Experimental Alchemy") class feature.

Perception
:   [Perception](/wiki/Perception "Perception") is used when a creature attempts to discover hidden or hard to find things in their surroundings, such as hidden containers, mechanisms, or traps, as well as when attempting to spot hidden creatures. During dialogue, it is used to notice hard to spot details.

Survival
:   [Survival](/wiki/Survival "Survival") is used when attempting to spot hidden treasure, as well as during dialogues when dealing with anything related to experience with animals or the wilderness.

#### Wisdom saves

Wisdom saves are some of the most common in the game, frequently made to resist spells that test a creature's willpower, composure, or self-control.

### Charisma

Main article: [Charisma](/wiki/Charisma "Charisma")

>
> „

— In-game ability description

Charisma represents a creature's ability to exert their will when interacting with others, and is the game's recommended primary ability for [bards](/wiki/Bard "Bard"), [sorcerers](/wiki/Sorcerer "Sorcerer"), [warlocks](/wiki/Warlock "Warlock") and [paladins](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"). It is the spellcasting ability of barbarians, bards, paladins, sorcerers, and warlocks.

Charisma checks
:   Charisma checks are attempts at influencing others during dialogue.

Deception
:   [Deception](/wiki/Deception "Deception") is used when attempting to deceive or trick others.

Intimidation
:   [Intimidation](/wiki/Intimidation "Intimidation") is used when attempting to frighten or coerce others.

Performance
:   Proficiency in [Performance](/wiki/Performance "Performance") allows characters to play [instruments](/wiki/Instruments "Instruments"), and the skill is used when attempting to entertain others.

Persuasion

#### Charisma saves

Charisma saves are rare, generally found only on effects which inflict the [![Banished](/w/images/thumb/0/0d/Banished_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Banished_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Banished_(Condition) "Banished (Condition)") [Banished](/wiki/Banished_(Condition) "Banished (Condition)") condition or given as class-based alternatives to sorcerers, warlocks, or bards on Wisdom or Intelligence saves found in dialogue, such as when reading the [![](/w/images/thumb/6/65/Book_Thay_A_Item_Icon.png/20px-Book_Thay_A_Item_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Necromancy_of_Thay "Necromancy of Thay") [Necromancy of Thay](/wiki/Necromancy_of_Thay "Necromancy of Thay").

## Improving or reducing ability scores

There are multiple ways of improving or reducing ability scores, either permanently or temporarily.

### Permanently

Ability scores can be permanently improved with the [Ability Improvement](/wiki/Ability_Improvement "Ability Improvement") feat, though this cannot raise any score above 20.

There are also several ways of [permanently improving](/wiki/Permanent_bonuses "Permanent bonuses") ability scores through [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") and interactions, which can raise ability scores over 20.

### Temporarily

Ability scores may be temporarily increased above 20 through various enhancements, such as magical [spells](/wiki/Spells "Spells"), [elixirs](/wiki/Elixirs "Elixirs"), or special [equipment](/wiki/Equipment "Equipment").

#### Ability score reduction

Some supernatural or magical effects (like [Devour Intellect](/wiki/Devour_Intellect "Devour Intellect")) can temporarily drain abilities, reducing one or more of the target's ability scores, potentially to 0. If any score is reduced to 0, the target [dies instantly](/wiki/Atrophied_(Condition) "Atrophied (Condition)").

### Priority

Ability score bonuses are applied in a three-tier hierarchical order, which can occasionally lead to unexpected interactions between competing modifiers.

The first tier of bonuses includes the *Ability Improvement* feat, which provides 2 ability points, and specific feats: [![Athlete](/w/images/thumb/1/15/Athlete_Standing_Up_Icon.webp/20px-Athlete_Standing_Up_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Athlete:_Standing_Up "Athlete: Standing Up") [Athlete](/wiki/Athlete:_Standing_Up "Athlete: Standing Up"), [![Lightly Armoured](/w/images/thumb/9/92/Lightly_Armoured_Icon.webp/20px-Lightly_Armoured_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Lightly_Armoured "Lightly Armoured") [Lightly Armoured](/wiki/Lightly_Armoured "Lightly Armoured"), [![Moderately Armoured](/w/images/thumb/0/05/Moderately_Armoured_Icon.webp/20px-Moderately_Armoured_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Moderately_Armoured "Moderately Armoured") [Moderately Armoured](/wiki/Moderately_Armoured "Moderately Armoured"), [![Performer](/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Generic_Feature_Icon.webp/20px-Generic_Feature_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Performer "Performer") [Performer](/wiki/Performer "Performer"), [![Tavern Brawler](/w/images/thumb/3/31/Tavern_Brawler_Icon.webp/20px-Tavern_Brawler_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Tavern_Brawler "Tavern Brawler") [Tavern Brawler](/wiki/Tavern_Brawler "Tavern Brawler"), and [![Weapon Master](/w/images/thumb/d/de/Weapon_Master_Icon.webp/20px-Weapon_Master_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Weapon_Master "Weapon Master") [Weapon Master](/wiki/Weapon_Master "Weapon Master"), which provide 1 ability point. Because these are internally categorised as Ability Improvements, they are identified in the ability score tooltip as bonuses "from Class."

These primary bonuses are subject to a hard cap of 20. When selecting one of these feats, the user interface accounts for all other feats selected during levelling, but excludes external modifiers from equipment or permanent world bonuses; if all relevant scores have already reached 20 through other feats, the game prevents the selection of these specific "Ability Improvement" feats.

Following Ability Improvements, the system adds *Uncapped Bonuses*. This second tier of modifiers applies regardless of the current ability score. They have no maximum value indicated in their description or tooltip, and are added to the base score before capped bonuses are considered. The only bonuses in the game which seem to act this way are:

- [![](/w/images/thumb/c/c1/Gloves_Leather_G_Unfaded_Icon.png/25px-Gloves_Leather_G_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Nimblefinger_Gloves "Nimblefinger Gloves") [Nimblefinger Gloves](/wiki/Nimblefinger_Gloves "Nimblefinger Gloves") ([![Nimblefinger](/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Generic_Feature_Icon.webp/25px-Generic_Feature_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Nimblefinger "Nimblefinger") [Nimblefinger](/wiki/Nimblefinger "Nimblefinger"))

Because second-tier values (and first-tier feats) are calculated early in the stack, they provide a foundational increase that later capped bonuses must account for.

The third tier comprises *Capped Bonuses*, which represent the majority of modifiers in the game. It includes all other feats, such as [![Resilient: Intelligence](/w/images/thumb/6/6c/Resilient_Icon.webp/20px-Resilient_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Resilient:_Intelligence "Resilient: Intelligence") [Resilient: Intelligence](/wiki/Resilient:_Intelligence "Resilient: Intelligence") and [![Heavily Armoured](/w/images/thumb/2/2a/Heavily_Armoured_Icon.webp/20px-Heavily_Armoured_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Heavily_Armoured "Heavily Armoured") [Heavily Armoured](/wiki/Heavily_Armoured "Heavily Armoured"), and items like [![](/w/images/thumb/9/9f/The_Graceful_Cloth_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-The_Graceful_Cloth_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/The_Mighty_Cloth "The Mighty Cloth") [The Mighty Cloth](/wiki/The_Mighty_Cloth "The Mighty Cloth") and [![](/w/images/thumb/5/5a/Amulet_Necklace_A_Bronze_A_1_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Amulet_Necklace_A_Bronze_A_1_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Khalid%27s_Gift "Khalid's Gift") [Khalid's Gift](/wiki/Khalid%27s_Gift "Khalid's Gift"). These bonuses only apply if the current total remains below the specific cap assigned to the bonus – typically 20, though [![](/w/images/thumb/e/e3/Birthright_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-Birthright_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Birthright "Birthright") [Birthright](/wiki/Birthright "Birthright") is an exception with its cap of 22. If a bonus provides more than a +1 increase, it may partially apply until the score hits the cap. These are applied in ascending order of their caps to maximise the final value, though existing uncapped bonuses can sometimes result in a capped bonus being ignored if the threshold has already been crossed.

The bonuses granted by the *[Mirror of Loss](/wiki/Mirror_of_Loss "Mirror of Loss")* (including the +1 Charisma from Patriar's Memory) possess the highest functional cap in the game at 24, and are therefore calculated after all other modifiers. This hierarchy establishes 24 as the standard maximum for most ability scores unless a fixed-value modifier, such as the [![](/w/images/thumb/4/40/ELX_Elixir_of_Cloud_Giant_Strength_Unfaded_Icon.png/20px-ELX_Elixir_of_Cloud_Giant_Strength_Unfaded_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Elixir_of_Cloud_Giant_Strength "Elixir of Cloud Giant Strength") [Elixir of Cloud Giant Strength](/wiki/Elixir_of_Cloud_Giant_Strength "Elixir of Cloud Giant Strength"), temporarily overrides the calculation.

However, it is possible for some ability scores to exceed 24 if the final total is derived exclusively from uncapped bonuses without the interference of capped modifiers. Strength can reach 26 early into Act Two with the Potion of Everlasting Vigour (or 28 temporarily with Broken Promises), and it can even reach 29 in the [High Hall](/wiki/High_Hall "High Hall") with Spirit of the Land. Charisma can reach 25 in High Hall, but it can actually reach 27 for one long rest in Act Two via Dark Lady's Grace, which can be obtained in the [Sharran Sanctuary](/wiki/Sharran_Sanctuary "Sharran Sanctuary"). Similarly, Intelligence and Wisdom may temporarily hit 26 using the buffs from the Sharran Sanctuary.
