# The Archfey

**The Archfey** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Warlock#Level_1 "Warlock") of [Warlock](/wiki/Warlock "Warlock"). Graced by a lady or lord of the fey, these warlocks are imbued with all the sumptuous and scary qualities of their patron's extraordinary realm.
