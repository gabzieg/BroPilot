# Bladesinging

**Bladesinger** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Wizard#Level_2 "Wizard") of [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"). It uses an elegant combination of sword and spell to defeat enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 2

![](/w/images/thumb/1/12/Bladesong_Resource_Icon.png/20px-Bladesong_Resource_Icon.png.webp) [Bladesong Power](/wiki/Bladesong_Power "Bladesong Power"): 2
:   Used to cast Bladesong. Replenished on a [Long Rest](/wiki/Long_Rest "Long Rest"). Bladesinging is not possible in Medium or Heavy armor, even if proficient; you will receive the "Bladesong Impeded" Condition while wearing them.

[![Bladesong](/w/images/thumb/8/80/Bladesong_Icon.webp/40px-Bladesong_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Bladesong "Bladesong") [Bladesong](/wiki/Bladesong "Bladesong") (![Bladesong Power](/w/images/thumb/1/12/Bladesong_Resource_Icon.png/20px-Bladesong_Resource_Icon.png.webp))

## Bugs

Due to a coding error, characters who cast a spell that applies a condition (such as [![Guidance](/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Guidance_Icon.webp/20px-Guidance_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Guidance "Guidance") [Guidance](/wiki/Guidance "Guidance")) on a Bladesinger wizard may inadvertently have their casting animations changed to the ones used by – and unique to – Bladesinger wizards. This effect is permanent, and is only removed if the affected character is respecced with [![Withers](/w/images/thumb/2/25/Portrait_Withers.png/25px-Portrait_Withers.png.webp)](/wiki/Withers "Withers") [Withers](/wiki/Withers "Withers").
