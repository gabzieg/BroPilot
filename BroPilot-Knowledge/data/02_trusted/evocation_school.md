# Evocation School

This page is about the Wizard subclass. For a list of all spells of this school, see [Evocation](/wiki/Evocation "Evocation").

**Evocation School** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Wizard#Level_2 "Wizard") of [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"). These Wizards are specialized in offensive magic that deal large damage to enemies and control the battlefield. They also have the ability to protect their allies from harmful Evocation spell effects.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 2

Evocation Savant
:   Halves the cost to learn [Evocation spells](/wiki/Evocation_spells "Evocation spells") from [Scrolls](/wiki/Scrolls "Scrolls"). Learning these spells will only cost **![25 gp](/w/images/thumb/f/f5/Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png/23px-Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png.webp) 25 gp** per spell level.

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

[![Sculpt Spells](/w/images/thumb/9/97/Sculpt_Spells_Icon.webp/40px-Sculpt_Spells_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Sculpt_Spells "Sculpt Spells")[Sculpt Spells](/wiki/Sculpt_Spells "Sculpt Spells")
:   Create pockets of safety within your [Evocation spells](/wiki/Evocation "Evocation"). Allies automatically succeed their ![Saving Throw](/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Saving_Throw_Icon.png/25px-Saving_Throw_Icon.png.webp) [Saving Throws](/wiki/Saving_Throw "Saving Throw") against these spells and take no damage from them.

### Level 6

[![Potent Cantrip](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Potent_Cantrip_Icon.webp/40px-Potent_Cantrip_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Potent_Cantrip "Potent Cantrip")[Potent Cantrip](/wiki/Potent_Cantrip "Potent Cantrip")
:   Your [cantrips](/wiki/Cantrip "Cantrip") become harder to evade entirely.
    When a creature succeeds its ![Saving Throw](/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Saving_Throw_Icon.png/25px-Saving_Throw_Icon.png.webp) [Saving Throw](/wiki/Saving_Throw "Saving Throw") against one of your cantrips, it still takes half the cantrip's damage, but suffers no additional effects.

### Level 10

[![Empowered Evocation](/w/images/thumb/d/d0/Empowered_Evocation_Icon.webp/40px-Empowered_Evocation_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Empowered_Evocation "Empowered Evocation")[Empowered Evocation](/wiki/Empowered_Evocation "Empowered Evocation")
:   Your grasp of Evocation magic has tightened, and you can add your Intelligence [Modifier](/wiki/Ability_Score_Modifier "Ability Score Modifier") to damage rolls with any Evocation spells.
