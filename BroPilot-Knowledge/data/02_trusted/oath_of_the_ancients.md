# Oath Of The Ancients

**Oath of the Ancients** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Paladin#Level_1 "Paladin") of [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"). These Paladins have a deep connection with the natural world and the light within it.

### Level 1

[![Oath of the Ancients Tenets](/w/images/thumb/1/14/Divine_Intervention_Arm_Thy_Servant_Icon.webp/40px-Divine_Intervention_Arm_Thy_Servant_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Oath_of_the_Ancients_Tenets "Oath of the Ancients Tenets")[Oath of the Ancients Tenets](/wiki/Oath_of_the_Ancients_Tenets "Oath of the Ancients Tenets")
:   Oath of the Ancients paladins abide by the following tenets:
    > **Kindle the Light.** Through your acts of mercy, kindness, and forgiveness, kindle the light of hope in the world, beating back despair.
    >
    > **Shelter the Light.** Where there is good, beauty, love, and laughter in the world, stand against the wickedness that would swallow it. Where life flourishes, stand against the forces that would render it barren.
    >
    > **Preserve Your Own Light.** Delight in song and laughter, in beauty and art. If you allow the light to die in your own heart, you can’t preserve it in the world.
    >
    > **Be the Light.** Be a glorious beacon for all who live in despair. Let the light of your joy and courage shine forth in all your deeds.

## Oathbreaking actions

This section details known actions which cause Paladins to break their Oath of the Ancients.

### Global

- Killing someone non-hostile, even by "accident" (e.g., an NPC steps into an area-of-effect spell, such as Moonbeam or Spike Growth, and dies). This includes the petrified drow in the underdark
- Actions that guards do not like, or add the [Witness](/wiki/Witness_(Condition) "Witness (Condition)") status, appear to cause a broken Oath; even attacking Duergar in [Grymforge](/wiki/Grymforge "Grymforge") after offering to free the deep gnomes has caused this.
  - Starting combat via conversational choices rather than attacking directly often prevents this from breaking the oath
- Shoving someone to their death also counts as breaking the Oath. This sometimes also includes evil characters, such as Assassins of Bhaal in [Orin](/wiki/Orin "Orin")'s arena.
