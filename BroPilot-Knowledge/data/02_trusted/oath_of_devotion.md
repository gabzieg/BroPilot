# Oath Of Devotion

**Oath of Devotion** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Paladin#Level_1 "Paladin") of [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"). These Paladins gain abilities that aid them in protecting the weak, dispelling falsehoods, and defeating fiends and undead.

![](/w/images/thumb/e/e6/Class_Paladin_Devotion_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Paladin_Devotion_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> Following the ideal of the knight in shining armour, you act with honour and virtue to protect the weak and pursue the greater good.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 1

[![Oath of Devotion Tenets](/w/images/thumb/1/14/Divine_Intervention_Arm_Thy_Servant_Icon.webp/40px-Divine_Intervention_Arm_Thy_Servant_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Oath_of_Devotion_Tenets "Oath of Devotion Tenets")[Oath of Devotion Tenets](/wiki/Oath_of_Devotion_Tenets "Oath of Devotion Tenets")
:   Oath of Devotion paladins abide by the following tenets:
    > **Courage.** Stride dauntlessly into action.
    >
    > **Compassion.** Show clemency when prudent, and lend your arm to those in need.
    >
    > **Duty.** Tend your responsibilities, obey just laws, and support those entrusted to your care.

[![Holy Rebuke](/w/images/thumb/c/c4/Holy_Rebuke_Icon.webp/40px-Holy_Rebuke_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Holy_Rebuke "Holy Rebuke") [Holy Rebuke](/wiki/Holy_Rebuke "Holy Rebuke") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Channel Oath Charge](/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Channel_Oath_Icon.png/20px-Channel_Oath_Icon.png.webp))
:   Call upon your Oath to grant a vengeful aura that deals 1d4[![Radiant](/w/images/thumb/7/78/Radiant_Damage_Icon.png/25px-Radiant_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Radiant "Radiant")[Radiant](/wiki/Radiant "Radiant") damage to anyone who hits them with a melee attack.

## Oathbreaking actions

This section details known actions which cause Paladins to break their Oath of Devotion.

### Act One

[Roadside Cliffs](/wiki/Roadside_Cliffs "Roadside Cliffs")

- Attacking [Damays](/wiki/Damays "Damays") and [Nymessa](/wiki/Nymessa "Nymessa") (tieflings who guard trapped [Lae'zel](/wiki/Lae%27zel "Lae'zel")) by any means, including options that occur through the dialogue
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), chopping off [Gale](/wiki/Gale "Gale")'s hand at the point of his possible recruitment

[Emerald Grove](/wiki/Emerald_Grove "Emerald Grove")/[The Hollow](/wiki/The_Hollow "The Hollow")

- When talking to [Zevlor](/wiki/Zevlor "Zevlor"), choosing dialogue options that imply the player character will kill the goblin leaders because [Kagha](/wiki/Kagha "Kagha") asked them to
  - OR Telling Zevlor that the player character is here to make sure the tieflings leave
- When the [Shadow Druids](/wiki/Shadow_Druids "Shadow Druids") reveal themselves, telling Kagha the player character won't stand in her way
- Prodding the wing of the [Exhausted Bird](/wiki/Exhausted_Bird "Exhausted Bird"). As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), the option is "Tear her wings off".
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), break [Pandirna](/wiki/Pandirna "Pandirna")'s legs

[Sunlit Wetlands](/wiki/Sunlit_Wetlands "Sunlit Wetlands")/[Putrid Bog](/wiki/Putrid_Bog "Putrid Bog")

- Succumbing to [Auntie Ethel](/wiki/Auntie_Ethel "Auntie Ethel")'s will while wearing a [Whispering Mask](/wiki/Whispering_Mask "Whispering Mask")

[Goblin Camp](/wiki/Goblin_Camp "Goblin Camp")

- In [Shattered Sanctum](/wiki/Shattered_Sanctum "Shattered Sanctum"), repeatedly stabbing [Abdirak](/wiki/Abdirak "Abdirak") while he refuses to fight back.
- Torturing [Liam](/wiki/Liam "Liam")
- Revealing the location of the [Emerald Grove](/wiki/Emerald_Grove "Emerald Grove") to [Minthara](/wiki/Minthara "Minthara"), either showing her or allowing her to read your thoughts

[Risen Road](/wiki/Risen_Road "Risen Road")

- Killing [Anders](/wiki/Anders "Anders") without confirming first that he is hunting [Karlach](/wiki/Karlach "Karlach") on the order of [Zariel](/wiki/Zariel "Zariel")
- Killing any of the Zhentarim in the [Zhentarim Basement](/wiki/Zhentarim_Basement "Zhentarim Basement"), if [Zarys](/wiki/Zarys "Zarys") has not tortured [Rugan](/wiki/Rugan "Rugan") or if it was not revealed that the [Iron Flask](/wiki/Iron_Flask "Iron Flask") was opened.

[Rosymorn Monastery](/wiki/Rosymorn_Monastery "Rosymorn Monastery")/[Creche Y'llek](/wiki/Creche_Y%27llek "Creche Y'llek")

- Killing any Githyanki in the [Infirmary](/wiki/Cr%C3%A8che_Y%27llek#Infirmary_and_Dormitory "Crèche Y'llek") without being provoked
- Killing [Varrl](/wiki/Varrl "Varrl") when he refuses to spar with the player character, or failing the roll to stab him non-fatally
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), kicking the [Githyanki Egg](/wiki/Githyanki_Egg "Githyanki Egg") off the cliffside when talking to [Lady Esther](/wiki/Lady_Esther "Lady Esther")

[Underdark](/wiki/Underdark "Underdark")

- Allowing [Nere](/wiki/Nere "Nere") to kill the enslaved deep gnomes after he is freed from the cave-in
- Siding with Nere against [Elder Brithvar](/wiki/Brithvar "Brithvar") and duergar rebels
- In [Ebonlake Grotto](/wiki/Ebonlake_Grotto "Ebonlake Grotto"), siding with [Sovereign Glut](/wiki/Glut "Glut") and helping it defeat [Sovereign Spaw](/wiki/Spaw "Spaw")
- Telling the duergar you will help them, then attacking them
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), throwing [Baelen](/wiki/Baelen "Baelen")'s bag in the midst of spores in the bibberbang field

[Camp](/wiki/Camp "Camp")

- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), after killing [Alfira](/wiki/Alfira "Alfira"), choosing to "admire this beautiful death"

### Act Two

[Shadow-Cursed Lands](/wiki/Shadow-Cursed_Lands "Shadow-Cursed Lands")

- During the [Punish the Wicked](/wiki/Punish_the_Wicked "Punish the Wicked") quest, ordering [Madeline](/wiki/Madeline "Madeline") to stab herself, or calling her a coward and denying her forgiveness
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), crushing [Dolly Thrice](/wiki/Dolly_Thrice "Dolly Thrice") after releasing her from the [Moonlantern](/wiki/Moonlantern "Moonlantern")

[Last Light Inn](/wiki/Last_Light_Inn "Last Light Inn")

- Encouraging [Mol](/wiki/Mol "Mol") to sign a contract with [Raphael](/wiki/Raphael "Raphael"), or praising her ambitiousness

- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), revealing the player character's depravity to the [Strange Ox](/wiki/Strange_Ox "Strange Ox")

[Moonrise Towers](/wiki/Moonrise_Towers "Moonrise Towers")

- Leting Questioners [Jasin](/wiki/Jasin "Jasin") and [Sumera](/wiki/Sumera "Sumera") wipe [Minthara](/wiki/Minthara "Minthara")'s mind
  - OR Taking part in the execution and erasing her (as [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), the "Make her NOTHING" option)
- Convincing [Acolyte Marls](/wiki/Marls "Marls") to jump from the pier
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), speaking with [Steelclaw](/wiki/Steelclaw "Steelclaw") and trying to remember what the player character did previously
- Killing the Warden unprovoked.

[Gauntlet of Shar](/wiki/Gauntlet_of_Shar "Gauntlet of Shar")

- Offering [Yurgir](/wiki/Yurgir "Yurgir") help to complete his contract with [Raphael](/wiki/Raphael "Raphael")
- As [Minthara](/wiki/Minthara "Minthara"), attacking [Balthazar](/wiki/Balthazar "Balthazar") and [Flesh](/wiki/Flesh "Flesh") unprovoked.
- In the [Shadowfell](/wiki/Shadowfell "Shadowfell"), letting [Balthazar](/wiki/Balthazar "Balthazar") take [Aylin](/wiki/Aylin "Aylin") to deliver her to [Ketheric Thorm](/wiki/Ketheric_Thorm "Ketheric Thorm")
  - OR Attacking and kill Aylin

[Mind Flayer Colony](/wiki/Mind_Flayer_Colony "Mind Flayer Colony")

- Killing the prisoners in the pods in the [Tadpoling Centre](/wiki/Tadpoling_Centre "Tadpoling Centre") rather than releasing them

[Camp](/wiki/Camp "Camp")

- Lying to [Arabella](/wiki/Arabella "Arabella") about not yet having found out what happened to her parents

### Act Three

[Rivington](/wiki/Rivington "Rivington")

- Trying to intimidate [Ben Golewits](/wiki/Ben_Golewits "Ben Golewits") and his family into leaving [Arfur Gregorio](/wiki/Arfur_Gregorio "Arfur Gregorio")'s mansion (regardless of roll result)
- Providing the newborn mind flayer in the [Abandoned Windmill](/wiki/Abandoned_Windmill "Abandoned Windmill") basement with a dead body to feed
- At the smugglers' cave, siding with [Farlin](/wiki/Farlin "Farlin") against [Caïros](/wiki/Ca%C3%AFros "Caïros")
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), revealing the player character's depravity to the [Strange Ox](/wiki/Strange_Ox "Strange Ox")

[Wyrm's Crossing](/wiki/Wyrm%27s_Crossing "Wyrm's Crossing")/[Wyrm's Rock Fortress](/wiki/Wyrm%27s_Rock_Fortress "Wyrm's Rock Fortress")

- Agreeing to take gold from Arfur Gregorio to 'forget' about rigged toys he donated for refugees
- Accepting [Gortash](/wiki/Gortash "Gortash")'s proposal of alliance at the ordaining ceremony
- Bringing [Wulbren](/wiki/Wulbren "Wulbren")'s head to [Manip Falcäo](/wiki/Manip_Falc%C3%A4o "Manip Falcäo")
- Killing the jailers in [Wyrm's Rock Prison](/wiki/Wyrm%27s_Rock_Prison "Wyrm's Rock Prison") if not having destroyed the [Steel Watch Foundry](/wiki/Steel_Watch_Foundry "Steel Watch Foundry") or not having made Gortash's forces hostile through other means

[Lower City](/wiki/Lower_City "Lower City")

- In front of [Sorcerous Sundries](/wiki/Sorcerous_Sundries "Sorcerous Sundries"), inviting [Aradin](/wiki/Aradin_Beno "Aradin Beno") to try and kidnap [Aylin](/wiki/Aylin "Aylin") from the camp.
- At Sorcerous Sundries, letting [Lorroakan](/wiki/Lorroakan "Lorroakan") capture Aylin OR helping him do it
- At the [Abandoned Cistern](/wiki/Abandoned_Cistern "Abandoned Cistern"), allowing the [Emperor](/wiki/Emperor "Emperor") to kill [Minsc](/wiki/Minsc "Minsc"), OR attack and kill Minsc directly
- Placing the [Runepowder Bomb](/wiki/Runepowder_Bomb "Runepowder Bomb") into the Neurocitor in order to blow up the [Steel Watch Foundry](/wiki/Steel_Watch_Foundry "Steel Watch Foundry")
- Letting [Churg Elvek](/wiki/Churg_Elvek "Churg Elvek") and the crowd execute [Volo](/wiki/Volo "Volo")
- Allowing [Astarion](/wiki/Astarion "Astarion") to complete the [Rite of Profane Ascension](/wiki/The_Pale_Elf#Outcomes "The Pale Elf")
  - OR, Convincing him not to ascend, but then deciding to kill the imprisoned spawn
- Becoming an Unholy Assassin of [Bhaal](/wiki/Bhaal "Bhaal")
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), at the [Graveyard](/wiki/Graveyard "Graveyard") burying [Bugthimble](/wiki/Bugthimble "Bugthimble") alive
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), accepting [Bhaal](/wiki/Bhaal "Bhaal")'s gift and blessings
