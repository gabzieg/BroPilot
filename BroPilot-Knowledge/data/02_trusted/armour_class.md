# Armour Class

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | This topic has a [community guide](/wiki/Guide:Armour_Class "Guide:Armour Class"). |

**Armour Class** (**AC**) is a measurement of how difficult a creature is to be hit by an attack. In order to successfully hit a creature, the results of an [attack roll](/wiki/Attack_roll "Attack roll") must have a number equal to or greater than the target's Armour Class. AC can be increased by equipping [armour](/wiki/Armour "Armour") and [shields](/wiki/Shields "Shields"), by selecting certain [feats](/wiki/Feats "Feats") when leveling up, or utilizing certain [spells](/wiki/Spells "Spells").

## Formulas

The default formula that determines AC is:

:   10 + [Dexterity modifier](/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") + armour bonus + shield bonus + other bonuses and penalties

[Medium](/wiki/Armour#Medium_armour "Armour") armour caps the Dexterity Modifier to +2. [Heavy](/wiki/Armour#Heavy_armour "Armour") armour ignores the modifier entirely.

Shields will grant the listed AC bonus to whoever equips them, regardless of which weapon slot they are currently using.

### Other formulas

Unarmoured creatures may use one of the following different formulas if they have certain features. Creatures always use whichever formula would result in a higher AC among those they have access to.

[![Mage Armour](/w/images/thumb/0/0d/Mage_Armour_Icon.webp/25px-Mage_Armour_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Mage_Armour "Mage Armour") [Mage Armour](/wiki/Mage_Armour "Mage Armour") or [![Draconic Resilience](/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Draconic_Resilience_Icon.webp/25px-Draconic_Resilience_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Draconic_Resilience "Draconic Resilience") [Draconic Resilience](/wiki/Draconic_Resilience "Draconic Resilience"):

:   13 + Dexterity modifier + shield bonus + other bonuses and penalties

[![Unarmoured Defence (Barbarian)](/w/images/thumb/2/27/Unarmoured_Defence_Barbarian_Icon.webp/25px-Unarmoured_Defence_Barbarian_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Unarmoured_Defence_(Barbarian) "Unarmoured Defence (Barbarian)") [Unarmoured Defence (Barbarian)](/wiki/Unarmoured_Defence_(Barbarian) "Unarmoured Defence (Barbarian)"):

:   10 + Constitution modifier + Dexterity modifier + shield bonus + other bonuses and penalties

[![Unarmoured Defence (Monk)](/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Unarmoured_Defence_Monk_Icon.webp/25px-Unarmoured_Defence_Monk_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Unarmoured_Defence_(Monk) "Unarmoured Defence (Monk)") [Unarmoured Defence (Monk)](/wiki/Unarmoured_Defence_(Monk) "Unarmoured Defence (Monk)"):

:   10 + Wisdom modifier + Dexterity modifier + other bonuses and penalties

## Mathematics

Armour Class becomes more useful the greater it is; for instance, the difference in effectiveness between 20 and 19 AC is *greater* than the difference in effectiveness between 16 and 15.

To illustrate this, if a defender has 15 AC and 10 HP, and the attacker has +4 (50% chance to hit) to attack rolls, and deals 2 damage per attack, the defender would on average survive for 10 turns.

If the defender's AC was increased to 16 (45% chance to be hit), they would instead survive for an average of 11.1 rounds (an 11% increase in effectiveness).

Meanwhile, if the defender has 19 AC (30% chance to be hit), they would survive for an average of 16.66 rounds. If their AC was increased to 20 (25% chance to be hit), they would be able to survive for an average of 20 rounds (a 20% increase in effectiveness).

The difference between 25 and 24 is even greater, with a *100%* increase in effectiveness (50 vs 100 rounds).
