# Oath Of The Crown

**Oath of the Crown** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Paladin#Level_1 "Paladin") of [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"). It focuses on protecting allies by healing them and redirecting attacks. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin "Paladin"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

Paladin's default armour

### Level 1

A paladin must follow their sworn oaths or lose their divine power. For more details, see [Oaths and Oathbreaking](/wiki/Paladin#Oaths_and_Oathbreaking "Paladin").

[![Oath of the Crown Tenets](/w/images/thumb/1/14/Divine_Intervention_Arm_Thy_Servant_Icon.webp/40px-Divine_Intervention_Arm_Thy_Servant_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Oath_of_the_Crown_Tenets "Oath of the Crown Tenets")[Oath of the Crown Tenets](/wiki/Oath_of_the_Crown_Tenets "Oath of the Crown Tenets")
:   Oath of the Crown paladins abide by the following tenets:
    > **Law.** Uphold civilisation and order.
    >
    > **Loyalty.** Your word is your bond; without loyalty, oaths and laws are meaningless.
    >
    > **Responsibility.** Deal with the consequences of your actions, and fulfil your duties and obligations.

[![Righteous Clarity](/w/images/thumb/5/57/Righteous_Clarity_Icon.webp/40px-Righteous_Clarity_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Righteous_Clarity "Righteous Clarity") [Righteous Clarity](/wiki/Righteous_Clarity "Righteous Clarity") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Channel Oath Charge](/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Channel_Oath_Icon.png/20px-Channel_Oath_Icon.png.webp))
:   Target yourself or an ally to additionally grant the target's [proficiency bonus](/wiki/Proficiency_bonus "Proficiency bonus") to their [Attack Rolls](/wiki/Attack_Rolls "Attack Rolls").

## Oathbreaking actions

This section details known actions which cause Paladins to break their Oath of the Crown.

### Global

- Evading being arrested once caught or refusing to be interrogated
- Making promises and then failing to keep them

### Act One

[Roadside Cliffs](/wiki/Roadside_Cliffs "Roadside Cliffs")

- Attacking [Damays](/wiki/Damays "Damays") and [Nymessa](/wiki/Nymessa "Nymessa") (tieflings who guard trapped [Lae'zel](/wiki/Lae%27zel "Lae'zel")) by any means, including options that occur through the dialogue
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), chopping off [Gale's](/wiki/Gale "Gale") hand at the point of his possible recruitment

[Emerald Grove](/wiki/Emerald_Grove "Emerald Grove")/[The Hollow](/wiki/The_Hollow "The Hollow")

- Letting [Arka](/wiki/Arka "Arka") shoot [Sazza](/wiki/Sazza "Sazza") (while in dialogue)
  - Alternatively, helping Sazza escape from the Makeshift prison
- Deceiving [Topaz](/wiki/Topaz_(Blue_Jay) "Topaz (Blue Jay)") in the [Sacred Pool](/wiki/Sacred_Pool "Sacred Pool") to make him throw the coin out of nest
- When the [Shadow Druids](/wiki/Shadow_Druids "Shadow Druids") reveal themselves, telling Kagha the player character won't stand in her way
- Prodding the wing of the [Exhausted Bird](/wiki/Exhausted_Bird "Exhausted Bird"). As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), the option is *"Tear her wings off"*.
- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), break [Pandirna's](/wiki/Pandirna "Pandirna") legs
- Betraying the tieflings and siding with [Minthara](/wiki/Minthara "Minthara"), when goblins raid the grove
  - OR Betraying Minthara, after promising to help her to assault the grove

[Sunlit Wetlands](/wiki/Sunlit_Wetlands "Sunlit Wetlands")/[Putrid Bog](/wiki/Putrid_Bog "Putrid Bog")

- Making a deal with [Auntie Ethel](/wiki/Auntie_Ethel "Auntie Ethel") to let her escape

[Goblin Camp](/wiki/Goblin_Camp "Goblin Camp")

- Torturing [Liam](/wiki/Liam "Liam") in [Shattered Sanctum](/wiki/Shattered_Sanctum "Shattered Sanctum")

[Risen Road](/wiki/Risen_Road "Risen Road")

- In [Waukeen's Rest](/wiki/Waukeen%27s_Rest "Waukeen's Rest"), declining to help or leaving the conversation when [Counsellor Florrick](/wiki/Florrick "Florrick") asks for help finding [Ulder Ravengard](/wiki/Ulder_Ravengard "Ulder Ravengard")
- Killing the Zhentarim in the [Zhentarim Basement](/wiki/Zhentarim_Basement "Zhentarim Basement") after completing the [Find the Missing Shipment](/wiki/Find_the_Missing_Shipment "Find the Missing Shipment") quest

[Rosymorn Monastery](/wiki/Rosymorn_Monastery "Rosymorn Monastery")/[Creche Y'llek](/wiki/Creche_Y%27llek "Creche Y'llek")

- Killing [Varrl](/wiki/Varrl "Varrl") when he refuses to spar with the player character, or failing the roll to stab him non-fatally
- Stealing the [Githyanki Egg](/wiki/Githyanki_Egg "Githyanki Egg") instead of persuading [Varsh Ko'kuu](/wiki/Ko%27kuu "Ko'kuu") to give it to you

[Underdark](/wiki/Underdark "Underdark")

- In [Ebonlake Grotto](/wiki/Ebonlake_Grotto "Ebonlake Grotto"), siding with [Sovereign Glut](/wiki/Glut "Glut") and helping it defeat [Sovereign Spaw](/wiki/Spaw "Spaw")

[Campsite](/wiki/Campsite "Campsite")

- As [The Dark Urge](/wiki/The_Dark_Urge "The Dark Urge"), hide [Alfira's](/wiki/Alfira "Alfira") body after killing her

[Gauntlet of Shar](/wiki/Gauntlet_of_Shar "Gauntlet of Shar")

- In the [Shadowfell](/wiki/Shadowfell "Shadowfell"), killing or freeing [Aylin](/wiki/Aylin "Aylin") if previously agreed to deliver her to Z'rell
