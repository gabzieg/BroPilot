# Battle Master

**Battle Master** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Fighter#Level_3 "Fighter") of [Fighter](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter"). They focus on using active skills on a limited resource to hinder and disable opponents as well as sometimes supporting allies.
