# Arcane Trickster

**Arcane Trickster** is a [subclass](/wiki/Rogue#Level_3 "Rogue") of [Rogue](/wiki/Rogue "Rogue"). These Rogues are capable of [spellcasting](/wiki/Spells "Spells") (from the [Wizard spell list](/wiki/List_of_Wizard_spells "List of Wizard spells")) and use their spells to attack enemies or support allies in combat. Arcane Tricksters use [Intelligence](/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") as their spellcasting modifier.

## Notes

- Multiclassing prior to level 10 results in standard [spellcaster](/wiki/Spellcaster "Spellcaster") progression with four Level 1 Spell Slots and three Level 2 Spell Slots, while multiclassing after level 10 results in retaining altered spellcaster progression with six Level 1 Spell Slots and two Level 2 Spell Slots. This is true even if the additional class is not a spellcaster.
