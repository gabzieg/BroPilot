# Necromancy School

This page is about the Wizard subclass. For a list of all spells of this school, see [Necromancy](/wiki/Necromancy "Necromancy").

**Necromancy School** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Wizard#Level_2 "Wizard") of [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"). These Wizards are specialized in powers of death, allowing them to protect themselves from harm and raise undead minions to serve them.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard "Wizard"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 2

Necromancy Savant
:   Halves the cost to learn Necromancy spells from [Scrolls](/wiki/Scrolls "Scrolls"). Learning these spells will only cost **![25 gp](/w/images/thumb/f/f5/Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png/23px-Gold_Pile_Single_Item_Image.png.webp) 25 gp** per spell level.

[![Grim Harvest](/w/images/thumb/5/59/Grim_Harvest_Icon.webp/40px-Grim_Harvest_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Grim_Harvest "Grim Harvest")[Grim Harvest](/wiki/Grim_Harvest "Grim Harvest")
:   Once per turn, if you kill a creature with a spell, you regain [Hit Points](/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to twice the [Spell Slot](/wiki/Spells#Spell_Slots "Spells") level used - thrice if it's a Necromancy spell. [Undead](/wiki/Undead "Undead") and [Constructs](/wiki/Constructs "Constructs") are unaffected.

### Level 6

[![Undead Thralls: Animate Dead](/w/images/thumb/1/11/Undead_Thralls_Animate_Dead_Icon.webp/40px-Undead_Thralls_Animate_Dead_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Undead_Thralls:_Animate_Dead "Undead Thralls: Animate Dead")[Undead Thralls: Animate Dead](/wiki/Undead_Thralls:_Animate_Dead "Undead Thralls: Animate Dead")
:   You gain the [![Animate Dead](/w/images/thumb/2/2f/Animate_Dead_Icon.webp/25px-Animate_Dead_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Animate_Dead "Animate Dead") [Animate Dead](/wiki/Animate_Dead "Animate Dead") spell, if you didn't have it already

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

[![Undead Thralls: Additional Undead](/w/images/thumb/0/0a/Undead_Thralls_Additional_Undead_Icon.webp/40px-Undead_Thralls_Additional_Undead_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Undead_Thralls:_Additional_Undead "Undead Thralls: Additional Undead")[Undead Thralls: Additional Undead](/wiki/Undead_Thralls:_Additional_Undead "Undead Thralls: Additional Undead")
:   When you use [![Animate Dead](/w/images/thumb/2/2f/Animate_Dead_Icon.webp/25px-Animate_Dead_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Animate_Dead "Animate Dead") [Animate Dead](/wiki/Animate_Dead "Animate Dead"), you can raise an additional corpse.

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

[![Undead Thralls: Better Summons](/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Undead_Thralls_Better_Summons_Icon.webp/40px-Undead_Thralls_Better_Summons_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Undead_Thralls:_Better_Summons "Undead Thralls: Better Summons")[Undead Thralls: Better Summons](/wiki/Undead_Thralls:_Better_Summons "Undead Thralls: Better Summons")
:   Creatures created with [![Animate Dead](/w/images/thumb/2/2f/Animate_Dead_Icon.webp/20px-Animate_Dead_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Animate_Dead "Animate Dead") [Animate Dead](/wiki/Animate_Dead "Animate Dead") have additional hit points equal to your wizard level, and your [Proficiency Bonus](/wiki/Proficiency_Bonus "Proficiency Bonus") is added to their damage.

### Level 10

[![Inured to Undeath](/w/images/thumb/c/c0/Inured_to_Undeath_Icon.webp/40px-Inured_to_Undeath_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Inured_to_Undeath "Inured to Undeath")[Inured to Undeath](/wiki/Inured_to_Undeath "Inured to Undeath")
:   You have steeped yourself so completely in death that you are [Resistant](/wiki/Resistant "Resistant") to Necrotic damage, and moreover your [hit point](/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") maximum cannot be reduced.
