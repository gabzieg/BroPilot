# Champion

**Champion** is a [subclass](/wiki/Fighter#Level_3 "Fighter") of [Fighter](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter") that focuses entirely on passive improvements.

![](/w/images/thumb/1/16/Class_Fighter_Champion_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Fighter_Champion_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> You approach the complex problems posed by combat with one distinctly effective solution - you hit those problems, really quite hard.
>
> „

## Subclass features

[![Fighter's default armour](/w/images/thumb/0/01/Default_Fighter_Armor.png/230px-Default_Fighter_Armor.png.webp)](/wiki/File:Default_Fighter_Armor.png "Fighter's default armour")

Fighter's default armour

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Fighter](/wiki/Fighter "Fighter"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 3

[![Improved Critical Hit](/w/images/thumb/8/83/Improved_Critical_Hit_Icon.webp/40px-Improved_Critical_Hit_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Improved_Critical_Hit "Improved Critical Hit")[Improved Critical Hit](/wiki/Improved_Critical_Hit "Improved Critical Hit")
:   The number you need to roll a [Critical Hit](/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") while attacking is reduced by 1. This effect can stack.

### Level 7

[![Remarkable Athlete: Jump](/w/images/thumb/2/2b/Remarkable_Athlete_Jump_Icon.webp/40px-Remarkable_Athlete_Jump_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Remarkable_Athlete:_Jump "Remarkable Athlete: Jump")[Remarkable Athlete: Jump](/wiki/Remarkable_Athlete:_Jump "Remarkable Athlete: Jump")
:   You're a master of your own body, your athletic prowess extended beyond the usual. Your Jump distance is increased by 3 m (10 ft).

![](/w/images/b/be/Bg3_content_hr.png)

[![Remarkable Athlete: Proficiency](/w/images/thumb/2/29/Remarkable_Athlete_Proficiency_Icon.webp/40px-Remarkable_Athlete_Proficiency_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Remarkable_Athlete:_Proficiency "Remarkable Athlete: Proficiency")[Remarkable Athlete: Proficiency](/wiki/Remarkable_Athlete:_Proficiency "Remarkable Athlete: Proficiency")
:   You're a master of your own body, your athletic prowess extended beyond the usual. You can add half of your [Proficiency Bonus](/wiki/Proficiency_Bonus "Proficiency Bonus") to any Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution [Checks](/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check") that you are not [Proficient](/wiki/Proficiency "Proficiency") in.

### Level 10

Fighting Style
:   Choose 1 additional [fighting style](/wiki/Fighting_style "Fighting style")
:   [![Archery](/w/images/thumb/8/83/Archery_Icon.webp/30px-Archery_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Archery "Archery")[Archery](/wiki/Archery "Archery")
    :   You gain a +2 bonus to ![Attack Roll](/w/images/thumb/b/ba/Attack_Roll_Icon.png/25px-Attack_Roll_Icon.png.webp) [Attack Rolls](/wiki/Attack_Roll "Attack Roll") you make with ranged [Weapons](/wiki/Weapons "Weapons").

    [![Defence](/w/images/thumb/e/e2/Defence_Icon.webp/30px-Defence_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Defence "Defence")[Defence](/wiki/Defence "Defence")
    :   You gain a +1 bonus to [![Armour Class](/w/images/thumb/a/ae/Armour_Class_Icon.png/25px-Armour_Class_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Armour_Class "Armour Class") [Armour Class](/wiki/Armour_Class "Armour Class") while wearing [armour](/wiki/Armour "Armour").

    [![Duelling](/w/images/thumb/a/ae/Duelling_Icon.webp/30px-Duelling_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Duelling "Duelling")[Duelling](/wiki/Duelling "Duelling")
    :   When you are wielding a melee weapon that is not Two-Handed in one hand, and no weapon in the other, you deal an additional 2 damage with that weapon.

    [![Great Weapon Fighting](/w/images/thumb/1/1f/Great_Weapon_Fighting_Icon.webp/30px-Great_Weapon_Fighting_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Great_Weapon_Fighting "Great Weapon Fighting")[Great Weapon Fighting](/wiki/Great_Weapon_Fighting "Great Weapon Fighting")
    :   When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack with a [Two-Handed](/wiki/Two-Handed "Two-Handed") melee weapon, that die is rerolled once.

    [![Protection](/w/images/thumb/7/73/Protection_Icon.webp/30px-Protection_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Protection "Protection")[Protection](/wiki/Protection "Protection")
    :   When you have a [Shield](/wiki/Shields "Shields"), impose [![Disadvantage](/w/images/thumb/b/b9/Disadvantage_Icon.png/27px-Disadvantage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Disadvantage "Disadvantage") [Disadvantage](/wiki/Disadvantage "Disadvantage") on an enemy who attacks one of your allies when you are within 1.5 m (5 ft). You must be able to see the enemy.

    [![Two-Weapon Fighting](/w/images/thumb/5/54/Two-Weapon_Fighting_Icon.webp/30px-Two-Weapon_Fighting_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Two-Weapon_Fighting "Two-Weapon Fighting")[Two-Weapon Fighting](/wiki/Two-Weapon_Fighting "Two-Weapon Fighting")
    :   When you make an offhand attack, you can add your [Ability Score Modifier](/wiki/Ability_Score_Modifier "Ability Score Modifier") to the damage of the attack.
