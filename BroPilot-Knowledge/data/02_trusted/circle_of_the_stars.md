# Circle Of The Stars

**Circle of Stars** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Druid#Level_3 "Druid") of [Druid](/wiki/Druid "Druid"). It can choose to transform into special Starry Forms instead of beast forms which grant unique abilities. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

![](/w/images/thumb/5/54/Class_Druid_Stars_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Druid_Stars_Badge_Icon.png.webp)

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Druid](/wiki/Druid "Druid"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*
