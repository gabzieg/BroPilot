# Nature Domain

**Nature Domain** is one of the [Subclasses](/wiki/Cleric#Level_1 "Cleric") of [Cleric](/wiki/Cleric "Cleric").

The player-selectable [deities](/wiki/Deities "Deities") directly associated with this domain are [![Eilistraee](/w/images/thumb/e/e2/Eilistraee_Icon.webp/14px-Eilistraee_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Eilistraee "Eilistraee") [Eilistraee](/wiki/Eilistraee "Eilistraee") and [![Mielikki](/w/images/thumb/4/4b/Mielikki_Icon.webp/20px-Mielikki_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Mielikki "Mielikki") [Mielikki](/wiki/Mielikki "Mielikki").

![](/w/images/thumb/3/3c/Class_Cleric_Nature_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Cleric_Nature_Badge_Icon.png.webp)

>
> You embody the vast viridian power of the natural world, an avatar of the subtle divinity of fruitfall, avian migration, woodland silence, and the landslide's roaring fury.
>
> „
