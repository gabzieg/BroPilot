# Circle Of The Land

**Circle of the Land** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Druid#Subclass "Druid") of [Druid](/wiki/Druid "Druid"). These Druids are more specialized in [spellcasting](/wiki/Spells "Spells") to attack enemies or support allies in combat. They also have the ability to recover a certain amount of [Spell Slots](/wiki/Spells#Spell_Slots "Spells") once per day.

![](/w/images/thumb/c/c1/Class_Druid_Land_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Druid_Land_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> You channel the natural arcana flowing through the earth and creatures atop it to cast powerful druidic magic.
