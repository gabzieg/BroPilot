# Circle Of The Spores

**Circle of the Spores** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Druid#Subclass "Druid") of [Druid](/wiki/Druid "Druid"). These druids use fungal growth and decay to inflict Necrotic damage through spells, as well as to raise corpses through fungal infestation to fight at their side.

![](/w/images/thumb/0/05/Class_Druid_Spores_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Druid_Spores_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
> “
>
> Viewing death, necrosis, fungal growth, and sporulation as just another part of life, you can manipulate such spores to augment yourself and harm your foes.
>
> „

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Druid](/wiki/Druid "Druid"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*
