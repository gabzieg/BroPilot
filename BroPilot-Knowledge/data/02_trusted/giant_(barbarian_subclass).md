# Giant (Barbarian Subclass)

See also: [Giant (creature type)](/wiki/Giant_(creature_type) "Giant (creature type)")

**Path of Giants** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Barbarian#Level_3 "Barbarian") of [Barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"). They can grow in size while raging and kick and throw other creatures. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

![](/w/images/thumb/2/21/Class_Barbarian_Giant_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Barbarian_Giant_Badge_Icon.png.webp)

>
> You walk the path of giants, invoking their elemental might and size by drawing strength from the same primal forces as them.

## Subclass features

*This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, [Barbarian](/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"), in addition to its unique features outlined below.*

### Level 3

[![Giant's Rage](/w/images/thumb/0/02/Giant%27s_Rage_Icon.webp/40px-Giant%27s_Rage_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Giant%27s_Rage "Giant's Rage") [Giant's Rage](/wiki/Giant%27s_Rage "Giant's Rage") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp) + ![Rage Charge](/w/images/thumb/d/d6/Rage_Charges_Icons.png/21px-Rage_Charges_Icons.png.webp))
:   Enter a [![Rage](/w/images/thumb/f/ff/Giant%27s_Rage_Condition_Icon.webp/20px-Giant%27s_Rage_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Giant%27s_Rage_(Condition) "Giant's Rage (Condition)") [Rage](/wiki/Giant%27s_Rage_(Condition) "Giant's Rage (Condition)") and increase in size. Your Rage damage bonus is doubled on Throw attacks.

[![Vaprak's Greed](/w/images/thumb/d/da/Vaprak%27s_Greed_Icon.webp/40px-Vaprak%27s_Greed_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Vaprak%27s_Greed "Vaprak's Greed")[Vaprak's Greed](/wiki/Vaprak%27s_Greed "Vaprak's Greed")
:   Your [carrying capacity](/wiki/Carrying_capacity "Carrying capacity") is increased by a quarter.

[![Thaumaturgy](/w/images/thumb/3/3f/Thaumaturgy_Icon.webp/40px-Thaumaturgy_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Thaumaturgy "Thaumaturgy") [Thaumaturgy](/wiki/Thaumaturgy "Thaumaturgy") (![Action](/w/images/thumb/f/f2/Action_Icon.png/23px-Action_Icon.png.webp))
:   Gain [![Advantage](/w/images/thumb/f/fc/Advantage_Icon.png/28px-Advantage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Advantage "Advantage") [Advantage](/wiki/Advantage "Advantage") on [Intimidation](/wiki/Intimidation "Intimidation") and [Performance](/wiki/Performance "Performance") [Checks](/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check").

### Level 5

[![Boot of the Giants](/w/images/thumb/b/b3/Boot_of_the_Giants_Icon.webp/40px-Boot_of_the_Giants_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Boot_of_the_Giants "Boot of the Giants") [Boot of the Giants](/wiki/Boot_of_the_Giants "Boot of the Giants") (![Bonus Action](/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Bonus_Action_Icon.png/23px-Bonus_Action_Icon.png.webp))
:   Try to kick a target away. Your chances depend on your [Athletics](/wiki/Athletics "Athletics"), and are higher if you're hidden or invisible.

### Level 6

[![Elemental Cleaver](/w/images/thumb/1/1e/Elemental_Cleaver_acid_Icon.webp/40px-Elemental_Cleaver_acid_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Elemental_Cleaver "Elemental Cleaver") [Elemental Cleaver](/wiki/Elemental_Cleaver "Elemental Cleaver")
:   Channel primordial might into your weapon. Until the end of your [![Rage](/w/images/thumb/5/54/Rage_Condition_Icon.webp/25px-Rage_Condition_Icon.webp)](/wiki/Rage_(Condition) "Rage (Condition)") [Rage](/wiki/Rage_(Condition) "Rage (Condition)"), your weapon will deal 1d6 additional [![Acid](/w/images/thumb/5/5f/Acid_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Acid_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Acid "Acid")⁠[Acid](/wiki/Acid "Acid"), [![Cold](/w/images/thumb/0/05/Cold_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Cold_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Cold "Cold")⁠[Cold](/wiki/Cold "Cold"), [![Fire](/w/images/thumb/8/84/Fire_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Fire_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Fire "Fire")⁠[Fire](/wiki/Fire "Fire"), [![Lightning](/w/images/thumb/3/31/Lightning_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Lightning_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Lightning "Lightning")⁠[Lightning](/wiki/Lightning "Lightning"), or [![Thunder](/w/images/thumb/8/87/Thunder_Damage_Icon.png/20px-Thunder_Damage_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Thunder "Thunder")⁠[Thunder](/wiki/Thunder "Thunder") damage, and gain the [![Thrown](/w/images/thumb/2/21/Thrown_Icon.png/25px-Thrown_Icon.png.webp)](/wiki/Thrown "Thrown") [Thrown](/wiki/Thrown "Thrown") property. If thrown, it will immediately reappear in your hand.

### Level 10
