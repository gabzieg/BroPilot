# Way Of Shadow

**Way of Shadow** is one of the [subclasses](/wiki/Monk#Level_3 "Monk") of [Monk](/wiki/Monk "Monk"). It can be chosen at level 3. These monks are more specialized in using the shadows to quietly strike their foes.

![](/w/images/thumb/a/a6/Class_Monk_Shadow_Badge_Icon.png/100px-Class_Monk_Shadow_Badge_Icon.png.webp)
